# Retirement KITAS E33F or Silver Hair E33E?

## Quick answer

**E33F and E33E are different long-term residence routes for older foreign nationals in Indonesia, not two price tiers of the same visa.** E33F is a retirement route with a shorter initial residence period, while E33E Silver Hair is a separate long-term category with special financial conditions.

Age requirements, sponsorship arrangements and evidence of income cannot automatically be transferred from one category to the other.

If you need assistance with a specific application, start with our [Retirement KITAS E33F](/bali/visas/retirement-kitas/) page or compare the requirements with [Second Home E33](/bali/visas/second-home/).

## Principal differences

| Item | E33F | E33E Silver Hair |
|---|---|---|
| Type | Retirement ITAS | Long-term Silver Hair |
| Initial stay | 1 year in the official table | 5 years under a separate category |
| Age | Current applicable threshold must be verified | Separate age requirement |
| Financial basis | Evidence of acceptable regular financial support | Special financial commitment and evidence of income |
| Sponsor | Must be checked according to the category and the authority of the specialist handling the application | Sponsorship arrangements differ from the standard E33F category |
| Work | No general work authorisation | No general work authorisation |

This table explains the principal differences between the categories. **It does not replace the current eVisa category information and does not guarantee that either category is available to a particular applicant.**

## Why outdated age thresholds mislead

Historical publications mention age thresholds of **55 and 60 years**. Descriptions of the minimum monthly income required for E33F also vary.

Some older public guides mention **USD 1,500**, while more recent category descriptions refer to **USD 3,000**.

These figures come from publications issued at different times. They must not be treated as automatically applicable to an application made in 2026.

**Before starting an application, both the age threshold and the income requirement must be confirmed using the current official requirements for the specific visa index.**

SAFRWAY does not present an unverified historical figure as an established government requirement in its client terms.

## How financial proof differs

For **E33F**, the assessment generally concerns evidence of pension payments or other acceptable regular financial support, together with the applicant's financial independence.

For **E33E Silver Hair**, published descriptions refer to a separate obligation to place funds in an Indonesian state-owned bank, as well as a requirement to demonstrate income.

This is not equivalent to simply providing an ordinary bank statement.

The rules for **Second Home E33** provide a different financial route.

In particular, the **USD 50,000** requirement mentioned in descriptions of Silver Hair must not be confused with the **USD 130,000** requirement or the property-based alternative published for Second Home E33.

The financial conditions of one category cannot automatically be applied to another.

## What to check before choosing

Before deciding which category to consider, check:

1. **Citizenship and age.** Confirm the applicant's nationality and exact age on the application date.

2. **Intended residence period.** Determine the initial length of stay the applicant is seeking.

3. **Source of funds.** Review pension payments, other recurring income, savings and eligible property.

4. **Sponsorship and subsequent obligations.** Check the applicable sponsorship requirements and whether the applicant can provide the necessary confirmation of obligations after arrival.

5. **Family circumstances.** Identify the family members involved and separately assess the applicability of [Family KITAS](/bali/visas/family-kitas/) to their circumstances.

6. **Actual employment plans.** Establish whether the applicant intends to work in Indonesia, which requires a different immigration category.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are E33F and E33E two prices for one visa?

No. They are separate immigration categories.

Their residence periods, documentary requirements and financial eligibility conditions differ.

### Is a pension alone sufficient for E33E?

This cannot be guaranteed.

The long-term category has specific financial conditions that must be checked separately.

### Is E33F definitely available from age 55?

Some sources mention this age threshold.

However, for an application in 2026, it must be confirmed against the current official information for the relevant category and the applicant's circumstances.

### Is USD 50,000 a SAFRWAY Silver Hair fee?

No.

This amount is mentioned as a financial obligation associated with the immigration category, not as the price of SAFRWAY's application assistance.

### Does a retirement visa permit employment?

Retirement residence categories must not be interpreted as providing general permission for employment or commercial activities in Indonesia.

### Could E33 Second Home be an alternative to Retirement KITAS?

In some circumstances, it may be worth considering.

However, E33 has different financial conditions. The appropriate choice depends on the applicant's individual circumstances and the immigration route actually available.
