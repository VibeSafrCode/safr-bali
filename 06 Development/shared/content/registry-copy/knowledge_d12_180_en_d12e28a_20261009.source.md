**Meta Description:** How D12 works: 180 days after entry, up to another 180 with an extension, a total limit of 12 months, and re-entry using a multiple-entry visa.

## Quick answer

A multiple-entry D12 has its own **overall visa validity period**, but each entry initially permits a stay of **up to 180 days** in Indonesia. If an extension is approved, you may receive **up to another 180 days** for that visit, subject to a total limit of **12 months**. If you leave and later re-enter on a still-valid D12, a new permitted stay begins under the entry conditions. This does not extend the visa's overall validity.

## Three time periods not to confuse

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| D12 visa validity | How long the multiple-entry visa can be used for travel; SAFRWAY offers one- and two-year options |
| Initial permitted stay | Up to 180 days following an authorized entry |
| Current stay after extension | Up to an additional 180 days, totaling no more than 12 months for that visit |

**A two-year visa is not permission to remain in Indonesia continuously for two years.** Check your actual permitted stay against issued records and your current status.

## Scenario 1: staying for 3–5 months

You arrive to research an investment project, hold meetings, inspect properties or sites, and depart before your current permit expires. If your D12 is still valid, you can use it on another trip, subject to entry requirements. Extending this visit may be unnecessary.

## Scenario 2: needing to stay without leaving for more than 180 days

You continue investigating a site and preparing the project, and your initial stay is close to expiring. Subject to D12 conditions, you may apply to extend your current ITK. Published rules allow **up to 180 additional days**, for a total of **up to 12 months** during the same visit.

SAFRWAY assists with one extension stage for **7,000,000 IDR**. Extension eligibility must always be assessed **before the current ITK expires**. [D12 extension service](/bali/visas/d12/extension/).

## Scenario 3: leaving and returning to Indonesia

A multiple-entry visa enables repeat travel during its validity. After you physically leave, the existing visit ends. On your next authorized entry using a valid D12, a new permitted stay begins.

However, **this does not guarantee an automatic “reset” of every restriction**. Entry, travel purpose, and compliance remain under Immigration's control. SAFRWAY does not recommend repeated border crossings as a substitute for the appropriate long-term status if your actual activities have changed.

## What happens when the D12 visa itself is nearing expiry?

Extending your stay does not extend the overall multiple-entry visa's validity for future trips. If you need another D12 period, a manager checks whether a new application and subsequent entry are possible.

At the same time, the legality of your **current stay** primarily depends on your valid ITK. Do not automatically assume that the expiry of the visa's travel validity immediately cancels a separately issued stay permit. Your actual documents and conditions must be checked. Where dates conflict, rely on Immigration records rather than promotional summaries.

## Can you remain in Indonesia for a year on D12?

Potentially, **up to 12 months in one visit with an approved extension**, provided you continue to meet the pre-investment purpose. Immigration is not obliged to approve an extension for every applicant. D12 does not become a work visa after extension.

## What happens after 12 months?

First establish whether you are still at the pre-investment stage or whether your actual activities now require a different basis. You may need to leave and apply for a new visa, or another lawful status may fit. Investor KITAS can be considered if the company and your ownership meet its requirements. **We do not promise direct in-country D12 → ITAS conversion**, because the official D12 description excludes the standard conversion route.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does a one-year D12 allow a whole year of uninterrupted stay immediately?

No. One entry initially allows up to 180 days; a longer uninterrupted stay requires an extension.

### Can I extend for another 180 days?

Yes, the published procedure provides this possibility. Approval depends on current status and document review.

### Must I extend D12 after every trip?

Not necessarily. If you end the current visit by leaving and the multiple-entry visa is still valid, a new stay is determined upon your next entry.

### Can I extend indefinitely while my two-year D12 is still valid?

No. The published limit for a single continuous visit is 12 months.

### Can I work on D12 after six months?

No. Permitted activities do not change simply because you have stayed longer.

### Must I visit Immigration for an extension?

Under the current published procedure, plan for photography, biometrics, and an interview; SAFRWAY checks the specific office's instructions.
