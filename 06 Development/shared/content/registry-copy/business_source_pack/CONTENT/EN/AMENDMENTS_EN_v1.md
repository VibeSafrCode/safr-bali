# PT PMA Changes in Indonesia: Shareholders, Directors, Address and KBLI

## Quick answer

**Changing shareholders, directors, the address or KBLI codes of a PT PMA may require notarial corporate action through AHU and subsequent OSS updates.** The procedure and documents depend on the change involved.

A company may continue operating, but its legal records must reflect reality. Where shareholders, management, address, capital, company name or activities have changed, editing the **OSS** account alone may not be enough.

SAFRWAY coordinates a review of PT PMA corporate changes and the required notarial and registration procedures.

## Changes we can discuss

- **Shareholders and ownership stakes:** adding or removing shareholders, changing share distribution and verifying permitted foreign ownership.
- **Directors and commissioners:** changes to management bodies and their powers.
- **Name and address:** reviewing new particulars, premises documents and related registrations.
- **Capital:** corporate resolutions and correct documentation in light of investment requirements.
- **KBLI codes and activities:** adding or removing an activity and checking sectoral permits.
- **Articles of association and other particulars:** amendments to corporate documents within the agreed scope.

## Why company records must be kept in sync

**AHU** deals with corporate records and the registration of certain changes to a legal entity through a notary. **OSS** records business activities and licences. Tax systems, banks and other registers may also need updating after particular changes.

Not every amendment follows the same route. Some require approval, others notification, while additional requirements depend on the sector and current legal standing.

## A special consideration when changing KBLI

OSS now includes **KBLI 2025**. Begin by classifying the company's actual activities and checking the appropriate new code, then determine whether corporate documents and permits need amendment. [NIB and OSS](/en/bali/business/oss-licenses/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I replace the director of a PT PMA?

Generally, yes, subject to corporate procedures and requirements for the incoming director. We review the documents needed and the systems that must be updated.

### Is changing OSS records enough when a shareholder changes?

There is no basis for assuming that is sufficient in every case. Corporate and notarial requirements usually need to be reviewed first.

### Must an NIB change when the registered address changes?

It depends on the change, project location and existing OSS data. We check which particulars can be updated without obtaining a new NIB.

### Can another KBLI code be added?

Possibly, provided the company is allowed to carry out the activity and satisfies investment and licensing requirements.

### Do you check the effect on a director's or investor's KITAS?

We can separately assess how corporate changes affect an existing immigration basis. This is an independent review, not an automatic component of every amendment service.

### Can everything be done without the director's involvement?

That depends on the documents, representative's authority and procedure. We do not promise in advance that no personal action will be required.
