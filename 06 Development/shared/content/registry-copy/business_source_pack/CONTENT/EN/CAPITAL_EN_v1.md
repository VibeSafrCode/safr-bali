# PT PMA Capital in 2026: Why IDR 2.5 Billion and 10 Billion Are Different

When establishing a foreign-invested company in Indonesia, it is easy to confuse three figures: **the PT PMA's issued and paid-up capital**, **the minimum planned investment** and **the individual shareholding requirement for an E28A Investor KITAS**. These are distinct measures governed by different rules.

## Quick answer

Under the general provisions of **BKPM Regulation No. 5/2025**, the minimum issued/paid-up capital for a PT PMA is **IDR 2.5 billion per company**, unless sector-specific rules require otherwise.

A separate provision requires **planned investment exceeding IDR 10 billion**, normally for the relevant five-digit KBLI activity and project location, subject to exceptions and alternative calculation methods for specific sectors.

These sums **are not SAFRWAY service fees**. The **E28A Investor KITAS** requirements for an individual foreign applicant's shareholding are assessed separately.

## 1. PT PMA paid-up capital — IDR 2.5 billion

BKPM Regulation No. 5/2025 lowered the general PT PMA issued/paid-up capital threshold compared with the older model that referred to IDR 10 billion. Meeting the capital minimum does not remove the need for a genuine investment project, lawful sector access and continuing corporate compliance.

The regulation also restricts how paid-up capital may be transferred or used during the initial period, with exceptions for permitted spending on assets, construction and company operations. Before signing, the current rule and related OSS declarations must be checked.

## 2. Planned investment — normally over IDR 10 billion

This is a separate indicator of **investment project scale**, not a permanent amount required to remain in a bank account. The general rule applies by **KBLI activity and project location**, while some sectors have special calculation methods and exceptions, including differences in the treatment of land and buildings.

One cannot calculate investment requirements identically for every restaurant, wholesale business, property development or digital service without checking the relevant sectoral rules.

## 3. E28A Investor KITAS — another eligibility test

For a standard [E28A Investor KITAS](/en/bali/visas/investor-kitas/), published visa requirements refer to the **individual applicant's shareholding** in the sponsoring company having a value of **at least IDR 10 billion**. This is **not** the same as the PT PMA's overall paid-up capital minimum, nor is it the same as the planned investment in a specific project.

Therefore, claims that "a PT PMA with IDR 2.5 billion capital automatically provides an Investor KITAS" are misleading.

## 4. Other changes in 2025–2026

- **PP No. 28/2025** updated the risk-based licensing framework and repealed the previous PP No. 5/2021.
- **BKPM Regulation No. 5/2025** updated OSS business-licensing and investment rules, including capital and investment criteria.
- OSS now publishes **KBLI 2025** and an official conversion tool for KBLI 2020 codes.

A new regulation and classification do not mean that every existing company must be re-registered immediately without review. Examine the actual registrations and transitional requirements first.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a PT PMA be established with IDR 2.5 billion in capital?

That is the general issued/paid-up minimum under BKPM Regulation No. 5/2025, but the company must also meet other requirements, including those relating to its investment project and sector-specific exceptions.

### Must I deposit IDR 10 billion as paid-up capital?

Do not confuse planned investment of over IDR 10 billion with the IDR 2.5 billion general minimum paid-up capital. The exact financial structure depends on the project.

### Can I get an E28A if the company's paid-up capital is IDR 2.5 billion?

That capital amount alone does not establish the applicant's eligibility for E28A. The investor's own shareholding requirements are assessed separately.

### Are there special requirements for restaurants, hotels or trade?

Yes. The business activity can affect investment calculations, permitted foreign-ownership share and licensing.

### Must an old KBLI 2020 code be updated?

Review your activity and its status in OSS 2025. Transitional actions depend on the individual registration.
