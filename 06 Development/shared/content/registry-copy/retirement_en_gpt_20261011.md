# Retirement KITAS E33F in Indonesia

## Quick Answer

**E33F is a separate Indonesian temporary residence category for foreign nationals planning to live in Indonesia after the end of their working lives.** It should not be confused with E33G for remote workers or the long-term E33E Silver Hair category.

Before applying, the applicable age requirement, financial means of support and sponsorship requirements must be confirmed.

SAFRWAY helps assess the specific situation, prepare the required documents and arrange assistance through qualified Indonesian specialists. The immigration authorities make the final decision on visa and ITAS approval.

## Who Should Consider E33F?

This route may suit a person who intends to live in Bali or elsewhere in Indonesia after their working life, has sufficient means of support and does not plan to work under a local employment arrangement.

A retirement KITAS should not be selected merely because someone wants a visa valid for one year.

**Age and financial thresholds require case-specific verification.**

Published descriptions of E33F from different years contain conflicting age thresholds and monthly income requirements, including requirements based on older regulations.

We do not present a figure from an archived guide as a universal requirement for October 2026.

Before offering services to a client, we check the current eVisa category information and the documents required by the specialist handling the application.

## E33F vs E33E Silver Hair — What's the Difference?

**E33F** is considered a standard retirement residence route with a shorter initial residence period.

**E33E Silver Hair** is a separate long-term category that may have different age and financial requirements, including an immigration guarantee.

The requirements of E33E cannot automatically be applied to E33F, and the requirements of E33F cannot automatically be applied to E33E.

For more information, see our [comparison of E33F and E33E](/bali/knowledge/retirement-kitas/e33f-vs-e33e/).

If you are considering living in Indonesia using substantial savings rather than retirement income, also see [Second Home E33](/bali/visas/second-home/).

## Validity and Extensions

According to official residence permit tables, E33F falls under the category of temporary residence with an initial period of **one year**, with the possibility of extension subject to the applicable requirements.

The possibility of extension **does not mean that an extension is automatic** or that the holder has a right to permanent residence.

Before the current residence period expires, we review the applicant's status, the requirements in force and the necessary documents.

Retirement residence status does not provide general permission to undertake paid work in Indonesia.

For local employment, an appropriate legal basis should be assessed through [Working KITAS](/bali/visas/working-kitas/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### At What Age Is E33F Available?

The age requirement must be confirmed using the current information for the relevant visa category and the applicant's individual circumstances.

Published information from different years contains conflicting age thresholds.

### Do I Need Pension Payments, or Are Savings Enough?

The acceptable source of regular financial support and the required supporting evidence must be assessed.

An arbitrary amount held in a bank account may not be sufficient on its own.

### Can I Work or Run a Business with E33F?

E33F should not be interpreted as providing a general right to undertake employment or other paid work.

Specific business roles, activities and sources of income require a separate legal assessment.

### Can I Obtain E33F Without a Sponsor?

Sponsorship requirements must be confirmed before submitting the application.

E33F should not be treated as interchangeable with the longer-term E33E category, which follows a different procedure.

### Can My Spouse or Children Join Me?

A separate residence basis must be assessed for each family member, including whether it is applicable to the principal residence holder's status.

For more information, see [Family KITAS](/bali/visas/family-kitas/).
