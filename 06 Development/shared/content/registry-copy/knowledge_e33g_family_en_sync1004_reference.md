# E33G for a family: arranging KITAS for a spouse and child

Principal applicant: E33G Remote Worker KITAS. Spouse: a separate E31B family ITAS. Child: a separate E31E family ITAS. E31E: a biological child under 18 who is unmarried.

## Key facts

- Principal applicant: E33G Remote Worker KITAS.
- Spouse: a separate E31B family ITAS.
- Child: a separate E31E family ITAS.
- E31E: a biological child under 18 who is unmarried.
- A family ITAS is not a work visa.
- Evidence: marriage or birth and the principal holder’s status.
- Translation, apostille or legalization may be required depending on the country and document.

If one family member receives an [E33G Remote Worker KITAS](/bali/visas/e33g/), this does not automatically give their spouse and children the right to live in Indonesia under that E33G.

Every family member needs their own lawful status. Family-reunification categories are used for the family of an ITAS holder.

The category mapping described here is:

- **E31B** — the spouse of an ITAS / ITAP holder;
- **E31E** — an unmarried biological child under 18 joining a parent who holds ITAS / ITAP.

A standard family arrangement can therefore look like this:

**principal applicant — E33G → spouse — E31B → child — E31E.**

## Spouse: E31B

E31B is a family category for a foreign national joining a husband or wife who holds ITAS or ITAP.

If the principal has obtained E33G, the spouse can apply for a separate family status based on the marriage and the principal's valid immigration status.

It is a separate application and a separate ITAS. The spouse does not become an E33G holder or automatically inherit the principal's remote-work permission.

## Documents for a spouse

Two basic points need to be established:

1. the marriage is legally recognized;
2. the principal family member has an appropriate valid status in Indonesia.

For an initial review, we normally prepare:

- spouse's passport;
- marriage certificate;
- principal E33G holder's passport;
- principal's E33G / e-ITAS;
- additional information and documents requested by the current application form.

Depending on the country that issued the marriage certificate, the following may be needed:

- an apostille;
- consular legalization;
- translation;
- the correct certification or presentation of the translation.

Do not order an arbitrary translation before the manager has reviewed the document and its issuing country.

## Child: E31E

The described classification provides E31E for the biological child of an ITAS / ITAP holder.

The child must:

- be a biological child;
- be under 18;
- be unmarried;
- be joining a parent who holds ITAS / ITAP.

Where these conditions are met, a separate family ITAS can be arranged for the child of an E33G holder.

## Documents for a child

The key document is the birth certificate establishing the relationship with the principal ITAS holder.

For an initial review, normally provide:

- child's passport;
- birth certificate;
- E33G-holding parent's passport;
- parent's current E33G / e-ITAS;
- parents' marriage certificate, if needed to connect the documents;
- additional documents depending on the family structure and current requirements.

The birth certificate may also require an apostille or legalization and translation.

## Does the principal need to obtain E33G first?

In SAFRWAY's standard workflow, we first build the case around the principal E33G applicant. We submit dependent applications once the principal's status and documents support the family application.

This reduces the risk of a dependent application being unable to reference the principal's status properly.

The sequence can differ in individual cases where the current system accepts an already-issued visa or another confirmed principal status. It is best to establish the sequence before submitting the family's applications.

## Can the whole family apply simultaneously?

Do not treat this as a universal rule. The sequence depends on:

- the stage of the principal E33G application;
- what has already been issued to the principal;
- current eVisa requirements;
- each dependent's category;
- the family's documents.

SAFRWAY plans the sequence in advance so a dependent application is not filed without sufficient supporting grounds.

## Family ITAS validity

Family status is separate from E33G. Under the described E31 classification, different ITAS periods can be available depending on the category and underlying basis.

Do not assume a dependent document will always have exactly the same validity period as the principal E33G.

Before filing, SAFRWAY checks:

- the available duration for the family category;
- the principal holder's validity period;
- how the family's expiry dates can be coordinated.

Where possible, the aim is to avoid inconveniently different expiry dates within the same family.

## Can a spouse work on E31B?

E31B is a family status, not an independent remote-worker E33G. Obtaining dependent ITAS does not automatically authorize every kind of work.

If the spouse also:

- works remotely;
- plans to work for an Indonesian company;
- runs a business;
- receives income in Indonesia,

the activity must be reviewed separately to determine whether family status is appropriate or a separate work or business status is needed.

## Can the child attend school?

Family status addresses the child's lawful stay, but schools may have their own requirements.

A school may request:

- passport;
- ITAS;
- birth certificate;
- residential address;
- medical documents;
- other paperwork.

Immigration status and school admission are related but separate matters.

## Must all family members have the same nationality?

Do not assume identical nationality is required. More important are:

- a legally documented family relationship;
- valid passports;
- correctly prepared civil-status documents;
- an appropriate principal status.

Tell the manager if spouses or children have different citizenships: documentation and legalization requirements may vary.

## What if the marriage was registered outside the country of citizenship?

This is a common situation. For example:

- the spouses are citizens of one country;
- the marriage was registered in another;
- the family lives in a third.

We need to establish:

- where the certificate was issued;
- whether the document is recognized;
- whether an apostille is required;
- whether translation is needed;
- whether additional marriage records are required.

Send the original document to the manager first.

## What if a marriage or birth certificate is not in English?

Do not order a translation “just in case.” The requirements for the specific document should be checked first.

You may need:

- an English translation;
- an Indonesian translation;
- a sworn or certified translator;
- an apostille before translation;
- a different preparation sequence.

SAFRWAY reviews the original and then explains the appropriate process.

## What if the parents are not married?

For the child, the legally established relationship with the parent is crucial.

Where the family situation involves:

- unmarried parents;
- different surnames;
- a parent not listed on the certificate;
- adoption;
- guardianship;
- remarriage;
- a child from a previous marriage,

do not select a category solely from its name.

**Send the documents to a SAFRWAY manager. We will review which category corresponds to the actual legal relationship.**

## Can the parents of an E33G holder apply?

The described family ITAS classification also includes **E31H**, for a parent joining a child who holds ITAS / ITAP.

This is a separate scenario and should not automatically be combined with the standard “E33G + spouse + child” package.

For a parent, SAFRWAY checks:

- whether E31H is applicable;
- the principal holder's status;
- evidence of the relationship;
- the available period;
- current filing requirements.

## Does the family receive the same rights as the E33G holder?

No. The principal and dependents reside in Indonesia on different grounds:

- E33G is based on remote work for a foreign employer;
- E31B is based on a spouse joining the family;
- E31E is based on a child joining the family.

The principal's permitted activity must not automatically be transferred to dependents.

## When should family preparation begin?

Think about family documents before the principal E33G application. The applications do not necessarily need to be filed on the same day.

Before starting, it helps to check:

- all family members' passports;
- marriage certificate;
- birth certificates;
- name spellings;
- apostilles or legalization;
- translations;
- passport expiry dates.

This avoids losing time correcting family documents after the principal E33G is issued.

## Why check the names carefully?

Different transliterations are a frequent issue for international families. A name may be written:

- one way in the passport;
- another way in the marriage certificate;
- differently again in the child's certificate.

This is not always critical, but it should be identified before filing. SAFRWAY reviews the whole family's documents as one connected package.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a spouse automatically included in E33G?

No. A separate family status is arranged for the spouse.

### Which category is used for an E33G holder's spouse?

E31B is the category described for a spouse joining an ITAS / ITAP holder.

### Which category is used for a child?

E31E is described for an unmarried biological child under 18 joining an ITAS / ITAP-holding parent.

### Does the child need a separate passport?

A valid travel document acceptable for the separate immigration application is needed. Have the child's actual documents reviewed for the specific case.

### Can family applications follow the E33G application?

Yes. This is the standard scenario described here.

### Can family preparation start earlier?

Yes, and it is recommended. Review marriage and birth documents before the principal E33G is issued.

### Can a spouse work on family KITAS?

Do not treat family ITAS as unrestricted work authorization. The spouse's actual activity needs separate assessment.

### Can parents apply?

The classification includes E31H for a parent joining an ITAS / ITAP-holding child. This requires individual review.

### Do certificates need translation?

It depends on the language, issuing country and current requirements. Send the originals first.

### What if the family situation is non-standard?

Contact the manager and provide the documents. SAFRWAY will identify the category corresponding to the legal family relationship.
