# NIB and OSS Licences: What Businesses Need in Indonesia

## Quick answer

**An NIB is a business identifier in OSS, not a universal licence to carry out every activity.** The necessary permits depend on KBLI 2025, risk, location and sector. We identify what is still required before operations begin.

**NIB (Nomor Induk Berusaha)** is the business identification number issued within Indonesia's **OSS** system. However, obtaining an NIB and receiving full approval to carry out a particular commercial activity are not always the same thing.

SAFRWAY helps review existing registrations, the accuracy of **KBLI** codes and the need for additional permits before a business starts, changes or expands its operations.

## Why an NIB alone may not be enough

Indonesia's risk-based licensing framework under **Government Regulation PP No. 28/2025** has **four risk levels**. Required documents depend on risk, sector, location and project scale. Some activities may need only an NIB, while others require a standard certificate, permits and verification of specific conditions.

It would be misleading to promise either that "an NIB is enough for every business" or that every sector requires an identical package of licences.

## Where we begin the review

### KBLI 2025

We identify the **KBLI 2025** codes corresponding to your actual activities. OSS provides a **KBLI 2020 → KBLI 2025** conversion tool. For an existing company, we must examine its real registered records and transitional rules rather than automatically changing every code. [Official OSS classification](https://oss.go.id/id/kbli).

### Company details and operating location

We compare the company's AHU records, the project's address or site, rights to use the premises and OSS data. Businesses with land, buildings or premises may require additional checks of spatial-planning compliance and other basic conditions.

### Risk level and sector-specific rules

We establish whether additional certificates, permits, environmental documents or other sectoral documentation are necessary. Requirements may differ significantly for hospitality, restaurants, medical services, food products and other industries.

**New PT PMA:** first establish the legal company and structure, then perform agreed OSS steps and obtain sector-specific permits.

**Existing PT PMA:** where a company adds a cafe, hotel or other new line of business, first review changes to its **KBLI codes and corporate documents**, then determine the new permit requirements. [Company amendments](/en/bali/business/company-changes/).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is an NIB?

The official business identification number in OSS. By itself, it does not replace all other permits.

### Must KBLI be updated after the switch to KBLI 2025?

Check whether your code has changed and what transitional actions are required within your own OSS account. We do not recommend automatically re-registering every company without reviewing its case.

### Does an NIB allow me to open a restaurant or hotel?

Depending on the exact activity, additional licences and approvals may be required.

### Can I obtain an NIB without establishing a company?

OSS recognises different types of business actors. For foreign investors, the lawful business form and basis for operating must be assessed first; registering as an individual is not a substitute for the required corporate structure.

### Can an existing PT PMA add a new business activity?

Often changes are possible, but corporate records, new KBLI codes, foreign-ownership restrictions and permits must be reviewed.
