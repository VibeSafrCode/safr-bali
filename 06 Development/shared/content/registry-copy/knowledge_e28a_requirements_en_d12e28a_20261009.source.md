## Quick answer

The standard Investor KITAS **E28A** is intended for a foreign investor in an Indonesian company. Published requirements call for proof that **the applicant personally owns shares worth at least 10 billion IDR** in the sponsoring company registered with the investment authority. The company's legal documents and banking history are also reviewed. E28A lasts **two years**. SAFRWAY's application service costs **16,000,000 IDR**, which is **not the investment threshold**.

## What does “10 billion IDR” actually mean?

It is not a government visa payment, an agency fee, or merely the company's total stated capital.

The crucial requirement is **this specific foreign applicant's verified shareholding** in the sponsoring company. Ownership and share value must be supported by documents and registration records. A company may have substantial capital, but if the applicant's portion is too small, the label “investor” alone does not establish E28A eligibility.

### An illustrative example

A company's total share value may exceed 10 billion IDR, yet that does not prove **this applicant's individual shares** meet the threshold. SAFRWAY checks registered ownership, not just the total value reported for the business.

## What kind of company qualifies?

The standard E28A scenario involves a company able to act as a **sponsor (penjamin)**, usually a PT PMA — an Indonesian company with foreign investment. We check:

- the registered company name and active status;
- incorporation documents and current amendments;
- shareholder records and ownership allocation;
- relevant investment-registration entries;
- management records if the applicant is a director or commissioner;
- the company's eligibility to sponsor the application.

Incorporating a PT PMA does not by itself qualify every foreign shareholder for E28A.

## Which documents are checked?

For an initial review, prepare:

**Applicant:** passport, photograph, current immigration status, intended role, and proof of participation in the company.

**Company:** registration documents, legal-entity approval, current shareholding structure, proof of the applicant's share value, and company bank statements.

The official E28A description refers to **two months of corporate current-account history**. Where the company is less than two months old, a published exception provides for certain bank records to be submitted to Immigration **within 90 days after ITAS issuance**. **This does not waive other requirements** or guarantee the exception applies to every case.

We confirm exact files, corporate forms, and supporting records against the current eVisa process.

## If your shareholding is below 10 billion IDR

Do not automatically apply under E28A. Official guidance notes that an investor whose stake is insufficient and who **serves as a director or commissioner** may require the corresponding **employment visa category** rather than the investor category.

SAFRWAY checks the person's actual function and selects a lawful status. Corporate documents should not be artificially changed purely to fit a visa label without business and legal analysis.

## May an E28A holder work?

E28A permits the functions covered by its category in the company receiving the investment, including applicable director or commissioner duties. It is **not unrestricted permission to work at any company, in any position, or for any salary**. Your actual responsibilities should be assessed before filing.

An investment basis may be insufficient where a person intends to work as an employee or perform other operational duties.

## Visa duration and first use

Published classifications describe E28A as a **two-year ITAS/KITAS**. There is also a separate period for first entry after the visa is issued: official guidance specifies **90 days**. Those 90 days are not the duration of your ITAS.

## If the PT PMA has not yet been established

Corporate preparation can be arranged separately. It is not automatically included in the E28A filing price. First determine the business structure, registration, shareholders, and documents; then check whether an eligible investor status basis exists.

If you are currently exploring properties or investment opportunities, compare [D12](/bali/visas/d12/), a pre-investment visit category that does not assume a company is already operating.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is being a company director enough?

No. E28A requires a qualifying basis and shareholding. If those conditions are not met, check the employment category appropriate to the actual position.

### Is 10 billion IDR the required balance in my personal bank account?

No. The official criterion concerns the proven value of your shares in the sponsoring company.

### Can two investors split the 10 billion IDR requirement?

Do not assume the combined amount qualifies both people. Every applicant's eligibility is reviewed separately.

### Is E28A impossible for a newly formed company?

Not necessarily. A special rule for recently incorporated companies is published regarding corporate bank records; we assess its applicability case by case.

### Can E28A be obtained without a PT PMA?

The standard process assumes an eligible sponsoring company. Any different structure must be checked against current requirements.

### Does my family automatically receive E28A status?

No. Family members need their own applicable family category and proof of relationship.

### How is E28A different from E33G?

E28A is based on an investment in an Indonesian company; E33G is for remote work for a foreign employer. They are separate immigration grounds.
