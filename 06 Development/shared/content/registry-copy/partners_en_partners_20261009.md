# Partner with SAFRWAY — solving client needs together

You help people travel, relocate, find property or conduct business. We help arrange the related services in Indonesia — from choosing a suitable visa to handling practical and organisational matters after arrival.

**SAFRWAY is open to working with companies, agencies and professional partners.** We can discuss one-off client requests, a regular flow of enquiries or joint work on more complex projects.

## Who can partner with us

### Relocation companies and visa agencies

When your clients move to Indonesia, they may need visas, KITAS, extensions, documents and help organising their arrival. We can discuss which parts of the work SAFRWAY can handle and how client communications should be divided.

### Travel agencies, travel concierges and trip organisers

We can help assess the visa arrangements before travel and coordinate related needs such as airport transfers, transport and other local services — where those services are available and confirmed for the specific request.

### Companies, HR teams and corporate advisers

If a business needs to invite a foreign specialist, arrange a business trip or explore an appropriate immigration status, we can review the request together and identify a permitted process.

### Villa managers, real-estate agents and accommodation providers

People renting accommodation for an extended period often need more than a villa: they may also need help with their immigration status, airport transfer or transport. We can assist with individual requests and discuss coordinated support.

### Lawyers, accountants and corporate-services professionals

We can work together on cases involving immigration, documentation, companies and residence. Any legal or regulated part of a service will be arranged only through a suitably qualified provider and after review of the specific case.

## What SAFRWAY can help with

**Visas and stays in Indonesia.** Tourist and business visas, extensions, advice on current immigration status, suitable KITAS categories, family permits and assistance with status changes where the procedure is allowed. Not every visa type is available to every nationality or from every existing status; we check the conditions before agreeing to provide a service.

**Relocation and arrival arrangements.** Choosing a suitable relocation approach, arranging transfers and first-day essentials, and helping with accommodation or transport on an agreed request.

**Companies, documents and specialised requests.** Preparing and coordinating cases and referring work to suitable professional providers when it calls for specific expertise, a licence or local representation.

We do not offer partners one universal product for every situation. **We first assess the request and establish the actual scope of work, providers, timelines and final terms.**

## How we work together

1. **Tell us about your business and your clients' needs.** A general description of your services and typical requests is enough to start.
2. **We identify the services we can provide.** We verify that SAFRWAY can actually arrange the work and identify any restrictions.
3. **We agree on the partnership model.** We discuss each party's role, client communication, responsibility for documents, communication procedures and commercial terms.
4. **We review the individual case.** We clarify the information and current requirements. Personal documents are shared only through an agreed secure channel.
5. **We coordinate delivery.** Partners and clients receive information about their cases to the extent agreed by the parties and permitted under applicable confidentiality requirements.

For ongoing cooperation, we can separately agree how enquiries are handed over and how our teams work together. We **do not promise** a dedicated platform, account access or automatic reward payments unless the technical and contractual setup has been confirmed.

## Possible partnership formats

- **One-off request.** You need help with a particular client or service.
- **Ongoing cooperation.** You handle a flow of enquiries; we take care of the agreed tasks.
- **Joint case management.** For a complex case, we agree responsibilities and client contact in advance.
- **Mutual professional referrals.** We can discuss passing relevant enquiries to each other when it works for both parties.

Financial terms, settlement arrangements and the legal structure are agreed **individually**. We do not publish promises of fixed commissions, exclusivity, white-label service or payments before those terms have been agreed.

## Why one service can make things easier

Moving to another country rarely ends with obtaining a visa. A person may later need a home, transport, assistance with documents or business matters. Working with a partner, we can coordinate the wider set of needs rather than leaving the client to find a new provider for every next step.

Nevertheless, SAFRWAY does not automatically undertake to deliver every requested service: **each service, timeline and provider is confirmed before work begins.**

## Frequently asked questions

### Who can become a SAFRWAY partner?

Agencies, companies and professionals involved in travel, relocation, accommodation, staffing, visas or business services. We agree the partnership format individually.

### Do I need to be based in Bali?

No. We can start the discussion remotely. Whether a particular service can be arranged depends on the country, documentation and delivery requirements.

### Can I refer just one client without a long-term contract?

We can discuss a one-off request. We agree how client data is shared, each party's role and the working terms before providing the service.

### Are there special B2B rates or commissions?

No universal public rate schedule has been approved yet. Terms are calculated based on the cooperation model, the services involved and each party's responsibilities.

### Can we collaborate on services other than visas?

Yes. We can discuss accommodation, transport, documents, business-related requests and other needs. Availability and cost are confirmed for each specific request.

### Who communicates with the client — the partner or SAFRWAY?

That is part of the agreement. We can choose a clear way of working that takes into account responsibilities, the client's consent and personal-data protection.

### Do I need to send the client's passport and documents straight away?

No. An anonymised summary of the request is sufficient for an initial discussion. Documents are requested only after the process and a secure transfer channel have been agreed.

### Can I work with other agencies at the same time?

Exclusivity does not apply automatically. If it is needed at all, it is discussed separately and expressly recorded in an agreement.

### Does SAFRWAY guarantee that a visa or KITAS will be issued?

No. The final decision rests with the competent government authority. We assess the requirements before filing and support the agreed parts of the process.
