## Who is the eVOA for?

Consider the eVOA if you:

- are traveling to Bali or another part of Indonesia for a short stay;
- want to arrange the visa before departure;
- want an initial stay of up to 30 days;

If you already know you will stay for several months, compare the eVOA with [C1](/bali/visas/c1/) and other longer-term options.

## eVOA vs VOA at the airport

Both are Visa on Arrival and provide the same initial stay period. The journey after landing is different.

### If you obtain a standard VOA on arrival

At the airport you need to:
1. Find the Visa on Arrival area.
2. Pay at the airport bank/payment counter.
3. Obtain a payment receipt or voucher.
4. Complete the visa and immigration procedures.

Queues may form at these stages during busy periods. If you also need to exchange cash for payment or initial expenses, that adds another stop; airport exchange rates are not always the most favorable. These are extra tasks after a long flight that you can avoid.

### If your eVOA is ready before travel

You arrive with your electronic visa already issued. You do not need to buy a VOA or obtain its payment voucher after landing.

If your passport and eVOA meet current autogate requirements, you can use automated passport-control gates rather than the regular queue for an immigration officer. Eligibility depends on current requirements, including your passport and visa type.

Preparing eVOA in advance therefore means fewer airport procedures and a simpler path to baggage claim and the exit. Read the [eVOA versus airport VOA comparison](/bali/knowledge/evoa/evoa-vs-voa/).

## How long is the eVOA valid?

After entry, eVOA allows up to 30 days in Indonesia. It can be extended once for up to another 30 days.

- Up to 30 days: initial stay.
- Up to 60 days: total after the one extension.

For the next 30-day stage, see [SAFRWAY eVOA / VOA extension](/bali/visas/voa/extension/).

## When must you enter after eVOA is issued?

Use eVOA to enter within its validity window. For a standard B1 eVOA, this is up to 90 days from issuance. The 30-day stay begins on the date you actually enter Indonesia, not when the visa is issued.

## FAQ

### How many days can you stay on eVOA?
Up to 30 days from entry.

### Can eVOA be extended?
Yes, once for up to another 30 days. The total stay is up to 60 days.

### Is eVOA multiple-entry?
No. It is single-entry.

### What is the difference between eVOA and VOA?
eVOA is arranged electronically before travel. VOA is obtained after arrival and involves payment and visa processing at the airport or another entry point.

### Can you use autogate with eVOA?
Yes, if you meet the current autogate requirements, including passport and visa requirements.

### Must you arrange eVOA in advance?
Not if you are eligible for and prefer a standard VOA on arrival. But eVOA saves airport steps because the visa is arranged before you travel.

### How much passport validity do you need?
At least 6 months on entry.

### Is a return ticket required?
Yes. eVOA / VOA officially requires a return ticket or an onward ticket to another country.

### Can you stay longer than 60 days?
Not under the standard eVOA / VOA cycle. Choose another visa route in advance for a longer stay.

### Do you need to complete All Indonesia?
Yes. It is the mandatory arrival declaration for international passengers entering Indonesia. SAFRWAY can complete it for you.

### Can you arrange currency exchange in advance?
