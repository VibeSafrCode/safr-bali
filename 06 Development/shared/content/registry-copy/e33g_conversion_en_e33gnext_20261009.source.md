## Quick answer

Moving from an existing VOA, C1, D12 or another KITAS to E33G depends on **the starting category, the remaining lawful stay and the procedure available through Immigration**. Sometimes an application within Indonesia via a Bridging Visa is possible; in other cases you must leave and submit a new application. **A listed SAFRWAY price does not imply that conversion without exit is available to everyone.**

## We start by checking your current status

If you are already in Bali and want an [E33G Remote Worker KITAS](/bali/visas/e33g/), your first step is not paying for a new visa but reviewing your current permission to stay.

**We will check your immigration status and determine whether you can move to the required category while in Indonesia or whether departure and a new application will be necessary.**

This distinction matters because ordinary `Alih Status ITK → ITAS`, the separate `Bridging Visa` mechanism and a new application from abroad are different legal procedures.

## VOA / eVOA → E33G: how Bridging differs

The general guidance for direct `Alih Status ITK → ITAS` excludes stays obtained through Visa on Arrival. At the same time, the official B1 category description mentions a separate possibility **through a Bridging Visa**.

That means we cannot promise an **ordinary direct VOA-to-E33G conversion**. The manager must first verify whether the lawful Bridging route is available, the remaining permitted stay and eligibility for E33G.

An official Bridging Visa announcement from 2024 described a 60-day transitional permit valid inside Indonesia and the need to file and pay on time. This is a historical description of the mechanism, **not a guarantee that it is available on every day in 2026**.

## C1 → E33G: the sponsor and target category matter

The official C1 category page allows conversion to ITAS subject to conditions, notably those concerning the original sponsor/penjamin. Standard E33G, however, is usually filed without a sponsor.

Therefore, saying “C1 is convertible” is not enough to conclude that “C1 → E33G without exit is definitely available.” SAFRWAY checks the actual eVisa procedure, the original filing details and possible restrictions for the target category.

## D12 → E33G: no promise of direct conversion

D12 is a pre-investment visitor visa. Current official explanations do not provide a basis for promising a standard direct D12-to-ITAS conversion.

SAFRWAY therefore first identifies a **lawful route for changing status**. If E33G requires departure and a new application, we tell the client before payment for the selected procedure. The **17 million IDR** service price cannot turn a technically unavailable conversion into an allowed one.

## Another KITAS → E33G

When changing an active ITAS, we need to know whether that particular category can be changed under the relevant procedure or whether a new application is necessary. The basis for the previous KITAS, its sponsor, validity and outstanding obligations may affect the route.

Service price: **17.5 million IDR**, including Bridging where applicable. We do not promise automatic in-country category changes for every KITAS.

## Who qualifies for E33G in the first place?

E33G is intended for remote work for a **company outside Indonesia**. The initial application includes an employment contract with a foreign employer, proof of annual income of **at least 60,000 USD**, a **three-month** bank history, funds of **at least 2,000 USD** and other documents.

SAFRWAY additionally requests **12 months** of bank history based on its application-preparation experience. This is our agency's additional requirement, not a new government minimum.

If the person actually works for an Indonesian employer, E33G must not be used instead of the appropriate employment category.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I change from VOA to E33G without leaving Indonesia?

A separate Bridging mechanism may sometimes be available, but an ordinary direct VOA-to-ITAS conversion is excluded. We assess route availability individually.

### Is Bridging included in the VOA → E33G price?

Yes, **if this lawful mechanism is available and used in the agreed case**; eligibility for the mechanism must first be checked.

### Can I automatically convert C1 to E33G?

No. The current document, sponsor/penjamin rules and eligibility to apply specifically for E33G must be checked.

### I have D12 — am I guaranteed E33G without exiting?

No. SAFRWAY **does not guarantee** a standard direct D12-to-ITAS conversion. Departure and a new application may be necessary.

### Do the 17 million and 17.5 million prices include flights if I must exit?

No travel expenses are automatically included. If an exit is necessary, the scope and additional expenses are agreed before proceeding.

### How early should I contact SAFRWAY?

For SAFRWAY: VOA and KITAS at least **7 days** before expiry; C1 and D12 at least **30 days** before expiry. These are **service lead times for an initial inquiry**; Immigration sets the lawful application deadlines.

### Can I still change status if my income or contract does not meet E33G requirements?

SAFRWAY does not recommend filing without a substantiated basis. First consider a [document readiness audit](/bali/visas/e33g/document-check/) or another suitable category.
