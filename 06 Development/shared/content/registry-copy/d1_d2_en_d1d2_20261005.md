# D1 or D2: which multiple-entry visa should you choose for Indonesia?

D1 and D2 work similarly but differ in the main purpose of the trip.

The simplest distinction:

**D1 — personal travel and tourism.**

**D2 — regular business visits.**

If your actual purpose is mixed, the SAFRWAY manager will check which category best matches your circumstances.

## D1 and D2 comparison

| Question | D1 | D2 |
|---|---|---|

| Local salary | No | No |

## When to choose D1

D1 is better if you:

- visit Bali regularly for holidays;
- spend several periods in Indonesia during the year;
- visit family or friends;
- travel around Asia and return periodically;
- want one multiple-entry tourist visa instead of repeated eVOA/C1 applications.

Read more: [D1 Multiple Entry](/bali/visas/d1/).

## When to choose D2

D2 is better if you:

- visit regularly for business meetings;
- conduct negotiations;
- discuss cooperation terms;
- sign contracts;
- purchase or check goods;
- visit an office, factory or production site;
- combine business travel with tourism.

Read more: [D2 Multiple Entry](/bali/visas/d2/).

## How multiple entry works

The main difference between D1/D2 and single-entry visas is that departure does not necessarily end the visa itself.

As long as D1/D2 remains valid, you can use it to enter Indonesia again.

Each new entry starts a new period of stay.

Initially — **up to 60 days**.

Therefore:

**visa validity ≠ duration of one stay.**

## Can I stay continuously for 1–2 years?

No.

If D1/D2 is issued for 1 or 2 years, that is the validity of the multiple-entry visa, not permission to live continuously in Indonesia throughout that period.

One entry initially allows up to 60 days.

The current stay can then be extended under the rules in force.

## D1/D2 extension

Under current rules, D1 and D2 stays can be extended.

With extensions, a particular visit can reach **up to 180 days**.

SAFRWAY price:

**2 500 000 IDR (≈ {{USD_2_5M}})**

## D1/D2 or C1?

### C1

- one entry;
- initially up to 60 days;
- extensions up to 180 days;
- the visa ends when you leave.

### D1/D2

- multiple entries;
- initially up to 60 days per entry;
- suitable for repeat trips.

For one long trip, C1 may be simpler.

For regular returns, D1/D2 may be more convenient.

## D1/D2 or eVOA?

eVOA is suitable for short trips.

D1/D2 suits people who already know they will return to Indonesia several times.

## D2 or D12?

D2 is for regular business visits.

D12 is a separate pre-investment category for more specific investment situations.

If the purpose involves exploring investments or a more complex business situation, the manager may suggest D12.

## What documents are required?

The basics:

- passport valid for at least 6 months;
- bank statements for 3 months;
- funds equivalent to at least 2 000 USD;
- a recent photograph;
- a CV;
- an itinerary;
- evidence of the trip’s purpose and sponsor / penjamin documents where required;
- additional evidence of the purpose of the trip.

D2 may require additional documents supporting the business purpose.

## Is a sponsor required?

For D1, the official page describes a general procedure without a mandatory sponsor, with exceptions for certain nationalities. SAFRWAY checks D2 requirements and the documents for the particular travel purpose separately before filing.

## Can I work on D1/D2?

No.

Both categories are visitor visas.

D2 allows business visits, but not local employment.

## Can I switch from D1/D2 to KITAS?

This should not be treated as a standard conversion route.

Current official information allows extension of a D1/D2 visitor stay permit, but direct conversion to ITAS is not promised.

If your ultimate goal is KITAS, the manager will check the lawful route currently available.

## How to choose between D1 and D2

### Choose D1 if:
your main purpose is tourism, personal travel and regular returns.

### Choose D2 if:
your main purpose is negotiations, meetings, purchases, contracts or other business-visit activities.

### If your purpose is mixed:
explain your actual travel plans to the manager. SAFRWAY will select the category based on the real purpose, not just the desired duration.

## Price of one extension

The quoted extension price is **2 500 000 IDR (≈ {{USD_2_5M}}) per stage**. Under the published procedure, one stage adds up to 60 days; reaching a total of 180 days from the initial 60 normally requires two consecutive extensions. They are arranged and paid for separately.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are both D1 and D2 multiple-entry?
Yes.

### What is the main difference?
The main purpose of the trip: D1 is for tourism/personal travel; D2 is for business visits.

### How long can I stay after one entry?
Initially up to 60 days.

### Can they be extended?
Yes.

### Can I work?
No.

### Can D2 be used for tourism?
Yes, but if tourism is the main purpose of your trips, D1 is more appropriate.

### Can D1 be used for negotiations?
If the actual purpose is business, D2 is the better choice.

### How much does an extension cost?
**2 500 000 IDR (≈ {{USD_2_5M}})** for D1 and D2.

### Are 5-year options available?
That format exists, but SAFRWAY confirms current availability and price individually.
