# eVOA or VOA on arrival: which should you choose for Bali?

If you are eligible for Visa on Arrival, you have two basic choices:

- arrange **eVOA online before travel**;
- obtain a standard **VOA after arrival**.

The stay period is almost the same.

Both give up to 30 days and allow one extension for up to another 30 days.

The real difference is **where and when you want to deal with the visa — before the trip or after landing**.

## In short: which is more convenient?

If you want to solve the visa question in advance and reduce airport steps, **eVOA is usually more convenient**.

If you prefer not to arrange anything in advance and are comfortable going through the VOA process after landing, standard VOA remains a valid option.

## What arrival looks like with standard VOA

After leaving the aircraft, you need to:

1. find the Visa on Arrival area;
2. complete the payment step;
3. receive the payment confirmation/voucher;
4. continue with visa processing and immigration control.

During busy arrival waves, queues can be long. In peak cases, traveler and airport-service reports describe the combined VOA/immigration process taking up to around 2–3 hours. This is not an official average, but a peak scenario.

If you also need currency exchange, transport, SIM and other arrival tasks, the first part of your arrival can turn into several separate stops.

Exchanging cash adds another stop, and airport rates are not always the most favorable. The first half-hour or hour after a flight can become a sequence of separate tasks.

## What arrival looks like with eVOA

With eVOA, the visa is already arranged before departure.

You do **not need** to buy the VOA or go through the separate airport visa-payment step.

If your e-passport, eVOA and entry point meet current requirements, you may be able to use the **autogate**.

A simple arrival path can look like:

**eVOA ready → All Indonesia ready → autogate when eligible → baggage → driver waiting.**

This reduces the number of tasks between leaving the aircraft and collecting your luggage.

## What is autogate?

Autogate is automated passport control.

For foreign travelers, access depends on the current immigration requirements, passport type, visa and entry point.

For travelers with a qualifying electronic passport and eVOA, it is one of the main practical advantages of arranging the visa electronically.

The convenience is not only being able to apply from home: eVOA may also make border processing faster.

## Stay period is the same

Both eVOA and VOA provide:

- up to 30 days after entry;
- one extension for up to another 30 days;
- maximum up to 60 days.

Both are single-entry.

Once you leave Indonesia, the current single-entry visa cycle ends.

## Extension process differs

### Extending eVOA

Holders of an electronic eVOA can extend online through Indonesian Immigration’s system.

### Extending a standard VOA

A non-electronic VOA extension is handled through the immigration office serving the place of residence. If you already expect to stay beyond 30 days, the electronic option is also convenient for this next stage.

SAFRWAY can [help extend eVOA / VOA for up to another 30 days](/bali/visas/voa/extension/).

## Return/onward ticket

You need either:

- a return ticket out of Indonesia;

or

- an onward ticket to another country.

This applies regardless of whether you choose eVOA or VOA.

Read more: [Return and onward tickets](/bali/knowledge/return-ticket-indonesia/).

## All Indonesia is required for both

Choosing eVOA or VOA does not remove the other entry requirements. You must also complete **All Indonesia** before arrival. The declaration brings together information for:
- immigration;
- customs;
- health;
- quarantine.

The government declaration itself is free. SAFRWAY can complete it for you. See the [All Indonesia guide](/bali/guides/all-indonesia/).

## Convenience comparison

| Situation | eVOA | VOA on arrival |
|---|---|---|
| Visa ready before departure | Yes | No |
| Need to pay for the visa at the airport | Not needed | Needed |
| Can check all visa data in advance | Yes | No; visa processing is after arrival |
| Online extension | Yes | Not for standard non-electronic VOA |
| Autogate when eligibility conditions are met | Yes | Requires a qualifying electronic status |
| Maximum stay with extension | Up to 60 days | Up to 60 days |

## When standard VOA is still a good option

VOA on arrival remains a normal working option if:

- you did not arrange eVOA in advance;
- you do not want to handle the visa before travel;
- the trip is short;
- you do not mind the additional airport step.

It is not a “bad” visa — it simply shifts work from before the trip to after landing.

## When eVOA is better

eVOA is especially convenient if you:

- want the visa solved before departure;
- do not want to buy the VOA after a long flight;
- want to use autogate when eligible;
- expect to extend beyond the first 30 days;
- want the arrival preparation handled in advance.

## If you want to stay more than 60 days

For more than 60 days, the eVOA-versus-VOA choice is no longer the main issue. Both cover a maximum of 60 days under the standard cycle. For several months, compare:
- [C1](/bali/visas/c1/);
- [D12](/bali/visas/d12/);
- [E33G](/bali/visas/e33g/).

Choose a status that fits your real travel duration from the beginning.

## FAQ

### Do eVOA and VOA give different stay periods?

No. Both give up to 30 days and one extension up to 60 days total.

### Which is faster at the airport?

[eVOA](/bali/visas/evoa/) usually reduces arrival steps because you do not need to purchase/pay for the visa after landing.

### Can I use autogate with eVOA?

Yes, if you meet the current e-passport, visa and entry-point requirements.

### Can I use autogate with a standard VOA?

A tourist using the Visa on Arrival route needs a qualifying electronic status and electronic passport for autogate. This is an advantage of obtaining eVOA in advance.

### Which is easier to extend?

eVOA, because the extension can be handled online.

### Do both require a return ticket?

Yes, or an onward ticket to another country.

### Is All Indonesia required?

Yes, regardless of eVOA or VOA.

## Which should you choose?

If your priority is the **simplest possible arrival**, eVOA is usually more convenient:

**arrange before departure → arrive with visa ready → autogate when eligible → collect baggage → leave with a pre-booked driver.**

If arranging the visa in advance is not important to you, VOA on arrival remains a normal option.
