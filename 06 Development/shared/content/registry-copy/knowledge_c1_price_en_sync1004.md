# How much does a C1 cost in Bali for 60, 120 and 180 days?

The price depends on how long you want to stay in Indonesia.

Through SAFRWAY, the first [C1](/bali/visas/c1/) application stage costs:

**IDR 2,000,000 (≈ {{USD_2M}}).**

This lets you enter on a C1 and stay in Indonesia for up to 60 days.

If you decide to stay longer, extensions are handled and paid separately.

## Price by stay length

| Stay | SAFRWAY service cost |
|---|---:|
| Up to 60 days | IDR 2,000,000 (≈ {{USD_2M}}) |
| Up to 120 days | IDR 4,000,000 (≈ {{USD_4M}}) |
| Up to 180 days | IDR 6,000,000 (≈ {{USD_6M}}) |

Each additional 60-day stage adds another IDR 2,000,000.

## Do you have to pay for all 180 days upfront?

No.

The process is staged.

First, the C1 itself is arranged:

**IDR 2,000,000 (≈ {{USD_2M}}).**

If you decide to stay beyond the first 60 days, you pay for the first extension:

**another IDR 2,000,000 (≈ {{USD_2M}}).**

If you need to remain beyond day 120, you pay for the second extension:

**another IDR 2,000,000 (≈ {{USD_2M}}).**

You therefore do not need to pay for the full 180-day cycle in advance if you are not yet sure how long you will stay.

## How much is the first stage?

**C1 application through SAFRWAY — IDR 2,000,000 (≈ {{USD_2M}}).**

As part of the service, SAFRWAY reviews the information and documents, prepares the application and supports the process through the result.

Read more: [C1 Visa for Indonesia: how to stay in Bali for up to 180 days](/bali/knowledge/c1/visa-indonesia/).

## How much is one extension?

One C1 extension stage through SAFRWAY costs:

**IDR 2,000,000 (≈ {{USD_2M}}).**

Each stage adds up to another 60 days.

Read more: [Extend your C1 in Bali for another 60 days](/bali/visas/c1/extension/).

## How much does a C1 cost for 120 days?

If you arrange the initial C1 and then complete one extension:

**IDR 2,000,000 + IDR 2,000,000 = IDR 4,000,000 (≈ {{USD_4M}}).**

That is the SAFRWAY service cost for the two stages.

## How much does a C1 cost for 180 days?

For the full stay cycle:

- initial C1 application — IDR 2,000,000;
- first extension — IDR 2,000,000;
- second extension — IDR 2,000,000.

Total:

**IDR 6,000,000 (≈ {{USD_6M}}).**

## What happens after 180 days?

You cannot simply continue the same C1 cycle with another standard extension.

If you want to stay in Indonesia longer, consider:

- a new C1;
- [D12](/bali/visas/d12/);
- [E33G](/bali/visas/e33g/);
- another suitable [KITAS](/bali/knowledge/kitas-indonesia/) or visa status.

For a longer stay, compare not just the first visa price but the full route that fits your actual plans.

## Is the C1 or a longer-term visa better value?

There is no universal answer.

The C1 can be convenient if you plan to spend several months in Indonesia and do not want to move directly to a KITAS.

If your stay will clearly exceed 180 days, compare the full future route: extensions, exit, a new visa and alternative long-term statuses.

Read more: [Choosing a visa](/bali/knowledge/which-indonesia-visa/).
