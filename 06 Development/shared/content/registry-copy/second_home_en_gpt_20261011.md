# Second Home E33: Long-term Residence in Indonesia

## Quick Answer

**Second Home E33 is a long-term immigration residence route in Indonesia subject to specific financial conditions.** It does not constitute citizenship by investment, a general permit to work or an automatic benefit of purchasing a villa in Bali.

SAFRWAY helps assess whether this route is suitable, understand the financial and documentary requirements, and arrange assistance through qualified specialists.

The availability of the specific visa category and all associated obligations must be confirmed against the current regulations before submitting an application.

## Who May Consider Second Home?

This category may be suitable for people planning a long-term stay in Indonesia who have substantial, verifiable financial resources and are prepared to fulfil the immigration financial guarantee.

It differs from [Investor KITAS E28A](/bali/visas/investor-kitas/), which involves corporate participation and separate requirements for investors, as well as from [E33G Remote Worker](/bali/visas/e33g/) and [Retirement KITAS](/bali/visas/retirement-kitas/).

Holding savings abroad, acquiring property through a lease agreement or owning a company does not, by itself, establish eligibility for E33.

## What Financial Requirements Do We Check?

In the officially published **Pelaporan Proof of Fund** procedure, a Jakarta immigration office describes two alternatives for demonstrating the financial requirements of Second Home.

The applicant must provide evidence of one of the following:

- **Bank funds:** personal funds held in an Indonesian state-owned bank amounting to **at least USD 130,000 or the equivalent**, supported by the appropriate bank certificate and account confirmation.
- **Qualifying property:** an eligible apartment or condominium in Indonesia worth **at least USD 1,000,000 or the equivalent**, supported by the required property ownership documents.

These amounts are **not SAFRWAY application fees**.

They also do not mean that every bank account or every property automatically qualifies.

The type of property right, location, BPN documentation, form of financial evidence and deadlines for fulfilling the relevant obligations must be assessed individually.

For more information, see our [Second Home E33 financial requirements guide](/bali/knowledge/second-home/e33-requirements/).

## Length of Stay and Obligations

Official immigration residence tables list E33 among the long-term categories, with initial residence periods of **five or ten years**, subject to the applicable rules.

The period available to a particular applicant must be confirmed using the selected visa category information and the relevant authority's decision.

After obtaining residence status, the holder may be required to provide evidence of the qualifying funds and maintain the required financial conditions.

**Do not confuse the validity of the entry visa, the duration of the ITAS and the deadline for fulfilling the financial obligation.** These are separate dates that must be monitored.

SAFRWAY does not guarantee that either financial alternative can be completed without additional personal involvement from the applicant.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Second Home E33 Available If I Am Not Retired?

Eligibility depends on the specific visa category and its requirements.

E33 should not automatically be treated as equivalent to the E33F retirement visa.

### Do I Have to Transfer USD 130,000 to the Agency?

No.

The official procedure describes evidence of funds held in an eligible bank account belonging to the applicant, not a transfer of that amount to SAFRWAY.

### Can I Rent a Villa Instead of Purchasing Property?

A lease agreement is not equivalent to the ownership documents required for qualifying property under the immigration financial guarantee.

### Does Second Home E33 Allow Me to Work in Indonesia?

E33 should not be treated as a general authorisation for employment.

If you intend to work for an Indonesian company, the appropriate legal basis must be assessed separately. See [Working KITAS](/bali/visas/working-kitas/).

### Can My Family Join Me?

Residence options for family members and their eligibility requirements must be examined separately.

There is no automatic guarantee that relatives will qualify. See [Family KITAS](/bali/visas/family-kitas/).
