# C1 extension in Bali: how to stay for up to 180 days

If you have already entered Indonesia on a [C1](/bali/visas/c1/) and want to stay beyond the initial 60 days, your current stay can be extended.

The standard sequence is:

- up to 60 days — initial stay;
- up to 120 days — after the first extension;
- up to 180 days — after the second extension.

This means you can stay in Bali for up to roughly half a year without leaving every 30–60 days to obtain a new visa.

## How many times can a C1 be extended?

After the initial 60 days, there are two sequential extension stages.

Each stage adds up to another 60 days.

| Stage | Total stay |
|---|---:|
| Initial entry | up to 60 days |
| First extension | up to 120 days |
| Second extension | up to 180 days |

After reaching 180 days, there is no standard third extension under the same C1 cycle.

## Do you need to leave Indonesia?

No.

A standard C1 extension increases the length of your current stay inside Indonesia.

However, the C1 remains single-entry. If you leave Indonesia, you cannot return on the same C1.

## When should you start the extension?

Do not leave the extension until the final day.

If you already know you want to stay longer, message a SAFRWAY manager in advance.

The manager will check:

- your current C1;
- your entry date;
- your current permitted stay;
- whether you have already extended;
- which next stage remains available.

## What documents are needed?

For an initial review, normally prepare:

- your passport;
- your current C1;
- your entry date;
- proof of the previous extension, if applicable.

If anything else is needed, the manager will tell you after reviewing your case.

## Can SAFRWAY extend a C1 issued by another agency?

Yes.

The fact that another company arranged the original C1 does not prevent you from using SAFRWAY for a later extension.

We first review the current visa, stay period and extension eligibility.

Read more: [Extend your C1 in Bali for another 60 days](/bali/visas/c1/extension/).

## What if the permitted stay has already expired?

If your permitted stay has already expired, do not treat the situation as a routine extension.

Your current immigration status needs to be reviewed first so the next possible step can be identified.

Message a SAFRWAY manager and include the exact expiry date.

## What happens after 180 days?

After the maximum period under the current C1, you need a new immigration scenario.

Depending on your plans, you may:

- leave and apply for a new C1;
- consider [D12](/bali/visas/d12/);
- check whether [E33G](/bali/visas/e33g/) fits;
- consider another suitable [KITAS](/bali/knowledge/kitas-indonesia/) or visa status.

If you are unsure, use the visa-selection guide or message SAFRWAY.

Read more: [Choosing a visa](/bali/knowledge/which-indonesia-visa/).

## FAQ

### How many days does one C1 extension add?

Up to 60 days.

### Can the C1 be extended twice?

Yes. After the initial 60 days, two sequential extension stages can bring the total stay up to 180 days.

### Do I need to leave after 60 days?

No, if you extend the stay.

### Do I have to pay for both extensions at once?

No. Each stage is paid separately.

### How much does one SAFRWAY extension stage cost?

**IDR 2,000,000 (≈ {{USD}}).**

### Can SAFRWAY help if another agency arranged the C1?

Yes. We first review the visa and the next available stage.

### Can I leave after an extension and re-enter on the same C1?

No. The C1 remains single-entry.
