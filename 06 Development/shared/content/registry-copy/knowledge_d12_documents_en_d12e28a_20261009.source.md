## Quick answer

For an initial D12 review, prepare your **passport, photo, three-month bank statement, CV, and travel itinerary**, along with a clear explanation of **which investment project you intend to investigate in Indonesia**. An invitation, correspondence with a prospective partner, or a site-inspection plan may help substantiate your purpose. The exact funds threshold and sponsorship arrangements must be checked against the live D12 procedure, not copied from another visa category.

## Why the purpose of your visit matters

D12 is called *Visa Kunjungan Pra-Investasi* — a multiple-entry pre-investment visit visa. It is meant for assessing investment opportunities or preparing to launch a business. Immigration's description includes **visiting potential sites** and **feasibility studies**.

This is not an employment status or a way to declare any arbitrary purpose just to stay for 180 days. Your application must reflect your actual plans.

## Applicant documents

For an initial review, SAFRWAY asks for:

1. **Passport.** A readable scan of the identification page; validity is checked for the selected D12 option and planned travel.
2. **Color photograph.** A recent photo showing your face properly, without edits that interfere with identification.
3. **Bank statement.** Three months of account history showing the account holder, period, and balances.
4. **CV.** A short summary of professional experience and activities relevant to the project being considered.
5. **Itinerary.** Expected dates, places to visit, tasks, and meetings.
6. **Contact details and address.** Application contact information and intended Indonesian accommodation, as required by the form.

A manager requests additional documents only after confirming the chosen category and current requirements.

## How much money must you show?

Accuracy matters here. Available sources state **different D12 minimums: USD 2,000 and USD 5,000**. Some information is from older or combined official pages; other figures appear in reproduced visa entries and agency practice. SAFRWAY therefore **does not claim a specific bank statement is guaranteed to be sufficient without checking the current eVisa requirements**.

Before filing, a manager verifies the applicable minimum and tells the client. If you are preparing statements early, show your genuine financial history rather than manufacturing transactions. Do not confuse funds for the visit with the 10 billion IDR investment threshold: the latter relates to the separate E28A category.

## What can substantiate a pre-investment project?

Depending on the case, useful evidence includes:

- a description of the business you are considering;
- correspondence with prospective partners or asset sellers;
- an initial meeting schedule;
- confirmation of a proposed site visit;
- documents describing the investment target or sector;
- an invitation or letter from an organization, if it actually exists and is relevant.

These are **examples of relevant evidence**, not a universal exhaustive list required by every Immigration office. If the project is still at an early stage and you have no invitation, do not invent one; explain the real situation to the manager.

## Does D12 require a sponsor?

Published materials differ on this point. One indexed official **D12** entry describes filing through a **penjamin** (guarantor) account with a sponsorship letter; other reproduced D12 descriptions mention self-filing without mandatory sponsorship. **Do not treat answers about D1, D2, or C1 as a universal D12 rule.**

SAFRWAY checks the current eVisa application route and documents for your nationality. If your application requires a sponsor, the manager explains the role and documents before payment.

## Five common mistakes

**1. Describing the purpose as “a one-year vacation.”** Compare tourist categories first rather than disguising a holiday as an investment project.

**2. Assuming a two-year visa means two years of continuous stay.** This is the multiple-entry visa's validity; the initial stay after entry is separately limited to 180 days.

**3. Copying the bank-balance requirement from another visa code.** Check the amount and statement format for D12 specifically.

**4. Submitting fictitious invitations or a nominal “project” with no real basis.** This creates immigration risk.

**5. Choosing D12 for actual employment.** A pre-investment visit is not local employment authorization.

## Frequently asked questions

### Must the company already be incorporated?

No. D12 is for the **pre-investment** stage. You must still explain credibly what you intend to investigate.

### Can I just say that I want to invest?

The filing must reflect your actual circumstances. Required documents depend on the project's stage and current form.

### Is a three-month bank statement sufficient?

Three months is a common statement period, but **the adequacy of the amount and format** for your specific D12 must be confirmed before filing.

### Can I apply without a sponsor?

That depends on the current eVisa procedure and applicant circumstances. We cannot make a universal promise.

### Do I need a 10 billion IDR shareholding for D12?

No. Do not apply E28A requirements to D12: they are different categories serving different purposes.

### Does D12 allow me to start working once a company is registered?

No. The right to work or manage under the appropriate status must be assessed separately.
