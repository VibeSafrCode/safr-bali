## Direct answer

E33G is issued to the remote worker only. A spouse and child are not automatically included. Each family member needs a separate family/dependent ITAS.

The standard mapping is:

**principal — E33G → spouse — E31B → eligible child — E31E.**

## Spouse: E31B

E31B is the family category for a spouse joining a husband or wife who holds ITAS / ITAP.

The spouse applies separately and receives a separate status.

A spouse does not automatically become an E33G holder and does not automatically receive the same work rights as the principal.

## Spouse documents

Normally prepare:

- spouse passport;
- marriage certificate;
- principal holder passport;
- principal E33G / e-ITAS;
- additional current-form information.

Depending on the issuing country, the marriage certificate may require apostille, legalization or translation.

## Child: E31E

E31E is used for an eligible biological child who:

- is under 18;
- is unmarried;
- joins a parent who holds ITAS / ITAP.

## Child documents

Usually:

- child passport;
- birth certificate;
- principal parent passport;
- principal E33G / e-ITAS;
- marriage certificate if needed to connect the family documents;
- other supporting documents depending on the case.

The birth certificate may require apostille / legalization and translation.

## Filing order

SAFRWAY normally builds the case around the principal E33G first.

After the principal documentation/status supports the dependent application, we proceed with family filings.

The exact sequence can vary depending on the live eVisa process and the status already issued to the principal.

## Can the whole family file at once?

Do not assume this is universal.

The sequence depends on:

- principal E33G stage;
- what has already been issued;
- current eVisa mechanics;
- each dependent category;
- family documents.

## Validity period

Dependent family status is separate from the E33G.

Do not assume every dependent document will always have exactly the same validity dates as the principal.

SAFRWAY checks how to align family timelines as cleanly as possible.

## Can the spouse work on E31B?

E31B is a family status, not an independent E33G.

It should not be treated as unrestricted work authorization.

If the spouse works remotely, works for an Indonesian company, runs a business or receives Indonesian income, that activity should be reviewed separately.

## Can the child study?

Family ITAS solves the immigration-status issue, but schools can request their own documents.

## Different citizenships

Family members do not need to share the same nationality.

What matters is documented legal relationship, valid passports and correctly prepared civil-status documents.

## Marriage registered in another country

This is common.

We review where the certificate was issued, whether apostille/legalization is needed and what translation format is appropriate.

## Non-English certificates

Do not translate blindly in advance.

First send the original document to SAFRWAY so we can determine the correct route.

## Non-standard family structures

If:

- parents are unmarried;
- surnames differ;
- one parent is absent from the certificate;
- adoption;
- guardianship;
- second marriage;
- child from a previous marriage,

send the documents first. The correct family category should be based on the real legal relationship.

## Parents of an E33G holder

E31H exists for a parent joining a child who holds ITAS / ITAP.

This is a separate scenario from the standard spouse/child package.

## Do dependents receive the same rights as the E33G holder?

No.

The legal basis is different:

- E33G — remote worker;
- E31B — spouse family reunification;
- E31E — child family reunification.

The principal's permitted activity should not be automatically transferred to dependents.

## When to prepare family documents

It is best to review family documents before or while preparing the principal E33G:

- passports;
- marriage certificate;
- birth certificates;
- spelling of names;
- apostille / legalization;
- translations;
- passport validity.

## FAQ

### Is the spouse automatically included in E33G?
No.

### Which category is used for the spouse?
E31B.

### Which category is used for the child?
E31E for an eligible biological child under 18 who is unmarried and joins an ITAS/ITAP-holding parent.

### Can family be filed after E33G?
Yes.

### Should family documents be prepared early?
Yes.

### Can the spouse work on family KITAS?
Do not treat the family ITAS as unrestricted work authorization. The actual activity should be reviewed separately.

### Can parents be included?
There is a separate E31H parent scenario.

### Do certificates need translation?
It depends on country and language. Send originals first.

### What if the family structure is unusual?
Send the documents to SAFRWAY so the correct category and filing order can be determined.
