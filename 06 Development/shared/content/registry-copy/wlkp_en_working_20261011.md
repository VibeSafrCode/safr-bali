# WLKP Online: employer registration and reporting in Indonesia

## Short answer

**WLKP (Wajib Lapor Ketenagakerjaan di Perusahaan)** is mandatory company employment reporting in Indonesia. It is an employer obligation, not a visa or an individual foreign-worker permit.

When hiring foreign employees, keeping WLKP information up to date is particularly important for checking the company's records. But WLKP also applies to employers hiring local staff. Its legal basis and online reporting procedures are separate from [RPTKA](/en/bali/business/foreign-worker-permits/).

## Why WLKP does not replace other procedures

WLKP reporting, RPTKA foreign-worker approvals and obtaining a [Working KITAS](/en/bali/visas/working-kitas/) deal with different obligations. One registration does not automatically prove compliance with the other two.

Business changes may also call for a related review of [OSS/NIB](/en/bali/business/nib-oss/) and [corporate documents](/en/bali/business/corporate-changes/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is WLKP a work permit for a foreigner?

No. It is employer reporting, not a personal document for a foreign employee.

### Is WLKP required only for KITAS cases?

No. Corporate employment reporting obligations exist independently of work-visa applications.

### Is an NIB enough to demonstrate WLKP compliance?

An NIB alone does not prove that every WLKP record and report is accurate. The company's system record must be checked.

### When should WLKP be updated?

When required by law, including relevant company changes and the employer's ongoing obligations. Specific deadlines are checked against the current procedure.

### Can a contractor handle WLKP?

Authority and system access must be assessed separately. The company remains responsible for the accuracy of its information.

### Are WLKP and RPTKA one service?

They are separate tasks. We can coordinate them in one hiring project, but scope each distinctly.
