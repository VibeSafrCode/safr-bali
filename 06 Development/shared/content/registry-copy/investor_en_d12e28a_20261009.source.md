# Investor KITAS E28A — residence in Indonesia based on investment

If you are investing in an Indonesian company and want your residence to be connected to that business, consider Investor KITAS E28A.

This is neither a tourist visa nor the same status as E33G for remote employees of a foreign company. Your basis here is your participation in a company in Indonesia.

**Message a SAFRWAY manager — we will review the company's documents, your shareholding, and your intended role to identify the appropriate application route.**

## Who Investor KITAS is for

The standard scenario is a foreign investor whose ownership of an Indonesian company is supported by documents. Before filing, it is important to verify not only the applicant's passport but also the corporate basis itself.

If the company has not yet been registered, the ownership structure is changing, or the business is only being planned, tell the manager. We first establish the sequence of corporate and visa steps rather than apply without a documented basis.

## How long is E28A valid?

Under the current classification, E28A is a **two-year Investor ITAS/KITAS**. Leaving Indonesia and returning is subject to having a valid re-entry permit.

Extensions are provided for, but the company's status, your ownership, and relevant documents must be reviewed before applying. Meeting the requirements for an initial application does not automatically mean any later renewal will be approved.

## What is checked about the investment?

Published E28A requirements refer to ownership of shares in the sponsoring company worth at least **10,000,000,000 IDR**.

**This is a requirement concerning the applicant's shareholding, not the price of a KITAS application.** Another shareholder's stake or a statement that “the company has capital” cannot substitute for your own qualifying participation.

SAFRWAY checks how your shareholding is recorded in the company's documents. Share capital, the allocation of shares, and the individual applicant's KITAS basis must be assessed separately.

If the stake is below the threshold, do not automatically attempt to apply as an investor. A director or commissioner may need a different visa category appropriate to their position.

## Can you manage a company?

E28A allows investment activity and those director or commissioner functions covered by the category in the company in which you invested.

This does not grant unrestricted permission to work in any occupation or for any company. You must match your actual activities with the immigration basis before filing.

Owning shares and carrying out separate operational work, for example, are not identical. Tell the manager what you will actually do, not only the position you hope to put on paper.

## Company and sponsor

E28A uses a sponsoring company. The standard investor scenario involves an Indonesian foreign-investment company, or **PT PMA**.

SAFRWAY initially checks incorporation records, ownership details, your shareholding, and any appointment to a relevant company role. For the standard E28A, the sponsor must meet PT PMA requirements and remain active.

Current official requirements also refer to the company's banking history for the past two months. Newly created companies are subject to a special route for providing this information after ITAS issuance; we verify its applicability under the live procedure before filing.

We request further corporate documents based on the review findings.

Paying for a visa application service does not itself create a qualifying investment.

## What to prepare for an initial review

Start with your passport, incorporation and current company documents, evidence of your ownership, and information about your intended activities. Have a photo and any existing bank documents ready as well.

The manager compares the package against current E28A requirements and explains what the applicant and company must add. Until then, there is no reason to order unrelated translations or amend company documents solely to match a visa title.

## If the company has not been formed yet

Setting up a business and obtaining personal immigration status are related but different tasks. Through SAFRWAY, you can first discuss company formation and the required sequence with a specialist partner.

If your current goal is to explore an investment opportunity on the ground, consider [D12](/bali/visas/d12/) separately. D12 is not a replacement for Investor KITAS and does not guarantee a later in-country conversion.

## Investor KITAS, D12, and E33G

**D12** is for pre-investment visits and researching a project.

**Investor KITAS E28A** is for living in Indonesia on a documented investment basis.

**E33G** is a separate option for remote work for a foreign company. See [E33G Remote Worker KITAS](/bali/visas/e33g/).

The word “KITAS” does not make these grounds interchangeable. The right choice depends on actual employment, investment, and documents.

## When must you enter after issuance?

Current official E28A information states that the issued visa must be used for entry **within 90 days of issuance**. The window for first entry and the duration of ITAS after entry are different periods.

## Family

We consider spouse and children's residence together with the principal applicant's case, but each family member needs their own appropriate status.

We do not automatically transfer the principal holder's work or investment rights to relatives. The specific family category is checked against relationship documents and the principal's actual immigration basis.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are Investor KITAS and E33G the same?

No. They are based on different grounds: investing in an Indonesian company versus remotely working for a foreign company.

### Can E28A be obtained without a qualifying company?

Do not assume this is a standard option. The corporate and investment basis must be assessed first.

### Is 10 billion IDR the visa price?

No. Official requirements use this amount for the value of the applicant's shares in the sponsoring company. SAFRWAY's two-year E28A application price is **16,000,000 IDR**. Any corporate services are agreed separately.

### Can I work anywhere after obtaining E28A?

No. Your activities must match the issued status and your role in the company.

### May I leave and come back?

Yes, with a valid re-entry permit and compliance with the other conditions of your status.

### Can my family apply too?

Family scenarios are checked separately. Relatives are not automatically covered by the principal applicant's E28A.
