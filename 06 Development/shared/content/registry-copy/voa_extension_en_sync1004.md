# Extend your VOA / eVOA in Bali for another 30 days

If you are already in Bali on a VOA or eVOA and 30 days is not enough, you can extend the stay once for up to another 30 days.

The total stay on the same entry can therefore reach up to 60 days.

With SAFRWAY, you do not need to work out which extension route fits your exact type of VOA. We check your current visa and stay period first and then support the appropriate process.

## Who is this service for?

It is suitable if:

- you are already in Indonesia on a valid VOA or eVOA;
- you want to stay beyond the initial 30 days;
- you have not used the one available extension yet;
- you want SAFRWAY to handle the review and extension support.

## How much longer does the extension give?

The standard VOA/eVOA initial stay is up to 30 days.

One extension is available for up to another 30 days.

Total: up to 60 days on one entry.

There is no second standard 30-day extension under the same VOA/eVOA cycle.

## FAQ

### Can VOA be extended without leaving Indonesia?
Yes. The standard VOA / eVOA allows one extension for up to another 30 days.

### How long can you stay after extension?
Up to 60 days on the same entry: up to 30 days initially and up to another 30 after extension.

### Can it be extended a second time?
Not within the standard VOA / eVOA cycle. The next visa route needs to be considered instead.

### Are eVOA and VOA extended identically?
The result is the same, up to another 30 days, but the technical route may differ depending on issuance. SAFRWAY checks your current visa first.

### Can SAFRWAY handle the process?
We handle the review and process support. If your case requires personal attendance or another action from you, we tell you in advance.

### What if my permitted stay has already expired?
Do not start a routine extension without checking your status and possible overstay consequences. Send your documents and entry date to the manager so the next step can be assessed.
