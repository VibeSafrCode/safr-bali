# A bank account that fits your situation

Do you need an account for everyday spending, regular transfers or living in Indonesia? We first discuss how you plan to use it and which documents you already hold.

SAFRWAY helps you prepare your bank enquiry and coordinate the agreed assistance. We do not promise an account at any bank: an available option must be checked against your profile.

## What we discuss

Your current stay status, the purpose of the account, preferred currency and typical transactions. We then check the requirements for a suitable option and explain the next steps.

Opening the account is not the only consideration. Before choosing, it is useful to understand fees, restrictions, the banking app and ongoing servicing arrangements. Specific conditions should be confirmed with the bank rather than assumed from another customer's experience.

## How we help

We gather the initial information, agree on the preparation scope and explain what you need to provide. Where included in your package, we coordinate practical arrangements and communication.

The bank makes the final decision on providing the service. Our assistance does not replace its checks or requirements.

## Cost

We agree on the assistance fee in advance and distinguish it from bank charges, deposits or other bank conditions.
