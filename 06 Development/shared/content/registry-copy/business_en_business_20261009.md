# Business in Indonesia: From an Idea to an Operating Company

## Quick answer

**SAFRWAY helps coordinate PT PMA incorporation, NIB/OSS registration, corporate changes and company closure in Indonesia.** The specific permits depend on your industry and documents; each service is quoted individually.

Indonesia is a large market, but starting a business here should not begin with buying a ready-made document package. First establish **what your business will actually do, who will own it and which permits your particular project requires**.

SAFRWAY coordinates corporate services in Indonesia, from forming a PT PMA and arranging business permits to updating company records and closing a company. We work with qualified local providers for legal and notarial matters; the participants' roles and responsibilities are agreed separately.

## How we can help

### Establish a PT PMA

Where a foreign investor is among the shareholders, a **PT PMA**—an Indonesian company with foreign investment—may be an appropriate structure. We check the industry, permitted foreign ownership, shareholder structure and investment plan, then coordinate notarial documents, company registration and next steps. [Learn about PT PMA formation](/en/bali/business/pt-pma/).

### Obtain an NIB and sector-specific permits

An **NIB** is a business identification number in the OSS system. The required licences nevertheless depend on the **KBLI** activity classification, risk level, project location and sector-specific rules. We establish what must be in place before operations begin. [Review OSS and licensing requirements](/en/bali/business/oss-licenses/).

### Make changes to a company

Have shareholders, directors, the address, company name, capital or business activities changed? We will outline the required corporate updates and identify what must be aligned across **AHU, OSS and other applicable registers**. [Corporate amendments](/en/bali/business/company-changes/).

### Close a company

Closing a PT PMA is more than cancelling an NIB: it can involve a corporate resolution, liquidation, settling creditors, taxes and formal termination of legal-entity status. We start by reviewing the company's position. [PT PMA closure and liquidation](/en/bali/business/company-closure/).

## A company and an Investor KITAS are different matters

Owning a PT PMA does not by itself guarantee an **E28A Investor KITAS**. Immigration eligibility has separate conditions, including the individual investor's interest in the company and the company's qualifications. [E28A Investor KITAS](/en/bali/visas/investor-kitas/) is a separate service.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a foreign national establish a company in Indonesia?

Yes. A PT PMA may be used in sectors open to foreign investment. The specific activity, permitted foreign ownership and company structure must be checked first.

### What is included in company registration?

The exact scope is specified in our proposal. Notarial incorporation, NIB, sectoral licences, banking and tax matters are separate stages and are not automatically included in a basic service.

### Is an NIB enough to operate?

Not always. Additional permits and approvals depend on the KBLI code and risk level.

### Can I form a company and immediately receive an Investor KITAS?

These are separate procedures with different requirements. PT PMA formation alone does not guarantee E28A eligibility.

### How much does company formation or closure cost?

It depends on the existing documents, sector and work required. SAFRWAY prepares a tailored quote.

### Do you work only in Bali?

We can coordinate business services across Indonesia when an appropriate qualified local provider is available in the relevant jurisdiction and region.
