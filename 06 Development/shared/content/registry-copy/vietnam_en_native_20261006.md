# Vietnam Visa — e-Visa Application Assistance and Trip Preparation

Planning a trip to Vietnam for a few weeks or months? First, it is worth checking whether you need a visa, how long you are permitted to stay and whether the option you choose fits your itinerary.

SAFRWAY can help you understand the entry requirements, prepare documents for an electronic visa and check your details before you submit an application. If you plan to make several trips, we will consider the multiple-entry option in advance.

**Message a manager — tell us your citizenship, travel dates and whether you plan to leave Vietnam and return.**

## Do you need a visa?

Not everyone needs a visa: the rules depend on your citizenship, passport, purpose of travel and length of stay.

For example, Russian citizens can enter without a visa for up to **45 days**, provided they meet the entry requirements. The applicable decision of the Government of Vietnam provides for this arrangement from 15 March 2025 to 14 March 2028.

If your trip falls within the visa-free period available to you, we will check that option first. If visa-free entry is not suitable, we will consider applying for a visa.

We check the conditions for other citizenships separately.

## Electronic visa for up to 90 days

A Vietnamese e-Visa may be issued for **up to 90 days** and can be single-entry or multiple-entry.

A **single-entry e-Visa** is suitable for one trip: you enter Vietnam and then leave.

A **multiple-entry e-Visa** allows you to enter and leave Vietnam several times during its period of validity. This option is worth considering if you want to visit a neighboring country and return to Vietnam.

It is important to look not only at the words “up to 90 days” but also at the start and end dates on the issued visa. Re-entering on a multiple-entry e-Visa should not be treated as the start of a new 90-day period: the validity dates of the specific document are what matter.

## Which option should you consider?

| Your plans | What we check |
|---|---|
| A short trip with no departures | Whether visa-free entry is available for your citizenship |
| A trip longer than your available visa-free period | Whether an e-Visa covers the required dates |
| Several entries during the trip | A multiple-entry e-Visa and its overall validity period |
| You are already in Vietnam or plan a longer stay | Your current status and a separate permissible procedure for the next steps |

## What SAFRWAY does

First, we ask about your citizenship, the country where you are currently staying, your travel dates and the purpose of your trip. We then check whether you need an e-Visa and which entry option fits your itinerary.

Before submission, we check your passport details, photograph and the information in your application. We pay particular attention to the dates and the selected port of entry: electronic visas can be used at international border crossings on Vietnam's approved list.

Before we begin the application process, we agree on the scope of the service and its final cost. Once the result is available, we pass the document to you and explain what details to check before your trip.

## What to prepare for the initial review

To get started, send the manager:

- your citizenship and the country where you are currently located;
- a clear image of the page of your passport containing your personal details;
- a photograph for the application;
- your intended entry and exit dates, port of entry and accommodation address;
- information about any planned departures from Vietnam and subsequent returns.

This list is for preliminary work with SAFRWAY. We check the final application requirements against the current form and your circumstances. For an electronic application, the official instructions require an image of the passport page and a portrait photograph.

If you are traveling with your family, we check each person's documents and entry conditions separately. We do not assume that the conditions for one person automatically apply to everyone else.

## If you are already in Vietnam

Tell the manager right away on what basis you entered and when your permitted stay expires.

The standard e-Visa application procedure is intended for foreign nationals who are outside Vietnam. You should therefore not automatically assume that a new electronic visa can be obtained as an extension while remaining in the country.

In that situation, we first check your current status and the available procedure for further steps. We do not promise an extension without leaving the country or a new visa through one universal process.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does everyone need an e-Visa for Vietnam?

No. Visa-free entry is available to some citizenships. For example, Russian citizens may enter for up to 45 days, subject to the entry requirements. We first check whether you need a visa in your circumstances.

### Can I get a visa for multiple entries?

Yes, a multiple-entry e-Visa is available. Your trips must fall within the validity period of the issued document.

### Does a multiple-entry e-Visa give me a new 90 days after each entry?

No. We do not apply another country's rules to it: we check the validity dates of the specific e-Visa and the permitted period of stay.

### Can I apply for an e-Visa myself?

Yes. You can apply online yourself through the official portal. SAFRWAY offers assistance to people who would prefer a specialist to check their information and help prepare the application.

### What should I do if I am already in Vietnam?

Tell the manager the basis on which you entered and the date your permitted stay ends. Your current status must be checked first; we do not turn the standard e-Visa application process from abroad into a promise of an in-country extension.

### Can I contact you about an urgent trip?

Yes. Tell us your departure date and whether you already have an application in progress or an issued visa. We will check the available options and limitations before agreeing on the service.
