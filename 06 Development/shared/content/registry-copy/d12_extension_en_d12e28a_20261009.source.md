## Quick answer

A D12 visa grants an initial stay of up to **180 days** per entry. Under published rules, this stay can be extended by **up to another 180 days**, provided the total continuous stay for that visit does not exceed **12 months**. SAFRWAY assists with a D12 extension for **7,000,000 IDR**. This extends your permission to stay, not the overall validity of your multiple-entry visa.

## D12 extension price

**7,000,000 IDR for one extension stage.** The website shows the USD equivalent using SAFRWAY's existing conversion system. Any additional services required under a separately agreed scenario are discussed before payment. **No expedited D12-extension tariff has been approved**; it must not be presented as an available service.

The price of a new D12 visa is different: for one year, **7,500,000 IDR** standard or **9,500,000 IDR** expedited; for two years, **10,500,000 IDR** standard or **13,000,000 IDR** expedited. Applying for a new visa and extending an existing stay are different procedures. [Apply for D12](/bali/visas/d12/).

## Visa validity versus permitted stay

**Visa validity** is the period during which the multiple-entry D12 can be used for travel. **Permitted stay** is the actual date until which you may remain in Indonesia after your latest entry. The initial D12 stay is up to 180 days.

If you are already in Indonesia and want to stay without leaving, it is your current **Izin Tinggal Kunjungan (ITK)** — visit stay permit — that is extended. A long-validity D12 visa does not protect you from overstaying if the permitted stay expires earlier.

## How long can you remain?

A typical single-visit scenario:

1. **Initial stay:** up to 180 days following entry.
2. **Next stage:** up to 180 further days if the extension is approved.
3. **Limit:** up to 12 months in total for the current visit under applicable rules.

Approval is not automatic. You must have lawful status, submit a correct application, and meet D12 conditions.

## When to contact SAFRWAY

Contact us in advance, **before your current permitted stay ends**. An Immigration office's published guidance describes an ITK application window starting no earlier than 14 days before expiry and ending on the last valid day; this is an **official application window, not a SAFRWAY processing-time promise**. We check the applicable window and local procedure before starting your case.

Do not plan to submit on the final day: system outages, additional-document requests, and an in-person Immigration visit may be necessary.

## Documents you may need

For the initial review, prepare:

- your passport and valid D12 visa;
- evidence of your most recent entry and the expiration date of your current ITK;
- your Indonesian residential address, including the postal code;
- a short explanation of your continuing pre-investment purpose;
- supporting documents, if available: meeting correspondence, an invitation, site-inspection plans, or project details;
- sponsor documents **if a sponsor is part of your application**.

Official ITK guidance lists a valid passport, a sponsor declaration where applicable, and a statement describing the purpose of your stay. The precise documents and filing procedure depend on the case and current rules.

## Do you need to visit Immigration?

Under procedure updates published in 2025–2026, applicants should **plan for an in-person visit for a photograph, biometrics, and an interview** after submitting online, unless Immigration provides an exemption for the individual case. SAFRWAY confirms which office to attend and the applicable steps in advance.

## If you plan to leave Indonesia

An extension is not always necessary. If you leave before your current stay expires and your multiple-entry D12 remains valid, a new permitted stay may begin when you return, subject to applicable entry requirements. Entry is always at the discretion of border officers.

If you need to remain continuously beyond the first 180 days, consider extending your current ITK instead.

## Does extending D12 allow you to work or obtain KITAS?

No. An extension does not turn D12 into a work authorization or Investor KITAS. Exploring an investment and actually working are different permitted purposes.

We also **do not promise a standard direct D12 → ITAS conversion**. Immigration's published description of the category permits extension of visit status but not direct conversion to ITAS. If you are ready to manage a business or need another immigration status, we assess the available lawful route, which may involve leaving Indonesia and applying for a new visa. ## Frequently asked questions

### Is a D12 extension for 30 or 60 days?

Neither standard period applies. D12 follows a separate procedure: published rules provide for an extension of up to 180 days, not the usual 60-day stages of D1/D2.

### What is included in the 7 million IDR price?

This is SAFRWAY's approved fee for assisting with one D12 extension stage. Exceptional additional services are agreed in advance. There is no implied promise of expedited processing.

### Can I extend D12 beyond one year for a single visit?

The published total limit for one visit is 12 months. If you need a different duration or basis, we assess lawful next steps.

### Must I attend in person?

Under the current published procedure, you should expect photography/biometrics and an interview for an extension. We check whether any exception applies to your case.

### What if my ITK has already expired?

This is no longer a routine extension. Your status and the consequences of overstay must be assessed first.

### Does a D12 extension automatically grant Investor KITAS?

No. They are different legal immigration grounds.
