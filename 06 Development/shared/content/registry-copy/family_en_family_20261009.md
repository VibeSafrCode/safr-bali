# Family KITAS in Indonesia — Residence Permits for Spouses, Children and Parents

## Quick Answer

Family KITAS is a family-based residence option in Indonesia when a close relative already holds an eligible ITAS/KITAS or ITAP/KITAP. A spouse, child or parent must obtain their own immigration status; they are not automatically covered by the principal applicant's visa. The appropriate category depends on the family relationship, the principal holder's specific status and the eVisa rules in force.

| Joining family member | Category in the general classification | Key consideration |
|---|---|---|
| Husband or wife | **E31B** | Legally valid marriage and an eligible ITAS/ITAP held by the spouse |
| Biological child | **E31E** | Under 18 and unmarried; documented parent-child relationship with an ITAS/ITAP holder |
| Father or mother | **E31H** | Parent-child relationship with the son or daughter holding ITAS/ITAP; actual eligibility must be checked |

## Family KITAS Is Not One Visa for the Whole Family

When one person obtains an Investor KITAS, employment-based KITAS or another temporary residence permit, their spouse and children do not automatically receive residence rights. Each dependent applicant has a separate passport, supporting documents and Immigration decision.

SAFRWAY helps identify the correct family category, prepare proof of relationship and arrange an application when there is a lawful basis and an available procedure. Our services are not limited to holders of [E33G Remote Worker KITAS](/bali/visas/e33g/): we also assess family cases involving other ITAS/ITAP categories where the rules allow dependent applications.

## Three Main Scenarios

### Spouse — E31B

If the husband or wife holds a valid ITAS/ITAP, the general classification provides E31B for the other spouse. We check the marriage certificate, principal holder's documents, applicable dates and the possibility of applying in the specific circumstances.

[Learn about KITAS for a spouse](/bali/visas/family-kitas/spouse/).

### Child — E31E

Under the published classification, E31E covers an unmarried biological child under 18 joining a parent who holds ITAS/ITAP. Adoption, guardianship, stepchildren, missing legal proof of parentage or turning 18 require a separate legal assessment: E31E must not be selected automatically.

[KITAS for a child](/bali/visas/child-kitas/).

### Parents — E31H

E31H is intended for a parent joining a son or daughter who holds ITAS/ITAP. Before applying, the principal holder's status, actual family relationship and current availability of the category must be checked. In particular, it must not be assumed that every E33G or Golden Visa supports every type of dependent visa.

[KITAS for parents](/bali/visas/family-kitas/parents/).

## What We Need to Get Started

An initial assessment usually requires basic information about both parties:

**Principal holder:** nationality, type and validity period of the current ITAS/ITAP, passport, permitted-stay expiry date and intended address in Indonesia.

**Family member:** nationality, passport, child's age (if applicable), marriage or relationship document, current country of residence and current visa if already in Indonesia.

We will also check whether translations, an apostille or consular legalization, financial evidence, sponsorship or category-specific documents are required. Do not order apostilles or translations before the correct sequence has been established.

## Can You Apply Without Leaving Indonesia?

Eligibility for an in-country application depends on the person's current immigration status and the permitted procedure. We do not promise an onshore conversion simply because a family member holds KITAS. If departure and a new application are required, SAFRWAY will explain the route in advance.

## How Long Is a Family KITAS Valid?

The general E31 framework includes limited-stay options of different durations. The available duration depends on the exact visa index, the type and remaining validity of the principal ITAS/ITAP and the application process. A dependent's permit should not automatically be assumed to have the same end date as the principal KITAS.

If the principal holder's permit will expire soon, we will first discuss its renewal and the sequence of family applications to avoid obtaining dependent status with an impractically short validity period.

## Can You Work on a Family KITAS?

**Family KITAS is not, by itself, a universal work permit.** If a spouse intends to take a job with an Indonesian company, run a business or undertake other paid work, the permitted activities and applicable requirements must be checked separately. The principal applicant's right to work or conduct business does not automatically pass to family members.

## Family KITAS for E33G Holders: An Additional Check

The general E31B/E31E/E31H classification does not mean that all three categories are technically available to every E33G/Golden Visa holder. In these cases we check the principal holder's actual status and Immigration's live eVisa process before promising a particular category.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can One Family KITAS Cover Everyone?

No. Each family member needs a separate application under the appropriate category.

### Is Family KITAS Available Only to the Family of an E33G Holder?

No. The general E31 framework covers different family grounds, including relatives of holders of other types of ITAS/ITAP.

### If I Hold a Two-Year Investor KITAS, Will My Family Receive Two Years Too?

That duration may be available in an eligible case, but it cannot be guaranteed without checking the dependent category and the principal holder's current permit.

### Can a Spouse Work on E31B?

E31B should not be treated as a universal work authorization. Employment and the source of income need a separate assessment.

### Can the Parents of a KITAS Holder Apply?

E31H exists in the general classification. Eligibility to apply depends on the principal holder's status and family documents.

### What If My Spouse Is an Indonesian Citizen?

That is a different basis: E31A, not E31B. We will assess your mixed-nationality marriage separately.

### How Much Does It Cost?

SAFRWAY has not set a fixed price. A manager calculates the price for each applicant after reviewing the documents and application route.
