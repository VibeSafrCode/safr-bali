# Apostille of Documents in Indonesia: When It Is Needed and How to Apply

## Quick answer

**An apostille in Indonesia applies to eligible documents originating in Indonesia and covered by the Apostille Convention, when they are to be used in another country where the Convention applies in the relevant relationship.** It authenticates an official signature, seal or signatory’s capacity; it does not establish that every statement in the document is true. SAFRWAY helps determine whether the procedure applies and coordinates the process through the current AHU system.

Whether you have a certificate, notarial deed, official letter or corporate document, the first step is to establish its legal category. Some records are eligible; others are excluded or require a different process.

## Documents commonly assessed for apostille

- Civil-status records that meet issuance and format requirements.
- Educational documents and official certificates.
- Certain notarial instruments and signature certifications.
- Some company documents and evidence of representatives’ authority, where they qualify as public or notarial documents.
- Other official documents accepted by the competent authority and destination country.

**Not all commercial, customs, tax and corporate papers qualify.** The Convention excludes certain administrative documents directly dealing with commercial or customs operations. Each file is evaluated by its legal nature, not merely the word “business” in its title. Where the procedure is unavailable, we consider [legalization](/en/bali/documents/legalization/).

## Changes in 2025–2026

Since **17 December 2025**, **Permenkum No. 50/2025** has governed the Indonesian apostille process, replacing the 2022 rules. In June 2026, AHU officially announced the move of applications to **[layanan.ahu.go.id](https://layanan.ahu.go.id)**. Old instructions referring to `apostille.ahu.go.id` should not automatically be used for new applications.

Once the document has been reviewed by AHU, the applicant receives information about payment and collection of the certificate or sticker. Availability of expedited procedures, the current government fee and collection location must be checked through the live system: AHU published fee updates in 2026.

## When an apostille is needed — and when it is not

1. The document must originate within the jurisdiction of the competent apostille authority.
2. The destination country must allow the Convention’s application in the relevant relationship.
3. The particular type of document must fall within its scope and applicable limitations.
4. The recipient may also prescribe the form of copy, recency of a certificate, translation language and other conditions.

A document **issued outside Indonesia** is normally apostilled by the competent body **in its country of origin**, not by Indonesia’s AHU. Specific arrangements and exceptions must be checked for the case.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does an apostille verify the document’s content?

No. It authenticates legally relevant features of an official document’s origin, not the truth of every statement in it.

### Can an Indonesian apostille be applied to a Russian document?

For a Russian-origin document, the usual approach is to check apostille services offered by the competent Russian authority and the destination country’s requirements. Indonesia’s AHU is not a universal apostille authority for foreign records.

### Do I need an apostille for every country?

No. You must check whether and how the Convention applies between the states involved, and whether the document falls into the relevant category.

### Does an apostille replace translation?

No. A foreign recipient may require a [sworn or certified translation](/en/bali/documents/certified-translations/), according to its own rules.

### Can every PT PMA document be apostilled?

No. Notarial and official corporate documents are assessed separately from records directly related to commercial or customs operations. A [corporate package review](/en/bali/documents/corporate/) can help establish the appropriate route.

### Does an apostille guarantee acceptance of the document?

No. The recipient may still check the document type, date of issue, translation and other formal requirements.
