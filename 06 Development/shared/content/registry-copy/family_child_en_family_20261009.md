# KITAS for a Child in Indonesia — E31E

## Quick Answer

**E31E** is a category for an **unmarried biological child under 18** joining a parent who holds ITAS/ITAP. The child needs their own immigration application. The main requirements are a legally documented parent-child relationship and eligible current status of the principal holder.

## When Does a Child Need Their Own KITAS?

If you relocate to Indonesia on an employment-based or Investor KITAS, your child's immigration documents are not automatically issued together with your permit. For lawful long-term residence, the child's appropriate family category must be assessed and a separate application filed.

Age is not the only consideration: family documents, passport validity, the parent's permit duration and the child's entry history if already in Bali also matter.

## What to Check Before Filing for E31E

- the child is under 18 when the application is submitted;
- the child is unmarried;
- the child is the biological child of the principal ITAS/ITAP holder, with documents proving it;
- the parent’s ITAS/ITAP type and remaining validity support a family application;
- the child has a valid passport and the required certificates;
- the chosen filing procedure is permitted given the child's current immigration status.

For an adopted child, child under guardianship, stepchild or a child whose parent's details differ in the documents, **we do not promise E31E automatically**. The suitable family basis must first be established.

## Child's Documents

The initial review usually requires the child's passport and birth certificate, the parent's passport and ITAS/ITAP, and the other parent's details where relevant to family verification and filing.

A parents' marriage certificate, other parent's consent, name-change documents, guardianship judgment, apostille or translation may be required in certain cases. This is not one universal mandatory list for every family.

[Detailed guide to family documents](/bali/knowledge/family-kitas/documents/).

## Can a Child Be Added to a Parent's KITAS?

No. A family relationship can establish the application basis but **does not replace the child's own permit**. Immigration status must be issued in the child's name.

## Duration of a Child's ITAS

The permitted duration depends on the E31 category, the parent's permit term and current rules. A child cannot be promised the same two years merely because the parent has a two-year Investor KITAS. We check both periods together to avoid a gap in residence status during the family move.

## What Happens When the Child Turns 18?

This is a separate milestone to plan for. A minor's status must not be assumed to continue unchanged once the age condition changes. If the child will soon turn 18, tell us early so we can review their current status and lawful next option.

## Can the Child Attend School?

Family ITAS addresses lawful residence but does not automatically enroll the child in a specific school. Schools may have their own documentation and age requirements.

## If the Parent Holds E33G

In the general classification, the biological child of an ITAS holder falls under E31E, but for E33G/Golden Visa specifically, eligibility must additionally be checked in the live electronic system. Until then, we do not promise every E33G holder a dependent-child filing under a particular visa index.

[Family of an E33G holder](/bali/knowledge/e33g/family/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a Child Automatically Included in a Parent's KITAS?

No. A separate immigration application is required.

### If the Child Is Adopted, Does E31E Apply?

The index cannot be determined without reviewing adoption or guardianship documents. The general E31E classification specifically refers to an unmarried biological child under 18.

### Can a Child Obtain KITAS If the Parents Are Not Married?

Documented parentage and the suitable category must be checked. The parents' unmarried status does not remove the need to examine the birth certificate and other legal grounds.

### Does the Child's Permit Always Expire at the Same Time as the Parent's?

There is no guarantee. The available duration is determined by the particular grounds and current rules.

### How Much Does a Child's KITAS Cost?

The price is calculated after reviewing the documents, requested duration and application route.
