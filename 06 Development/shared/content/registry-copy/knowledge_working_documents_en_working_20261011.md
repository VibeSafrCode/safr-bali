# Working KITAS E23 documents: employer and employee checklist

## Short answer

**Two parties prepare Working KITAS documents: the Indonesian employer and the foreign professional.** The company must substantiate the lawful employment basis and required manpower approvals; the applicant must establish identity and eligibility for the approved role. There is no single document list for every professional category.

The following is a preliminary assessment guide, not a universal mandatory eVisa form.

## 1. Employer documents

The review usually begins with current company documentation: incorporation, NIB, tax details, actual business activity, address and authorised representative. We then check whether [RPTKA](/en/bali/business/foreign-worker-permits/) is needed and in place, along with related manpower approvals and [WLKP](/en/bali/business/wlkp/) reporting.

Not every company needs the same documents in the same form. For example, a change of director or activity may require AHU/OSS updates before applying for the working status.

## 2. Foreign professional's details

An initial assessment typically requires nationality and a valid passport, role and experience description, qualifications where relevant, intended work location and current immigration status.

Passport-validity rules, photograph formats, evidence of experience, diplomas or medical documents must be taken from the particular visa-index and sector requirements. Do not treat an old checklist for another KITAS as universally applicable.

## 3. Manpower-approval documents

Depending on the case, the employer may need to document the eligible role, employment period, local-staff requirements and completion of applicable procedures. A separate **DKPTKA** payment may apply; it is not a visa agency commission.

## 4. If the person is already in Indonesia

We will also need the current visa or ITAS, evidence of extensions and exact permitted-stay expiry date. These determine whether a [Working KITAS status change](/en/bali/visas/working-kitas/conversion/) may be possible or a new entry process is required.

## 5. Checks before sharing documents

- Names and identifying details match across documents.
- The position reflects the work actually performed.
- Company records, address and signatory authority are up to date.
- Employment authorisation period and intended immigration route are aligned.
- Any documents requiring notarisation or [certified translation](/en/bali/documents/certified-translations/) are identified.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are a passport and employment contract enough?

No. The company and applicable employer permits must also be checked for a working immigration basis.

### Can I use any acquaintance as a sponsor?

A lawful basis and valid employer/sponsor authority are necessary. Fictitious relationships are not an acceptable route.

### Is a degree required for every role?

Requirements vary by profession and permitted category. We check the qualification requirements for the actual proposed role.

### Is WLKP an employee document?

No. WLKP is company reporting and cannot be replaced by the applicant's personal documents.

### Must every document be translated into Indonesian?

The list and required language depend on the procedure. Check the receiving authority's requirements first.

### What should I send in the first message?

Initially, describe the role, employer, nationality and current status. Passports and sensitive information should be shared through an agreed secure process.
