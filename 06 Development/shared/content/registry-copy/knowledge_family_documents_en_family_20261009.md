# Documents for Family KITAS in Indonesia: Preparing Your Family's Application

## Quick Answer

A Family KITAS application requires documents proving **the applicant's identity, the principal family member's valid status and their legal relationship**. For a spouse, the key document is the marriage certificate; for children and parents, birth and relationship documents. The full set depends on the E31B/E31E/E31H category, country of issue and current filing procedure.

## Identify the Category First

Do not begin by ordering translations. First determine which basis applies:

- **E31B** — spouse of an ITAS/ITAP holder;
- **E31E** — unmarried biological child under 18 whose parent holds ITAS/ITAP;
- **E31H** — parent of an ITAS/ITAP holder.

If the other spouse is an Indonesian citizen, a separate **E31A** basis is generally considered. Adoption, guardianship, stepchildren and unusual documents require an individual category assessment.

## Checklist: Principal KITAS Holder

1. Passport.
2. Current visa or ITAS/ITAP, including the permitted-stay expiry date.
3. Principal immigration category (for example, E28A, E23 or E33G; each has its own conditions).
4. Current country and residence address, where required for the application.
5. Principal holder or guarantor documents requested by the relevant eVisa procedure.

If the principal KITAS application is still in progress, prepare information about its stage and any document already issued to the principal applicant. Do not assume that a family application is possible before their ITAS is activated.

## Checklist: Spouse E31B

- Husband's or wife's passport.
- Legally valid marriage certificate.
- Name-change documents if the certificate and passport have different surnames.
- Proof of the connection to the principal holder's valid ITAS/ITAP.
- Additional financial, sponsor or registration records where required by the chosen procedure.

If the marriage took place in a third country, check who issued the certificate, its language and whether further registration with the competent authorities is required.

## Checklist: Child E31E

- Child's passport.
- Birth certificate naming the parent who holds ITAS/ITAP.
- Child's age and evidence of qualifying marital status.
- Parent's documents and current immigration status.
- If required: parents' marriage certificate, other parent's documents or consent, court order, name changes and additional family evidence.

Special cases include adoption, guardianship, children from previous relationships, parents with different surnames and transliteration mismatches. Do not automatically classify these as E31E.

## Checklist: Parent E31H

- Mother's or father's passport.
- Son or daughter's birth certificate naming the applying parent.
- Name-change documents if the details differ.
- Son or daughter's passport and ITAS/ITAP.
- Other documents requested in the current eVisa process, including proof of the family basis and guarantor where necessary.

## Translation, Apostille and Legalization

Requirements depend on **the country of issue**, certificate type, document language, recognition of the document and current filing route. An apostille and consular legalization are not the same thing; they must not both be demanded for every certificate indiscriminately.

Some official instructions require a sworn translation into Indonesian when a civil document is not in English. But older published instructions should not be applied to every modern application without checking: the manager verifies the current route and acceptable evidence format.

**Correct sequence:** review the original document → determine required certification and translation → agree on the spelling of names → prepare the files → submit the application.

## Why Different Surnames Can Cause Problems

For example, a child has the mother's surname, a marriage certificate records a parent's former passport, and their ITAS shows a different transliteration. This does not necessarily make the application impossible, but it may call for an intermediate document such as proof of a name change, an additional translation or another record.

Before filing, cross-check names in the passports, certificates, visas and ITAS documents of the entire family.

## How Long Must the Passport Remain Valid?

Passport validity requirements may depend on the requested family ITAS duration and current rules. Do not rely on a universal “6 months” or “18 months” for every family category. Check the expiry date of each passport **before payment**, particularly for a requested two-year family status.

## Is a Bank Statement Needed?

Some official and commercial procedures call for proof of funds or a guarantor's bank statement. We do not set USD 2,000 as an immutable mandatory threshold for every E31 category without checking the exact visa index and current eVisa procedure.

Do not confuse this with SAFRWAY's separate E33G rule: **12 months of bank history are additionally requested when preparing the principal E33G application**, but this does not automatically become a blanket requirement for all E31 family members.

## If the Family Is Already in Indonesia

Tell the manager every applicant's current visa type and expiration date. Not every form of temporary stay can be converted to family ITAS without leaving the country. The correct route depends on the rules for the current status and the intended category.

## Checks Before Sending Documents to SAFRWAY

- Every applicant has a valid passport with a visible expiry date.
- Certificates prove the required legal relationship.
- Names are consistent or discrepancies have been identified.
- The principal ITAS/ITAP holder is clearly identified.
- Current countries of residence and visa expiry dates are provided.
- For nonstandard families, documents explaining parentage and guardianship have been collected.
- Translation and legalization have been ordered only after identifying the applicable procedure.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a Marriage Certificate Alone Enough?

No. E31B also requires verification of the applicant's identity and the principal holder's eligible status; additional documents may be needed.

### Must a Birth Certificate Be Translated?

This depends on the language, country of issue and current instructions for the chosen category. Show the original document to the manager first.

### Is It a Problem If a Child and Their Father Have Different Surnames?

Not necessarily. The relationship must be proved by documents and any differences must be explainable.

### Can We Pay First and Gather the Certificates Later?

We recommend checking eligibility and possible mandatory documents first to avoid unnecessary expense or an unsuitable application.

### Can the Principal Holder's Bank Statement Be Used?

In some cases, the guarantor's financial evidence may be accepted, but requirements depend on the chosen category and the eVisa system.
