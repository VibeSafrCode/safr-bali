# D1 / D2 extensions in Indonesia — staying longer than 60 days

If you entered Indonesia on D1 or D2 and want to stay beyond the initial 60 days, the current period of stay can be extended.

**Extension through SAFRWAY — 2 500 000 IDR (≈ {{USD_2_5M}}).**

The key is not to confuse:

**the validity of your multiple-entry visa**  
and  
**the duration of a particular stay after entry**.

They are different things.

## What exactly is extended?

The D1/D2 visa itself is not extended for another 1 or 2 years.

What is extended is **the current period of stay** that began after your most recent entry into Indonesia.

For example:

- you have a 1-year D1;
- you enter Indonesia;
- receive a stay of up to 60 days;
- decide to stay longer;
- extend that particular 60-day period.

The validity of the multiple-entry visa itself remains a separate matter.

## How long can the stay be extended?

Under current rules, the initial D1/D2 stay is up to **60 days**.

It can then be extended, with the total length of that visit reaching **up to 180 days**.

This does not mean that 180 days are granted automatically at the outset.

The initial period comes first; an extension is then arranged under the current Immigration procedure.

## When should I request an extension?

We do not recommend waiting until the last day.

The published procedure includes an in-person visit to the immigration office for a photograph and interview. Submitting documents online does not replace that visit.

Depending on the case, you may also need:

- an additional check;
- another document;
- clarification of information;
- another step required by the procedure.

It is therefore best to send the documents to the manager early, so SAFRWAY can check the current procedure before your stay permit expires.

After reviewing your documents, the manager will agree an appropriate starting date and sequence of actions.

## What documents are required?

For an initial review, the basics are:

- passport;
- valid D1 or D2;
- date of your most recent entry;
- current permitted-stay expiry date;
- entry evidence / current stay permit;
- residential address in Indonesia;
- additional documents required by the current procedure.

SAFRWAY checks the exact list when you contact us.

## Do I need to extend D1/D2 if I am leaving?

Not always.

If you leave before your current permitted stay expires and later re-enter using a valid multiple-entry visa, the new entry starts a new period of stay.

Sometimes it is therefore more sensible to leave and return later rather than extend.

However, the decision depends on:

- your dates;
- the validity of the visa itself;
- your travel plans;
- the entry rules in force.

The manager can help compare both options.

## What happens after departure?

Departure ends the current period of stay.

If the D1/D2 visa itself remains valid, you can use it for your next entry.

A new period of stay starts after the new entry.

This is why D1/D2 is convenient for people who travel regularly and return to Indonesia.

## A stay extension does not renew the visa for another year

This distinction matters.

If you hold a 1-year D1 and the visa itself is approaching expiry, an ordinary extension of your current stay does not automatically give you another year of D1 validity.

These are two different services:

### Extending your current stay

This is needed if you are already in Indonesia and want to remain beyond the current 60-day period.

### A new D1/D2 visa

This is needed when the overall validity of the multiple-entry visa is ending and you want to continue travelling under this type of visa.

## Can D1/D2 be extended after an overstay?

An overstay is no longer an ordinary extension case.

If your permitted stay has already expired, your immigration status and the possible consequences of overstaying must be checked first.

In this situation, do not submit an ordinary extension application on your own. Tell your SAFRWAY manager the actual expiry date immediately.

## Can you work after extending your stay?

No.

An extension increases the length of your permitted stay but **does not change the purpose of the visa**.

### D1

It remains a tourism/visitor category.

### D2

It remains a business visitor category.

An extension does not turn D1/D2 into a work KITAS or create a right to receive a local salary.

## Can you move from D1/D2 to KITAS?

Do not treat an extension as a step towards automatic conversion.

Current official information allows D1/D2 visit stay permits to be extended, but a direct D1/D2 → ITAS conversion must not be promised.

If KITAS is your long-term goal, discuss it separately with your manager.

## How does a D1/D2 extension differ from a C1 extension?

C1 and D1/D2 work differently.

### C1

- a single-entry visa;
- the current visa ends when you leave;
- an extension lets you continue that particular trip.

### D1/D2

- a multiple-entry visa;
- the visa itself may remain valid after departure;
- the next entry starts a new period of stay.

Before extending D1/D2, it therefore helps to establish whether you actually need to remain in Indonesia without leaving.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can D1 be extended?

Yes.

### Can D2 be extended?

Yes.

### How much does an extension cost?

**2 500 000 IDR (≈ {{USD_2_5M}})** for both D1 and D2.

### How many days are granted initially?

Up to 60 days per entry.

### What is the maximum length of the current visit with extensions?

Under the current rules, up to 180 days in total.

### Does an extension add 1–2 years to the visa's validity?

No. It extends the current stay permit.

### If I leave and re-enter, do I receive a new stay period?

Yes, provided the D1/D2 visa itself remains valid and you meet the entry conditions.

### Do I need an extension if I am flying out soon?

Possibly not. Compare the expiry date of your current stay with your departure date.

### Can I work after the extension?

No. The visa category and permitted activities do not change.

### Can I extend after an overstay?

That is no longer a standard extension. Your status needs a separate assessment.
