# Extend eVOA or VOA in Bali for another 30 days

Initial stay: up to 30 days. Extension: once for up to another 30 days. Maximum on one entry: up to 60 days. The technical route for a standard VOA can differ; issuance method and current system must be checked.

## Key facts

- Initial stay: up to 30 days.
- Extension: once for up to another 30 days.
- Maximum on one entry: up to 60 days.
- eVOA extension is available online.
- The technical route for a standard VOA can differ; issuance method and current system must be checked.
- A valid departure ticket is required: return or onward to another country.
- SAFRWAY price: IDR 850,000 (≈ {{USD}}).

If you entered Indonesia on eVOA or a standard Visa on Arrival and 30 days is not enough, the stay can be extended once.

After the extension, the total stay on the same entry can reach up to 60 days.

## How long is the extension?

- up to 30 days — initial stay;
- up to another 30 days — after the one available extension;
- maximum up to 60 days on one entry.

There is no second standard extension after that.

For a longer stay, compare [C1](/bali/visas/c1/), [D12](/bali/visas/d12/) and [E33G](/bali/visas/e33g/) in advance.

## Are eVOA and standard VOA extended the same way?

The result is the same, but the technical route can differ.

### If you have eVOA

The extension is available online through Indonesia's electronic immigration system.

### If you have a standard VOA issued on arrival

The process is not identical to eVOA and should be checked based on the current visa and system route.

SAFRWAY verifies the visa before starting.

Both can add up to 30 days once. The online eVOA option is one reason to arrange it before travel. For an airport VOA, checking the current system and visa status before filing avoids starting the wrong process.

## When should you start?

Do not leave the extension until the final day. Even when an online route is available, you may still need:
- data checks;
- payment;
- a further action by Immigration;
- an error correction;
- confirmation of your current address or status.

Message the manager in advance.

## What do you need?

Prepare:

- passport;
- current eVOA/VOA;
- entry date;
- current stay-expiry date;
- ticket out of Indonesia;
- address in Indonesia;
- any previous visa-action confirmation.

After the review, the manager confirms what your particular case requires.

## Is a departure ticket required?

Yes.

A valid ticket out of Indonesia is required for the extension.

Plan the next departure date and have a valid ticket ready before extending.

## If the stay period is already ending

Do not wait until the last day.

If the stay has already expired, it is no longer a routine extension and the current immigration status must be reviewed separately.

The nearer the expiry date, the less time remains to correct errors or satisfy additional requirements.

## After 60 days

The same standard eVOA / VOA cycle cannot continue beyond 60 days. You need to:
- leave Indonesia;
- or plan another visa route in advance for your next entry.

For several months in Bali, a short VOA may not be the best initial choice. Compare:
- [C1](/bali/visas/c1/);
- [D12](/bali/visas/d12/);
- [E33G](/bali/visas/e33g/).
