# Permits for a Hotel, Guesthouse and Commercial Villa in Bali | SAFRWAY

**Direct answer:** This is a separate SAFRWAY service that we arrange with specialized Indonesian partners. The specific process depends on the activity, documents and requirements of the relevant government systems.

## About the service

Planning to open a hotel, guesthouse or small tourist accommodation business, or rent out a villa by the day? The first step is to establish **the legal business model and permitted use of the property**. Having a building, a lease agreement or a listing on a booking platform does not, in itself, establish that the accommodation business operates legally.

SAFRWAY helps assess a project in Bali and coordinate the appropriate business permits. A legal review of the business activity is **separate from the service of finding a villa for personal residence**.

## A hotel, guesthouse and villa are not always the same thing

Short-term accommodation falls within **KBLI sector 55**, but that sector contains different categories, including hotels and other forms of temporary lodging. Long-term property rental or acting as an intermediary for bookings may have a different classification and be subject to different rules.

There is therefore no single KBLI code that suits every villa. Before filing, we establish who the operator is, who receives payments, how many properties are involved, how long guests stay, what services are provided and on what basis the property is used.

## What we check before launch

- **KBLI 2025 and OSS/NIB:** whether the registered business activity matches the accommodation service actually provided.
- **Land and location:** zoning and whether commercial use of the property is permitted.
- **Rights to the property:** ownership documents, lease terms, the operator's rights and whether the proposed model of use is allowed.
- **Building:** existing PBG/SLF documents and whether further steps are needed for construction or a change in the building's function.
- **Tourism and other sector-specific requirements:** standard certificates, self-assessment, sanitary/environmental and other documents for the particular property type.
- **Company and investment:** whether foreign participation is permitted and special investment-value rules for the accommodation sector.

For a tourism business, it is especially important to distinguish **the right to own or lease property** from **the right to carry out a particular activity on that property**.

## Can a villa that is already operating be brought into compliance?

SAFRWAY can start with an assessment of the existing property: land and building documents, the registered company, OSS records, the actual rental arrangements and permits already obtained. But we cannot promise in advance that every property can be brought into the required status without changes or additional work. The outcome depends, among other things, on its location and designated use.

## What is needed for an initial assessment

The address or map location, a description of the project, photographs and a floor plan, the expected length of guests' stays, the owner's and operator's documents, the lease agreement (if any), existing NIB/KBLI, PBG/SLF and other licences. If documents are missing, tell us what is currently known: we can begin by identifying the gaps.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can any villa be rented out to tourists if there is a lease agreement?
There is no universal guarantee. A lease agreement alone does not replace the requirements applicable to the business activity and the property.

### Does a foreign operator need a PT PMA?
It depends on the business structure, whether foreign participation is permitted and the type of activity; this is checked before choosing a company form.

### Is an NIB enough for a hotel?
Not always. Requirements depend on the sector-specific category and the status of the property's documents.

### Is there a special licence for every villa?
The specific set of permits depends on the accommodation model, the property and the operator. We determine the KBLI code and type of activity first, then the documents.

### Do you check PBG and SLF?
Yes. As part of the preliminary assessment, we establish whether these documents exist and whether they correspond to the property. Any need for further action is determined for the specific property.

### If a villa is already listed on a booking platform, does that mean everything is legal?
Not necessarily. Publishing a listing does not prove compliance with OSS requirements, building rules or local regulations.

### Can you check a location before purchase or rental?
Yes. This is one of the most useful stages: establishing restrictions before committing funds.
