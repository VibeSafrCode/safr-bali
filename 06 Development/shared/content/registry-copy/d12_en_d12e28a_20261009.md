# D12 — multiple-entry visa for exploring investment opportunities in Indonesia

If you are considering establishing a business or investing in Indonesia, it is important to assess the project on the ground first: visit locations, evaluate conditions, and determine whether the direction you have chosen is right for you.

D12 is intended for these preliminary investment visits. It allows repeated travel to Indonesia with an initial permitted stay of up to 180 days after each entry.

**Message a SAFRWAY manager about your plans, and we will check whether D12 suits your trip.**

## Who D12 is for

Consider this category if you need time to assess a project before starting full business activities. You might want to inspect a potential site and evaluate the feasibility of launching a business, compare several investments, or return to the project after an initial fact-finding trip.

The key question is not just how many months you want to stay in Bali, but your actual purpose of travel. D12 is a pre-investment visa, not a general replacement for a tourist or employment status.

## What D12 offers

**Up to 180 days after entry.** The initial permitted stay begins when you actually enter Indonesia, not when you first discuss the application with an agency.

**Repeat travel.** While the multiple-entry visa remains valid, you may leave and use it to seek re-entry if you meet the entry conditions. Immigration has final discretion over admission.

**The possibility of extending your current stay.** A D12 stay may be extended under the applicable procedure. The extension is processed separately; it is not automatically included with the initial visa.

## Visa validity and stay duration are different

The validity of D12 determines how long it can be used for travel. The permitted-stay period tells you until what date you may remain in Indonesia after a particular entry.

Even if the multiple-entry visa has a longer validity, you must follow the permitted-stay expiration date while in the country. The visa's long validity alone does not allow you to remain without an extension.

Before filing, SAFRWAY confirms the available visa variant and explains how it fits your travel plans.

## How a D12 extension works

Published Immigration guidance specifically addresses D12: the initial stay is up to 180 days, an extension may also be granted for up to 180 days, and the total continuous stay for the current visit is limited to 12 months.

Therefore, the 60-day D1/D2 extension scheme must not be applied to D12.

We check your current visa, the expiration of your permitted stay, past extensions, and current requirements. Following approval, the new date on the issued stay permit governs your stay.

**SAFRWAY D12 extension service: 7,000,000 IDR per extension stage.** Availability and procedure are checked before filing.

## D12, D2, or Investor KITAS?

**D12** is for preliminary investigation of an investment opportunity and preparation to establish a business.

**D2** is generally for regular business visits, such as meetings and negotiations. See [D2 Multiple Entry](/bali/visas/d2/).

**Investor KITAS** is a separate status for living in Indonesia on an investment basis. It is not automatically included with D12. See [Investor KITAS](/bali/visas/investor-kitas/).

SAFRWAY chooses a category based on what you will actually do, not only your preferred number of months.

## Can you work on D12?

D12 is not a work KITAS. Researching a future project does not grant permission to start any type of work in Indonesia or use the visa for local employment.

If you intend to perform a specific function in a company, tell the manager. We will separately assess the status required for that work.

## Can you switch from D12 to KITAS without leaving Indonesia?

Rules and available procedures may change. We therefore do not promise that every D12 holder can convert to KITAS in Indonesia.

**We will review your current immigration status and determine whether you can move to the required category inside Indonesia or need to leave and file a new application.**

A later change of status is assessed separately from the D12 application. Its fee is not the price of a new D12 visa or a regular D12 extension.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is D12 the same as Investor KITAS?

No. D12 is a multiple-entry visa for pre-investment visits. Investor KITAS is issued on a different basis.

### How many days do you get after entry?

Initially up to 180 days. Check the actual expiration date in your stay permit.

### Can I leave and return?

Yes, a multiple-entry visa may be used for repeat entries while valid, provided you meet entry conditions.

### Can I stay longer than the initial 180 days?

Extensions are provided for under the rules. Your current eligibility and the next stage are checked before applying.

### Can I obtain D12 just for a long vacation?

The category must match your real travel purpose. If leisure is your primary goal, the manager will compare tourist options instead of disguising it as D12 pre-investment travel.

### How much does the application cost?

One-year D12: standard **7,500,000 IDR**, expedited **9,500,000 IDR**. Two-year D12: standard **10,500,000 IDR**, expedited **13,000,000 IDR**. Extension: **7,000,000 IDR per stage**; a later change of status is assessed separately.
