# Extending Investor KITAS E28A: timing, company changes, and shareholding

## Quick answer

Investor KITAS **E28A** can be extended if the current requirements are met and a qualifying investment basis remains in place. Before renewal, check the **ITAS expiration date, current passport, sponsoring company's status, the investor's actual shareholding, and changes to corporate records**. Published classifications provide an initial two-year E28A; an extension may be of comparable length, while the cumulative series of ITAS under this basis is limited by current rules to **up to six years**. The appropriate route after that limit is reviewed separately.

## What to check first

Do not rely solely on the date printed on a KITAS card or in one PDF. Compare:

1. your current electronic ITAS and its expiration date;
2. passport information;
3. the details of the sponsoring company;
4. current shareholder records and the applicant's **individual shareholding**;
5. actual work and position;
6. past extensions and total time already spent under this basis.

If records differ, identify the corrections required before selecting a procedure.

## When can you apply for an extension?

Published general Immigration guidance states that for ITAS valid for **more than one year**, an extension application may be submitted **no earlier than three months before expiry** and no later than the last valid day. This is a **regulatory filing window, not SAFRWAY's processing-time guarantee**.

We recommend an early eligibility check. Corporate records may require updates, and Immigration may require photography, biometrics, or further review. Do not wait until the last day.

## What if the ITAS expires while the application is being processed?

Published guidance states that if the application is correctly submitted and the applicable immigration payment is made **before ITAS expiry**, processing that finishes after the expiry date is not treated as an overstay under this procedure. However, do not rely on that rule without evidence of valid filing and payment: an incorrect or incomplete application can create risk. SAFRWAY checks the actual submission confirmation.

## The company changed its name or address

Check that the company's current registration details match the sponsor information and previously issued ITAS. Changes of address, legal name, ownership structure, or other registration records may require additional filings or notifications before or alongside renewal.

**We do not promise one universal process for every change.** The manager determines the appropriate procedure from current company records and Immigration requirements.

## The investor's shareholding changed

For E28A, the applicant's **individual shareholding** must continue to meet eligibility requirements. If shares were sold or reallocated, an old KITAS card does not establish that the previous basis still applies. Do not conceal ownership changes during renewal.

Published standard E28A requirements include a minimum personal stake of **10 billion IDR**. If the basis has changed, a different lawful status or procedure may be necessary. [E28A requirements](/bali/knowledge/investor-kitas/e28a-requirements/).

## Position or actual duties changed

Investor status does not permit arbitrary work activities. If the investor becomes an operational director, manager, or employee of another business, check whether those duties are allowed under E28A or require an employment category.

A new title in a company resolution alone is not a substitute for assessing the immigration implications of the actual role.

## The company has ceased operating

If the sponsor company is liquidated, loses active status, or the investor no longer meets the qualifying basis, an ordinary renewal may not be appropriate. Check the implications for ITAS promptly and identify how to lawfully amend or end the status.

## Documents you may need

An initial review generally requires your passport, current ITAS, sponsor details, up-to-date company documents, and proof of share ownership. Additional requirements may include evidence of ongoing compliance and current financial or corporate information.

General Immigration ITAS-renewal guidance requires proof that the original basis continues; in certain cases this involves updated statements, company deeds, ownership evidence, and other records. Requirements vary by application.

## Can I simply obtain a new E28A after expiry?

A fresh visa and an extension are not the same procedure. First review your total ITAS history, current company, reason for staying, and available options. An initial two-year ITAS does not create an automatic right to unlimited two-year renewals.

## How much does an Investor KITAS extension cost?

**SAFRWAY has not yet approved a fixed E28A extension price.** A new two-year E28A application costs **16,000,000 IDR**, but that amount must not be shown automatically as the extension price. The manager checks the route and provides a separate quote before filing.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is E28A always extended for another two years?

Renewal is permitted, but the outcome depends on current conditions, continued eligibility, and Immigration's decision.

### Can I renew E28A after selling some shares?

Your remaining shareholding must be assessed against current requirements and updated company records. Old documents may no longer suffice.

### Must I report a replacement passport?

Yes. Before applying, check whether your passport information must be updated in immigration records.

### Can I apply three months before expiry?

For ITAS longer than one year, published rules permit filing no earlier than three months before expiration. Applicability is confirmed against your actual status at the time of preparation.

### Can I renew indefinitely?

No. Published rules limit the total duration of consecutive ITAS under E28A.
