# Setting Up a PT PMA in Indonesia

## Quick answer

**A PT PMA is an Indonesian company with foreign investment.** Before registration, foreign-ownership eligibility, structure, capital, notarial and AHU documents, and subsequent OSS licences must be assessed. An Investor KITAS is arranged separately.

A **PT PMA** is one way to conduct business in Indonesia with foreign investment. Depending on the applicable rules, it may be suitable for services, trade, projects with local partners and other activities—provided foreign participation is permitted and specific requirements are satisfied.

## Four questions to answer before registration

### 1. What business will you actually operate?

Simply saying "I want to open a company in Bali" does not define a permitted activity. We check your business model, **KBLI 2025** codes, permitted foreign-ownership share, region and restrictions. Restaurants, hotels, online services and trading businesses may be subject to different rules.

### 2. Who will be the shareholders and management?

We establish whether the shareholders are individuals or entities, their respective holdings and the powers of the director and commissioner. We do not promote sham nominee ownership as a blanket way around legal restrictions.

### 3. How much capital and planned investment is required?

Under the general rules of **BKPM Regulation No. 5/2025**, a PT PMA must have at least **IDR 2.5 billion in issued/paid-up capital**, and there is a separate threshold of **more than IDR 10 billion in planned investment** for the relevant activity and location, subject to sectoral exceptions. Neither amount is the incorporation fee, and the investment threshold does not automatically mean keeping IDR 10 billion in cash in a personal bank account. [Understand PT PMA capital requirements](/en/bali/knowledge/business/pt-pma-capital-2026/).

### 4. Which licences are required after incorporation?

After notarial incorporation and registration with **AHU**, the company may need an NIB through OSS, risk classification, sector-specific permits and tax registration steps. **A registered company is not a substitute for sectoral licences.** [NIB and OSS](/en/bali/business/oss-licenses/).

2. **Company structure.** Agree shareholders, capital, management bodies and address.

4. **Core registrations.** Coordinate the agreed tax and OSS procedures.
5. **Readiness to operate.** Identify any outstanding licences and operational prerequisites.

## Can you obtain an Investor KITAS after forming a PT PMA?

In some cases, incorporating a company is one step toward an [E28A Investor KITAS](/en/bali/visas/investor-kitas/). However, the rules for **ordinary PT PMA incorporation** and for an **individual investor's immigration status** are not identical. A company's IDR 2.5 billion paid-up capital does not itself establish that a foreign shareholder meets E28A requirements.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a PT PMA a type of visa?

No. It is an Indonesian company with foreign investment. Visas and KITAS are obtained under separate immigration grounds.

### Does a PT PMA require IDR 10 billion in paid-up capital?

Do not confuse different thresholds. For an ordinary PT PMA the general paid-up capital minimum is IDR 2.5 billion. Planned investment is regulated separately, normally at more than IDR 10 billion, with exceptions. The applicable KBLI must be reviewed.

### Can a PT PMA be formed for any activity?

No. Restrictions on foreign investment and sector-specific requirements apply.

### Does company registration mean I can start selling services immediately?

Not necessarily. The permits needed depend on the activity and its risk classification.
