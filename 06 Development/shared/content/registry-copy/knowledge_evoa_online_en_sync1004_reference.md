# How to apply for an Indonesian eVOA online before arrival

Type: Electronic Visa on Arrival. Apply online before the trip. Use for entry within 90 days of issuance. Initial stay: up to 30 days. Extension: once for up to another 30 days.

## Key facts

- Apply online before the trip.
- Initial stay: up to 30 days.
- Extension: once for up to another 30 days.
- Maximum on one entry: up to 60 days.
- Number of entries: one.
- Passport: at least 6 months of validity on entry.
- A valid departure ticket is required: return or onward to another country.
- Use for entry within 90 days of issuance.
- Autogate is subject to current passport, visa and entry-point requirements.

If you are flying to Bali for a short stay, the eVOA lets you solve the visa question before departure.

Instead of buying Visa on Arrival after landing, you receive the electronic visa in advance and arrive with the document ready.

For travelers, the main benefit is convenience: fewer steps after a long flight and a faster route from the aircraft to baggage and the airport exit.

## What is eVOA?

eVOA means Electronic Visa on Arrival.

The stay period is the same as a standard VOA:

- up to 30 days after entry;
- one extension for up to another 30 days;
- maximum up to 60 days on one entry.

The difference is that the eVOA is arranged online in advance.

Read more: [eVOA or VOA on arrival: which should you choose for Bali?](/bali/knowledge/evoa/evoa-vs-voa/).

## What do you need?

Prepare:

- passport;
- passport biodata page;
- recent color photo;
- contact details;
- return ticket or onward ticket to a third country;
- other information requested by the current eVisa form.

Your passport must be valid for at least 6 months on entry.

Make sure every detail on the eVOA exactly matches your passport.

An error in your name, passport number or other key details can make the visa unusable.

## Is a return ticket required?

Yes.

For eVOA/VOA you need either:

- a return ticket out of Indonesia;

or

- an onward ticket to another country.

It is better to have this solved before departure rather than dealing with it during airline check-in or on arrival.

Read more: [Return and onward tickets](/bali/knowledge/return-ticket-indonesia/).

## How does self-application work?

A typical process:

1. create or use your account in Indonesia's electronic visa system;
2. select the correct Visa on Arrival/eVOA;
3. enter data exactly as shown in your passport;
4. upload the required documents;
5. provide the return/onward ticket;
6. review everything carefully;
7. pay the application;
8. monitor your account and email;
9. save the issued eVOA on your phone and preferably keep a backup copy.

The visa itself does not guarantee entry; final admission remains with Indonesian Immigration.

Use an available payment method and monitor the account and email until you receive the result.

## When should you apply?

Do not leave it until boarding.

A standard B1 eVOA is valid for entry for up to 90 days after issuance.

The 30-day stay itself starts from the actual date of entry, not the issue date.

You can therefore prepare the visa in advance rather than leaving it to the last moment, and travel with the document already ready.

## What happens after landing with eVOA?

If your eVOA is ready, you do not need to:

- buy a standard VOA after landing;
- complete the separate airport visa payment step;
- obtain the VOA payment voucher.

If your passport, eVOA and airport meet current autogate requirements, you may be able to use the automated immigration gates.

This is particularly useful during busy arrival periods, when several flights may reach the manual visa and immigration areas at the same time.

## What else should you prepare before arrival?

Check:

- return/onward ticket;
- All Indonesia;
- passport validity;
- accommodation address;
- airport transfer;
- money for initial expenses.

The eVOA alone does not cover every part of your entry preparation.

## All Indonesia

[All Indonesia](/bali/guides/all-indonesia/) is the required arrival declaration for international passengers.

It combines information for:

- immigration;
- customs;
- health;
- quarantine.

After submission, it produces a QR code.

The official declaration is free.

## How long can you stay after entry?

- up to 30 days initially;
- up to another 30 days after one extension;
- maximum up to 60 days.

If you decide to remain beyond the first 30 days, see [SAFRWAY eVOA / VOA extension](/bali/visas/voa/extension/).

## What if you want more than 60 days?

eVOA / VOA is intended for short trips. For several months in Indonesia, compare:

- [C1](/bali/visas/c1/);
- [D12](/bali/visas/d12/);
- [E33G](/bali/visas/e33g/).

Choose around your actual travel duration so a short-stay visa does not become the wrong starting point.
