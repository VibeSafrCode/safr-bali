# KITAS for the Parents of a KITAS Holder — Family Category E31H

## Quick Answer

**E31H** is a family basis for a parent joining a son or daughter who holds a valid ITAS/ITAP. Before filing, the family relationship must be documented and the child's particular status checked for compatibility with this category in the current eVisa system. Each parent needs a separate application.

## When E31H Is Considered

A person with a long-term basis for residence in Indonesia wants their mother or father to live lawfully nearby for longer than an ordinary tourist visit. E31H exists for this purpose, but eligibility must not be inferred solely from the son or daughter holding KITAS.

Legal limitations are particularly important if the principal holder has a special E33G or Golden Visa category: the general E31 table does not guarantee technical compatibility with every such status.

## What Must Be Verified

- the family relationship between the applying parent and the principal ITAS/ITAP holder;
- the validity and sufficient remaining duration of the son or daughter's residence permit;
- the parent's passport and current immigration status;
- whether sponsor/guarantor documents and financial evidence are required;
- the available residence duration and application route for the parent;
- additional documents where names or family records differ.

## Documents to Prepare

The initial review generally requires the parent's passport and civil documents, the son or daughter's birth certificate identifying the parent, the principal holder's passport and ITAS/ITAP, current address, and intended duration of the parent's family stay.

The adult child's birth certificate may have been issued decades ago. Check its condition, any name or surname changes, whether a reissued certificate is necessary, and whether apostille/legalization or translation is required.

## Is There an Age Limit for Parents?

Under the general E31H classification considered here, the basis is a parent joining an ITAS/ITAP holder. We do not impose a universal age threshold without reviewing the current conditions of the particular application. E31H should not be confused with separate retirement visa categories.

## Can a Parent Work on E31H?

E31H is for family residence. Employment or other paid activities require a separate lawful basis; a family KITAS must not be treated as work authorization.

## Does E31H Apply to Parents of E33G Holders?

E31H is included in the general classification, but applicability to an individual E33G/Golden Visa case may depend on the principal holder's conditions and the current technical eVisa process. SAFRWAY checks this **before confirming that an application can be filed**, not after payment for a promised outcome.

## Duration and Place of Application

The final family ITAS duration and the possibility of filing without leaving Indonesia depend on both the parent's and the principal holder's specific status. SAFRWAY checks before filing whether departure, a new visa or additional proof of eligibility is needed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I Bring Both Parents to Indonesia?

The grounds are checked separately and an individual application is filed for each parent.

### If I Have Investor KITAS, Is E31H Guaranteed?

No. Before promising that category, the principal holder's specific status and its validity period, as well as the current application procedure, must be checked.

### Is E31H Suitable for a Retired Parent?

Age alone does not define E31H eligibility. The family-based route is different from retirement KITAS; SAFRWAY will assess which option better fits the case.

### Can My Parent Apply from Inside Indonesia?

This depends on current immigration status and the category-change rules. We do not guarantee an in-country application without assessment.

### What Does E31H Cost?

The price is confirmed individually after checking documents and eligibility.
