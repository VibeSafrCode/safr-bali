# Restaurant and Café Licences in Bali | SAFRWAY

**Direct answer:** This is a separate SAFRWAY service that we arrange with specialized Indonesian partners. The specific process depends on the activity, documents and requirements of the relevant government systems.

## About the service

Opening a restaurant, café, bar or kitchen in Bali starts not with a universal “restaurant licence”, but with checking **the operating format, address, legal entity and OSS requirements**. An NIB may be necessary, but it does not, by itself, prove that all conditions for starting operations have been met.

SAFRWAY helps clarify which permits the establishment needs and coordinate the procedures through specialized local partners. It is especially useful to carry out these checks **before signing a long-term lease and undertaking expensive renovations**.

## What kind of business are you planning to open?

From a regulatory perspective, a sit-down restaurant, small café, delivery kitchen, catering operation, bar and sale of packaged food may fall under different business activities. You need to identify the appropriate **KBLI 2025** code rather than mechanically copying a code from an old business plan.

We check the proposed menu and service format, whether there is an on-site kitchen, the number and roles of employees, alcohol sales, whether the business operates inside a hotel or separately, delivery and additional services.

## Which permits might be required?

- **OSS/NIB and the applicable standard/certificate or permit**, depending on the KBLI code and risk category.
- **Suitability of the premises** for their designated purpose and the conditions for using the building/land.
- **PBG and SLF**, where required for construction, reconstruction or operation of the premises under the applicable rules.
- **Sanitation and hygiene documents** (including SLHS, if applicable to the establishment's format).
- Additional permits, where required, for alcoholic products, special goods, particular production processes or the specific operating model.
- Environmental, fire-safety, tourism or employment procedures, where they actually apply.

**Not every café needs the same set of certificates.** Requirements depend on size, risk, the activities performed and current regulations. SAFRWAY's separate page on [OSS/NIB](/bali/business/oss-nib-licensing/) explains the general process.

## Checking the premises before renting

In Bali, it is especially important to compare the property's intended commercial use with zoning, the building's permitted use and the terms of the lease. An agreement with the owner does not replace government permits. We recommend checking in advance whether kitchen equipment, ventilation, wastewater drainage and other installations can be put in place if your project requires them.

## What to prepare for a preliminary assessment

A description of the business, the property's address or map location, company documents (if it already exists), the current NIB, a floor plan, the proposed menu/alcohol sales, photographs of the premises and information on how the business will operate. Where foreign participation is involved, PT PMA requirements, KBLI eligibility and investment requirements must also be checked, including sector-specific aspects of **BKPM Regulation No. 5/2025**.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is one NIB enough to open a café?
Not always. It depends on the KBLI code, risk level, sanitation requirements and the condition of the premises.

### Can I open a restaurant through a PT PMA?
Whether foreign participation is permitted and what investment requirements apply are checked for the relevant business category under current law.

### If an establishment operates inside a hotel, does it need a separate KBLI code?
It depends on whether the restaurant operates as a separate business and which legal entity runs it. This is checked separately.

### What if we are not going to sell alcohol?
Then permits for alcohol sales should not be included automatically, although other requirements for the establishment may still apply.

### Are PBG and SLF required?
This depends on the building, the nature of any construction work, the existing documents and the applicable rules for operating the property. We check this as part of the property assessment.

### Can you assess the property before we sign the lease?
Yes. A preliminary review of the location and documents is especially useful before major investment.

### How long does the process take?
A single timeframe cannot be stated without establishing the business activity and the list of required procedures.
