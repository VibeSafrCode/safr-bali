**H1:** What documents are needed for E33G Remote Worker KITAS?

## Direct answer

For E33G you need to prove that you genuinely work for a company outside Indonesia and earn at least USD 60,000 per year.

The key documents are the employment agreement, bank history, income evidence and employer information.

The official bank-history minimum is 3 months. SAFRWAY asks for 12 months when preparing a case.

## Base document set

- passport;
- bank statement;
- at least USD 2,000 equivalent in funds;
- proof of at least USD 60,000 annual income;
- employment agreement with a foreign company;
- CV;
- itinerary;
- recent color photograph.

## Why consistency matters

The package should tell one coherent story:

**employer → contract → role → income → bank inflows.**

If the documents conflict, it is better to identify the issue before filing.

## Employment agreement

We review:

- legal company name;
- country of registration;
- employee name;
- role;
- start date;
- income;
- currency;
- payment frequency;
- working arrangement;
- signatures.

The agreement should reflect the real relationship.

## Remote-work wording

Because E33G is for remote work, the documentation should make it understandable that the work can be performed remotely.

This can appear in the agreement, an addendum or another real employer document.

## USD 60,000 annual income

The amount should be supported by:

- the contract;
- real bank inflows;
- payment frequency;
- payer identity;
- currency;
- income stability.

## Gross vs net

A difference between gross salary in the contract and net salary received in the bank is not automatically a problem.

The reason should simply be understandable and documented.

## Bank history

### Official minimum
3 months, plus at least USD 2,000 equivalent in funds.

### SAFRWAY standard
12 months.

This helps review income stability, payment patterns, payer identity, salary changes and gaps.

## What should the bank statement show?

Ideally:

- account holder name;
- bank;
- covered period;
- transaction dates;
- amounts;
- currency;
- payer identity if available.

## Payroll intermediaries

If salary arrives through a payroll service, group company, payment intermediary or another currency, tell the manager.

Do not hide it. We need to connect the real payments to the employer and contract.

## Recent job change

A recent employer change is not an automatic disqualifier.

We review the new contract, start date, previous bank history, current inflows and other supporting documents.

## Employer verification

SAFRWAY may check:

- legal name;
- registration number;
- country;
- address;
- company status;
- contract signatory;
- consistency with public registry data.

## CV and itinerary

Both are part of the standard package.

The CV should be simple, consistent and aligned with the current role.

The itinerary does not mean you must buy non-refundable flights before approval.

## Common issues

Cases often need extra attention when:

- contract income and bank income differ;
- salary arrives from another entity;
- the employer is new;
- the agreement is recent;
- part of the income is bonus-based;
- payments arrive in several currencies;
- names are spelled differently;
- company details changed;
- CV job title differs from the contract;
- remote work is not documented;
- most annual income arrived as one large payment.

These are not automatic refusals, but they should be reviewed before filing.

## First package to send SAFRWAY

Start with:

1. passport;
2. employment agreement;
3. 12-month bank statement;
4. short employer information;
5. actual income information;
6. CV if ready.

## FAQ

### Is a 3-month statement enough?
That is the official minimum. SAFRWAY asks for 12 months for case preparation.

### What if bank income differs from the contract?
Tell the manager. We need to understand and document the difference.

### What if salary comes through payroll?
Show us the structure. We will check how to connect the payroll payments to the employer.

### What if I recently changed jobs?
The case should be reviewed individually.

### Is an employment agreement required?
Yes, with a company established outside Indonesia.

### Can I use a contract with my own foreign company?
Do not assume it automatically qualifies. The real employment/business structure should be reviewed first.

### What if my case is unusual?
Send the documents to SAFRWAY before filing.
