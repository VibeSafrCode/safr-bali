### EN — suggested direct answer / FAQ replacement

In the general family classification, E31B covers the spouse of an ITAS/ITAP holder, while E31E covers an eligible unmarried biological child under 18. However, eligibility for these categories with a particular E33G/Golden Visa principal must be verified under the current rules and live eVisa filing process before an application is promised. E31H for parents must also be checked on a case-by-case basis.

**Can relatives apply after the principal receives E33G?** Family applications may be possible when the principal's status and current requirements allow them. SAFRWAY checks the grounds and eVisa procedure before confirming the route.

**What does dependent Family KITAS cost?** SAFRWAY provides an individual quote for each applicant after checking the family category, duration and filing route. No fixed price has been approved for an E31 dependent application.

Die allgemeine Familienklassifikation sieht E31B für Ehepartner eines ITAS/ITAP-Inhabers und E31E für geeignete leibliche, unverheiratete Kinder unter 18 Jahren vor. **Was kostet ein Family KITAS für Angehörige?** SAFRWAY berechnet den Preis individuell pro Person nach Prüfung der Kategorie, Laufzeit und Antragsroute. Für E31-Angehörige besteht kein genehmigter Festpreis.
