# Working KITAS E23 in Indonesia

## Short answer

**Working KITAS is an immigration status for a foreign national who will work in Indonesia on a lawful basis.** The standard E23 route involves more than applying for a visa: the employer, permitted position, required Ministry of Manpower approvals and immigration documents must also be checked.

If you plan to work for an Indonesian company in Bali, start by checking the employer and the nature of the work. A Working KITAS cannot be selected correctly based only on your preferred length of stay.

## Who this service is for

This route may be relevant when a foreign professional has an offer from a company operating in Indonesia and will perform an approved role here. Directors, commissioners and certain professional or sector-specific roles may fall under other visa indexes or special rules. We do not automatically label every employment situation E23.

If you work remotely **for a company outside Indonesia**, consider [E33G Remote Worker KITAS](/en/bali/visas/e33g/) separately. If you own or invest in a company, see [Investor KITAS E28A](/en/bali/visas/investor-kitas/). These are different legal grounds.

## What we check before applying

- **Employer:** valid corporate documents, business activity, address and authorised representative.
- **Position:** whether the actual duties match an eligible category, with any professional or sector restrictions.
- **Manpower approvals:** whether [RPTKA](/en/bali/business/foreign-worker-permits/) applies, whether exceptions exist and what evidence has been obtained.
- **Company reporting:** whether [WLKP](/en/bali/business/wlkp/) records and related employer details are current.
- **Immigration route:** applying before entry or assessing a possible change of status within Indonesia separately.

Official working-ITAS lists include initial periods of **180 days, 1 year and 2 years**. The available duration depends on the category and approved basis; we confirm it for the specific application.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does a Working KITAS allow employment with any company?

No. The work must correspond to the valid immigration basis, approved role and employer conditions.

### Can I obtain a Working KITAS without an Indonesian employer?

For the standard employment route, the lawful employer and its authority must be checked. If your company is based outside Indonesia, E33G may be more relevant.

### Is RPTKA automatically included in a KITAS?

No. [RPTKA](/en/bali/business/foreign-worker-permits/) is a separate employer-side procedure; its applicability and inclusion in the package are checked in advance.

### How long can an E23 ITAS be issued for?

The official list includes 180 days, 1 year and 2 years. The permitted period and extension for a particular job depend on the category and documents.

### Can I apply while already in Bali?

Certain changes of status are sometimes possible, depending on the current visa and applicable conditions. See our [Working KITAS status-change assessment](/en/bali/visas/working-kitas/conversion/).

We quote after defining the scope: manpower approvals, visa filing and additional employer actions may be charged separately.
