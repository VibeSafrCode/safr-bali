# Documents for Indonesia and Other Countries: Translation, Apostille and Legalization

## Quick answer

**SAFRWAY helps determine which steps a document requires: translation, certification, an apostille, consular legalization or preparation of a corporate document package.** The procedure depends on the country of issuance, destination country, document type and the requirements of the receiving organization. We quote the price and estimated timeframe after assessing your case.

A document suitable for one procedure may not be suitable for another. A bank, for example, may ask for translated corporate records, while a foreign government authority may need confirmation of the origin of an official document. Before paying, it is better to establish the end purpose rather than order every procedure “just in case.”

## What we can help with

### Sworn and certified translations

For documents to be submitted in Indonesia or abroad, we check the required language pair and the translator’s status. If a **penerjemah tersumpah** (Indonesian sworn translator) is required, we arrange the work through a suitably qualified specialist. This is not the same as ordinary machine or editorial translation. [Learn about certified translations](/en/bali/documents/certified-translations/).

### Document apostilles

For eligible public documents intended for use in another country where the Apostille Convention applies in the relevant relationship, we check whether an [apostille](/en/bali/documents/apostille/) is available. An apostille authenticates the origin of an official signature or seal, not the document’s content or automatic acceptance by the receiving authority.

### Consular legalization

Where a specific document or route is outside the apostille process, a sequence of authentications and an embassy or consulate visit may be necessary. [Check the legalization route](/en/bali/documents/legalization/).

### Corporate documents

For PT PMA and other lawfully operating companies, we establish which constitutional, registration, notarial, translated and supporting records are needed for the particular purpose. A [corporate document package](/en/bali/documents/corporate/) is distinct from incorporating a company or obtaining OSS/NIB business licenses.

## Why we establish the purpose first

- **Country of issuance:** Indonesia or another country.
- **Place of submission:** the destination country and, if known, the receiving organization.
- **Document type:** personal, educational, notarial, judicial, corporate, tax or other.
- **Available format:** original, official extract, notarized copy or scan.
- **Recipient’s requirements:** translation into a particular language, signature authentication, apostille or consular procedure.

Not every corporate or commercial document can be apostilled. A document issued outside Indonesia does not automatically fall under the Indonesian process: the competent authority in the country of origin must be checked. [Apostille vs. legalization](/en/bali/knowledge/documents/apostille-vs-legalization/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Are translation and apostille the same service?

No. Translation conveys content into another language; an apostille is a separate way of authenticating the origin of certain official documents.

### Can Indonesia issue an apostille for a document issued abroad?

As a rule, the competent apostille authority is in the country where the document originated. We first check the foreign document’s country of issuance and applicable procedure.

### Is a translation always needed after an apostille?

There is no universal rule. The receiving authority specifies the language and format; depending on the process, translation may be needed before or after authentication.

### Is a digital scan sufficient?

A legible scan is often enough for an initial assessment. Certain procedures may still require an original, official copy or additional evidence.

### Can you help with PT PMA company documents?

Yes. We can coordinate assessment and preparation of an agreed corporate document package. [PT PMA incorporation](/en/bali/business/company-registration/), licensing and changes to a company are separate services.
