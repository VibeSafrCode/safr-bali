# Extend eVOA or VOA in Bali for another 30 days

If you entered Indonesia on eVOA or a standard Visa on Arrival and 30 days is not enough, the stay can be extended once.

After the extension, the total stay on the same entry can reach up to 60 days.

**SAFRWAY price — IDR 850,000 (≈ {{USD}}).**

Message a SAFRWAY manager. We will check the exact visa type, current stay period and which extension route applies to your case.

## How long is the extension?

- up to 30 days — initial stay;
- up to another 30 days — after the one available extension;
- maximum up to 60 days on one entry.

There is no second standard extension after that.

## Are eVOA and standard VOA extended the same way?

The result is the same, but the technical route can differ.

### If you have eVOA

The extension is available online through Indonesia's electronic immigration system.

### If you have a standard VOA issued on arrival

The process is not identical to eVOA and should be checked based on the current visa and system route.

SAFRWAY verifies the visa before starting.

## When should you start?

Do not wait until the final day.

Even for an online extension, there can be data checks, payment, immigration actions, corrections or additional information requirements.

## What do you need?

Prepare:

- passport;
- current eVOA/VOA;
- entry date;
- current stay-expiry date;
- ticket out of Indonesia;
- address in Indonesia;
- any previous visa-action confirmation.

## Is a departure ticket required?

Yes.

A valid ticket out of Indonesia is required for the extension.

## If you arranged the eVOA yourself

That is not a problem.

SAFRWAY can still help with the extension.

## If you received VOA at the airport

Send passport, VOA details, entry date and stay information. We first check the correct route for your case.

## If the stay period is already ending

Do not wait until the last day.

If the stay has already expired, it is no longer a routine extension and the current immigration status must be reviewed separately.

## After 60 days

You cannot stay longer on the same standard eVOA/VOA cycle.

You need to leave Indonesia or choose another visa scenario for the next entry.

If you plan to live in Bali for several months, compare C1, D12 and E33G.
