# Working KITAS E23 or E33G: which status fits work in Indonesia?

## Short answer

**E23 and E33G are distinct immigration grounds.** Working KITAS E23 is used in eligible cases for work with an Indonesian employer. E33G is for remote work performed for a company based outside Indonesia. The actual employer matters more than the location of your laptop.

## Key differences

| Question | Working KITAS / E23 | Remote Worker / E33G |
|---|---|---|
| Intended for | Foreign professional employed by an Indonesian company | Professional working for an overseas company |
| Employment documents issued by | Indonesian employer | Overseas employer |
| Indonesian manpower approvals | RPTKA and other documents, where required | Does not replace authorisation for local employment |
| Duration | Depends on index and employment basis | Governed by E33G-specific rules |
| Work for another local company | Not automatically permitted | Not authorised by E33G alone |

The table describes the purposes of these statuses; it is not an assessment of any person's visa eligibility.

## If you are employed in Bali

For actual employment with a local company, consider [Working KITAS E23](/en/bali/visas/working-kitas/) and check the company, role and labour approvals. [RPTKA](/en/bali/business/foreign-worker-permits/) and [WLKP](/en/bali/business/wlkp/) are separate employer considerations.

## If your employer is overseas

[E33G Remote Worker KITAS](/en/bali/visas/e33g/) may suit remote employees of an overseas company, subject to its own employment-contract, income-evidence and permitted-activity conditions. It must not be treated as a universal route to work for Indonesian clients or on a local payroll.

## If you own a company

[Investor KITAS E28A](/en/bali/visas/investor-kitas/) is a third, separate category with personal shareholding requirements. Incorporating a PT PMA does not by itself establish E28A eligibility, nor does investing automatically permit work for any company.

## If you entered on a tourist visa

Do not start work on the assumption that a future application will fix your status. A [Working KITAS transition assessment](/en/bali/visas/working-kitas/conversion/) checks the initial visa index, deadlines and lawful route. Not every status can be changed without leaving.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I obtain E33G and work for a local company?

E33G is intended for overseas employment. Working for an Indonesian company requires a separate review of the permitted employment basis.

### Is E23 appropriate for any freelancer in Bali?

Not automatically. E23 requires verification of the employer, position and relevant manpower processes.

### How is E23 different from Investor KITAS?

E23 relates to employment; E28A has separate requirements for the foreign investor's participation in a company. These conditions are not interchangeable.

### Does every E23 holder need RPTKA?

An employer needs RPTKA in many cases, but the law provides exemptions for specific situations. The actual basis must be checked.

### Can I get E33G first and then find local employment?

Starting local employment requires a separate review of immigration status and approvals. E33G alone does not permit such work.

### Which category is cheaper?

Comparing only the application price is misleading: the grounds, documents, employer duties and possible additional payments differ.
