# E33G Remote Worker KITAS — live in Bali for 1 year without visa runs

## Key facts

- Type: Limited Stay Visa / ITAS for a remote worker.
- Duration: 1 year.
- Remote work for a company outside Indonesia.
- Verifiable income: at least USD 60,000 per year.
- No sponsor is required for a standard E33G.
- Exit and re-entry while the re-entry permit is valid.
- E33G extension is available online.
- e-ITAS and the re-entry permit are generated automatically after entry.
- Standard application: IDR 12,000,000 (≈ {{USD_12M}}).
- Expedited application: IDR 14,000,000 (≈ {{USD_14M}}).

If you work remotely for a foreign company and want to live in Indonesia for a full year rather than a few months, E33G is one of the most practical options.

E33G lets you:

- live in Indonesia for up to 1 year;
- continue working for a company outside Indonesia;
- leave the country and return without applying for a new visa each time;
- extend your status online;
- obtain an ITAS without an Indonesian sponsor.

## Who is E33G for?

Consider E33G if you:

- work remotely;
- are formally employed or have documented working arrangements with a foreign company;
- work for a company registered outside Indonesia;
- can prove annual income of at least USD 60,000;
- want to live in Bali long-term;
- do not want to arrange a new visa or visa run every 60–180 days;
- want an ITAS and the ability to leave and return.

If your income, contract or employment structure differs from the standard scenario, **tell your SAFRWAY manager right away**. We will review your circumstances and check which options for obtaining E33G may be available in your case.

## The main difference from C1 and eVOA

eVOA and [C1](/bali/visas/c1/) suit short- or medium-term visits.

E33G is a different type of status.

Instead of:

- 30–60 days on eVOA;
- up to 180 days on C1;
- having to plan your next visa cycle,

you receive a **one-year ITAS**.

For someone who actually lives in Bali and works remotely, this is a more practical long-term arrangement.

## What E33G provides

### One year of residence

E33G is issued for one year.

You do not need to leave Indonesia every few months solely to start a new visa cycle.

### Multiple trips

While your re-entry permit remains valid, you may leave Indonesia and return.

A trip to Singapore, Dubai, Europe or another destination does not, by itself, end your status.

### Remote work for a foreign company

E33G is intended for residence in Indonesia while carrying out work for a company established outside Indonesia.

This distinguishes it from a visitor visa.

### No sponsor

A standard E33G does not require an Indonesian sponsor, or *penjamin*.

### Online extension

The current official procedure allows the stay permit to be extended online through eVisa.

## The income requirement

For E33G, you must prove income of:

**at least USD 60,000 per year.**

That is equivalent to USD 5,000 per month when calculated annually.

Simply entering that amount in the application is not enough.

The income must be documented and consistent with your work for a foreign company.

Before filing, SAFRWAY therefore reviews the entire document package, not just the stated figure.

## What kind of employer is required?

The essential point is:

**the company must be established outside Indonesia.**

You need a document confirming your working relationship with that company.

In practice, we review:

- the company's legal name;
- its country of registration;
- employer details;
- your job title;
- employment start date;
- income;
- remote-working conditions;
- consistency between the agreement and the other documents.

If the agreement is poorly drafted or lacks important wording, it is better to address that before applying.

## What documents are needed?

The basic package includes:

- a passport valid for at least 6 months;
- a bank statement covering at least the last 3 months;
- for an application through SAFRWAY, a bank statement for the last 12 months;
- proof of funds equivalent to at least USD 2,000;
- a recent colour photograph;
- a CV;
- trip information or an itinerary;
- proof of annual income of at least USD 60,000;
- an employment agreement or another suitable agreement with a company outside Indonesia.

### Why are both 3 months and 12 months mentioned?

Officially, a bank statement for the last 3 months is required. We additionally request a 12-month statement based on practical experience with visa issuance.

The official E33G requirements list a bank statement for at least the last **3 months**.

For applications through SAFRWAY, we ask for **12 months**.

A longer history allows us to review income over time, compare payments with the employment agreement and identify inconsistencies between:

- stated annual income;
- actual deposits;
- the employer;
- the contract;
- banking documents.

In other words:

**3 months is the official minimum reference.**  
**12 months is the additional history requested when preparing a SAFRWAY case.**

The precise package should be checked before filing. The combination of **contract + income + bank records + employer** is central to E33G.

## What happens after approval?

Once the visa is issued, you have a limited period in which to enter Indonesia.

Current official information states that E33G must be used for entry within **90 days of issuance**.

After you pass immigration control on entry, the electronic ITAS and re-entry permit are issued automatically.

You do not need a separate visit to an immigration office solely to obtain the initial e-ITAS.

## Can you leave Indonesia?

Yes.

E33G is not a single-entry visitor visa.

With a valid re-entry permit, you may leave Indonesia and return during the validity of your status.

This is one of the main differences from [C1](/bali/visas/c1/).

## Can you work for an Indonesian company?

E33G is intended for remote work for a company outside Indonesia.

It should not be used as an ordinary work permit for employment with an Indonesian company or as unrestricted permission to sell services locally.

If your work involves an Indonesian employer or local commercial activity, a different category is needed.

## Family

If you are moving with family, each family member's status is arranged separately.

The appropriate category for a spouse or child depends on the family documents and the principal ITAS holder's status.

We separately check:

- the marriage certificate;
- the birth certificate;
- legalisation, apostille and translation where required;
- each family member's current status;
- the order of applications.

Read more: [arranging family status alongside E33G](/bali/knowledge/e33g/family/).

## Why E33G is useful for life in Bali

E33G leads to [KITAS / ITAS](/bali/knowledge/kitas-indonesia/), making it more practical for long-term life than a visitor visa.

In practice, an ITAS may be useful for:

- local paperwork;
- dealing with banks;
- registering for long-term services;
- obtaining SKTT;
- buying and registering a vehicle;
- other everyday and administrative matters.

Requirements still depend on the bank, authority or service. A KITAS does not mean automatic approval for every service.

## Extension

E33G can be extended online.

Before extending, the requirements and documents in force at that time must be checked again.

Do not assume that the documents needed for an initial application and for a later extension will always be identical.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long is E33G issued for?

One year.

### Is an Indonesian sponsor required?

No. A standard E33G does not require a sponsor or penjamin.

### What income is required?

At least USD 60,000 per year.

### Do you have to work for a foreign company?

Yes. E33G is intended for remote work for a company registered outside Indonesia.

### Can you leave and return on E33G?

Yes, while your re-entry permit is valid.

### Do you need visa runs?

No. This is a one-year ITAS, not a short-term visitor visa.

### Can E33G be extended?

Yes. Online extension is provided for by the official process.

### When is the ITAS issued?

The e-ITAS and re-entry permit are issued automatically after entry and immigration clearance.

### Can you bring your family?

Yes, but family members need their own dependent statuses. The category depends on the relationship and documents.

### Can you open a bank account?

ITAS makes dealing with local banks easier than a tourist visa, but the decision and required documents depend on the bank.
