# E33G Remote Worker KITAS — live in Bali for 1 year without visa runs

If you work remotely for a foreign company and want to live in Indonesia for a full year rather than a few months, E33G is one of the most practical options.

E33G allows you to:

- live in Indonesia for up to 1 year;
- continue working for a company outside Indonesia;
- leave and re-enter while your re-entry permit is valid;
- extend the status online;
- obtain an ITAS without an Indonesian sponsor.

**Standard SAFRWAY tariff — IDR 12,000,000 (≈ {{USD_12M}}), usually 7–10 working days.**

**Expedited SAFRWAY tariff — IDR 14,000,000 (≈ {{USD_14M}}), usually 5 working days, rarely 6.**

Message a SAFRWAY manager. We will review your employer, contract, income and banking documents and tell you whether E33G fits your case.

## Who is E33G for?

E33G is worth considering if you:

- work remotely;
- have documented employment or an equivalent working relationship with a foreign company;
- work for a company registered outside Indonesia;
- can prove at least USD 60,000 in annual income;
- plan to live in Bali long-term;
- do not want to restart a visa cycle every few months;
- want an ITAS and the ability to travel in and out.

If your income, contract or employment structure differs from the standard scenario, **tell the SAFRWAY manager immediately**. We will review the case and check what options may still work for E33G.

## How is E33G different from C1 and eVOA?

eVOA and C1 are short- or medium-term visitor solutions.

E33G is a residence status.

Instead of:

- 30–60 days on eVOA;
- up to 180 days on C1;
- planning the next visa cycle,

you receive a **one-year ITAS**.

For someone genuinely living in Bali while working remotely, this is a much more logical long-term structure.

## What does E33G provide?

### One year of residence

E33G is issued for one year.

You do not need to leave Indonesia every few months simply to start a new visa cycle.

### Re-entry / multiple trips

While the re-entry permit remains valid, you can leave Indonesia and return.

Travel to Singapore, Dubai, Europe or another country does not automatically cancel the status.

### Remote work for a foreign company

The official purpose of E33G is residence in Indonesia while performing work for a company established outside Indonesia.

### No sponsor

A standard E33G does not require an Indonesian sponsor / penjamin.

### Online extension

The current immigration process allows the stay permit to be extended online.

## Income requirement

You need to prove at least:

**USD 60,000 per year.**

That is roughly USD 5,000 per month in annualized terms.

The amount cannot exist only on paper. It should be supported by the broader document set.

SAFRWAY checks the combination:

**employer → employment agreement → stated income → real bank inflows.**

## Employer requirements

The employer must be established outside Indonesia.

We normally review:

- legal company name;
- country of registration;
- employer details;
- job title;
- employment start date;
- income;
- remote-work structure;
- consistency between the agreement and banking evidence.

If the agreement is weak or missing important points, it is better to fix the documentation before filing.

## Documents

The base set includes:

- passport valid for at least 6 months;
- official bank-history minimum of at least the last 3 months;
- for SAFRWAY preparation: bank statements for the last 12 months;
- proof of funds equivalent to at least USD 2,000;
- recent color photograph;
- CV;
- itinerary / trip information;
- proof of at least USD 60,000 annual income;
- employment agreement or another suitable agreement with a company outside Indonesia.

### Why both 3 months and 12 months?

Three months is the official minimum reference.

SAFRWAY asks for 12 months because it allows us to review income continuity and identify inconsistencies between the contract, actual payments and the employer before filing.

## After approval

E33G must be used for entry within 90 days after issuance.

After immigration clearance on entry, the e-ITAS and re-entry permit are generated automatically.

## Can you leave Indonesia?

Yes.

Unlike a single-entry visitor visa, E33G supports leaving and returning while the re-entry permit remains valid.

## Can you work for an Indonesian company?

E33G is intended for remote work for a company outside Indonesia.

It should not be treated as a general work permit for an Indonesian employer or as unrestricted authorization to conduct local commercial activity.

If your work is tied to an Indonesian employer or local business activity, another category is required.

## Family

Family members apply separately.

For a spouse, the relevant family category is normally E31B. For an eligible biological child under 18 who is unmarried, E31E is used.

See: E33G family guide.

## Why E33G is more practical for long-term life in Bali

Because it is an ITAS, E33G provides a stronger administrative basis than a visitor visa.

In practice, ITAS can be useful for:

- local documents;
- banks;
- SKTT;
- vehicle registration;
- longer-term services and other administrative matters.

This does not guarantee approval by every bank or authority. Each organization can have its own requirements.
