# What documents are needed for E33G Remote Worker KITAS?

E33G is not a category where uploading a passport and stating one income figure is enough.

Immigration needs a coherent picture:

**who employs you → where the company is registered → your role → how much you earn → how your bank records support that income.**

This is why much of the E33G review is about consistency between documents, rather than the number of documents.

## Basic document set

For a standard case, prepare:

- a passport valid for at least 6 months;
- a bank statement;
- evidence of funds equivalent to at least USD 2,000;
- evidence of annual income of at least USD 60,000;
- an employment agreement with a company registered outside Indonesia;
- a recent color photograph;
- a CV;
- an itinerary or information about your planned trip.

Additional documents may be needed for an individual case.

## Passport

You need a valid passport for the application. The stated official minimum validity for E33G is **at least 6 months**.

Before applying, check:

- spelling of your name;
- passport number;
- issue date;
- expiry date;
- place of birth;
- citizenship;
- consistency with the other documents.

Even a small difference in name spelling between a passport, contract and bank records can require further checking.

## Employment agreement

E33G requires an employment agreement with a company registered **outside Indonesia**. This is one of the first documents SAFRWAY reviews.

The agreement should make the following clear:

- who the employer is;
- the company's legal name;
- country of registration;
- who the employee is;
- job title;
- employment start date;
- income amount;
- currency;
- payment frequency;
- nature of the employment relationship;
- both parties' signatures.

Ideally, the agreement should leave no doubt that you genuinely work for the foreign company and receive the stated income.

## Should the agreement explicitly say that the work is remote?

E33G is specifically designed for remote workers. If the agreement does not make it understandable that the work can be performed remotely from Indonesia, have it reviewed before applying.

Depending on how the contract is structured, this may be supported by:

- a separate remote-work clause;
- a statement of the remote working arrangement;
- the place where the work is performed;
- an addendum;
- another document confirming the employment arrangement.

Do not add wording solely as a formality for the visa. Documents must reflect the actual employment relationship.

## Annual income of USD 60,000

E33G requires **at least USD 60,000 per year**, or approximately USD 5,000 per month on an annualized basis.

In practice, simply putting this amount in the agreement is not enough. The income must be supported by evidence.

We review:

- the amount in the contract;
- bank inflows;
- payment frequency;
- the payer;
- currency;
- income stability.

If the agreement states USD 5,000 per month but the account consistently receives substantially less, explain the difference before applying.

## Gross or net income

A gross amount in the contract and a different net amount received in the bank are not necessarily a problem. The payment structure needs to be clear.

If your agreement includes:

- gross salary;
- net salary;
- guaranteed take-home pay;
- deductions;
- taxes;
- bonuses;
- reimbursements or compensation,

explain to the manager in advance how the actual payment is calculated.

The objective is to avoid documents suggesting that the declared income exists only on paper.

## Bank statements: 3 months and 12 months

Officially, a statement covering the last 3 months is required. In addition, SAFRWAY requests 12 months of statements based on practical experience with visa issuance.

### Official minimum

The published E33G requirements specify statements for the last **3 months**. You also need to show funds equivalent to at least **USD 2,000**.

### SAFRWAY's practice

For preparation through SAFRWAY, we request statements for the last **12 months**. This does not replace the official minimum: it supports a fuller review of the case.

The longer record helps establish:

- whether income is genuinely stable;
- how often payments arrive;
- who sends them;
- whether the payment pattern matches the agreement;
- whether there are long periods without income;
- whether the salary has changed;
- whether large one-off transfers replaced regular payments.

**3 months is the official minimum. 12 months is SAFRWAY's additional review standard based on practical experience.**

## What should the bank statement show?

Ideally, the document should clearly identify:

- account holder's name;
- account number or identifier;
- bank;
- statement period;
- transaction dates;
- amounts;
- currency;
- payer details, if the bank provides them.

If the statement is in another language or an unusual format, send it to the manager before applying.

## What if salary does not come directly from the employer?

This can happen. For example, payments may arrive:

- through a payroll service;
- from another company in the same group;
- through a payment intermediary;
- in several transfers;
- in a different currency.

Do not hide this or try to alter the statement to make it fit.

**Tell the manager how the payments work.** We will review which documents can connect the actual inflows to the employer and employment agreement.

## What if your income changed?

A pay increase, currency change or new agreement does not by itself make E33G unavailable. The documents should explain the change.

It helps to prepare:

- the new agreement;
- an addendum;
- an employer letter;
- evidence of a change in position;
- other supporting documents, if they actually exist.

## What if the employer is new?

If you recently joined the company and do not yet have a long payment history from that employer, tell the manager.

We will review:

- employment start date;
- contract terms;
- previous banking history;
- current inflows;
- other available documents.

It is better to assess a non-standard case before filing, not after Immigration requests clarification.

## Checking the foreign company

For E33G, the employer must genuinely exist and be registered outside Indonesia. In preparing the case, SAFRWAY may check:

- full legal name;
- registration number;
- incorporation date;
- country;
- registered address;
- company status;
- contract signatory's details;
- consistency with public register information.

If company documents differ from the contract, clarify this in advance.

## CV

The CV helps connect:

- your profession;
- current role;
- experience;
- employer;
- stated working arrangement.

It does not need to read like an advertising brochure. It should be clear and consistent with the employment agreement.

## Itinerary

An itinerary is also part of the official document set. This does not mean you must buy non-refundable flights before the visa is issued.

SAFRWAY recommends **buying flights after the E33G has actually been issued**.

If tickets are already booked or the entry date is critical, tell the manager before filing.

## Photograph

A recent color photograph is needed. Do not use:

- an old photograph copied from another document;
- a low-quality image;
- heavily retouched images;
- a photograph that no longer resembles your current appearance.

## How SAFRWAY checks the package before filing

We review documents as a connected set, not as isolated files.

### 1. Passport

Do the personal details match?

### 2. Employer

Does the company exist, and do its details match?

### 3. Contract

Does it reflect the current role and working arrangement?

### 4. Income

Is annual income of at least USD 60,000 supported?

### 5. Bank

Do actual inflows support the stated income?

### 6. Overall consistency

Do the documents contradict one another?

This is the best stage to identify a problem, rather than after filing.

## Common document issues

Extra attention is often needed when:

- contract income and bank inflows differ;
- salary is not paid by the employer;
- you recently changed employers;
- the agreement was signed recently;
- some salary is paid as bonuses;
- payments arrive in several currencies;
- names are spelled differently;
- the company changed its address or legal name;
- the role in the CV differs from the agreement;
- there is no clear evidence of remote work;
- most annual income arrived in one payment.

These are not automatic grounds for refusal, but it is better to explain them and connect the evidence before applying.

## Do documents need translation?

It depends on the document and language. Do not translate or rewrite documents before they have been checked. Send the originals to SAFRWAY first.

We will explain:

- whether translation is needed;
- the appropriate format;
- whether a separate supporting document is needed;
- whether the contract itself needs correction.

## Can a new contract be prepared specifically for E33G?

Documents must reflect the real relationship between the employee and company. If the current agreement is incomplete or outdated, the documentation can be brought up to date.

Do not create fictitious employment or invent income solely for a visa. SAFRWAY works with genuine cases and helps prepare appropriate supporting documents.

## What to send for an initial review

To assess the case, usually start with:

1. passport;
2. employment agreement;
3. bank statements for 12 months;
4. a short description of the employer;
5. information about actual income;
6. CV, if already available.

The manager will then explain what is missing.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a 3-month statement enough?

That is the official minimum. SAFRWAY additionally requests 12 months based on practical experience with visa issuance.

### What if bank income differs from the agreement?

Tell the manager. We need to understand the reason and what documents explain it.

### What if a payroll company sends the payments?

Show the manager the arrangement. We will check how to connect payroll payments with the employer and contract.

### What if I recently changed jobs?

The case needs individual review. A new job does not automatically make E33G unavailable.

### Is an employment agreement required?

Yes. E33G's official requirements include an employment agreement with a company outside Indonesia.

### Can I use a contract with my own foreign company?

Do not assume any contract with your own company automatically qualifies. The actual employment structure and documents need to be checked first.

### Must the USD 60,000 income be shown in the bank?

You need to prove annual income of at least USD 60,000. Banking history is a key part of that evidence.

### Do I need to buy tickets for the itinerary?

SAFRWAY recommends buying tickets after the visa is issued. Tell the manager about any tickets already purchased.

### What if my situation is not standard?

**Contact the manager directly.** We will review your documents and check which E33G application options are available in your case.
