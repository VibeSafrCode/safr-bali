# E33G KITAS extension in Indonesia — SAFRWAY assistance

## Quick answer

E33G is a KITAS category for remote work for a company outside Indonesia. Official immigration information provides for the possibility of extending the ITAS in this category. However, whether **your particular** E33G can be extended, the deadline and the required documents must be assessed against your active status and the rules in force when you contact us.

**E33G extension through SAFRWAY costs 12,000,000 IDR.** There is no expedited extension option.

## Is your E33G expiring? Let's check your status first

If your current [E33G Remote Worker KITAS](/bali/visas/e33g/) is nearing expiry, you do not automatically have to leave Indonesia to obtain a new visa. An ITAS extension is available in some cases, but the correct approach depends on your documents, immigration history and the functions currently available in the eVisa system.

SAFRWAY starts by checking which ITAS is active, when it expires, whether you still meet the E33G conditions, whether any restrictions affect the application and whether your employment evidence from a foreign company must be updated.

## When should you apply for an extension?

Under the published general procedure for one-year ITAS permits, an extension application may be submitted during the final **30 days** before expiry, up to the expiry date of the current permit. This is a procedural window, **not advice to apply on the last day**. We recheck availability and application mechanics for the specific category before proceeding.

Contact us early: preparing documents and additional checks takes time. If your permit has already expired, that is a separate situation and should not be treated as an ordinary extension.

## What documents does SAFRWAY review?

- Passport and electronic ITAS / KITAS.
- Number and validity dates of the current permit.
- Evidence of your last entry and current address when required.
- Valid employment contract with an employer **outside Indonesia**.
- Evidence of income and payment regularity, if required by the applicable procedure.
- The current document package and technical eVisa requirements.
- Changes in circumstances since the original E33G was issued.

For an **initial** E33G, the official category page refers to a three-month bank statement and available funds of at least the equivalent of 2,000 USD. Based on its preparation experience, SAFRWAY additionally asks for **12 months** of bank history. This **does not mean** that Immigration necessarily requires 12 months for every extension. We check the extension document list separately.

## When might a new visa be needed instead?

If your ITAS cannot be extended or your circumstances have changed substantially, SAFRWAY will assess a new application or another lawful category. Do not automatically pay for an extension simply because your KITAS is expiring: the correct procedure must be selected first.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can E33G be extended at all?

Current general guidance allows E33G ITAS extensions. For each client, we still check the requirements, active status and procedure at the time of the request.

### How much does an extension cost through SAFRWAY?

**12,000,000 IDR.**

### Can I get an urgent E33G extension?

SAFRWAY has **no** separate expedited extension. The expedited price of 14 million IDR applies to a **new application**, not an extension.

### Do I have to leave Bali to extend my KITAS?

Not always. Where your status allows an extension, the process may be available in Indonesia. Leaving or submitting a new application is considered only after verification.

### Is a 12-month bank statement required by law?

No. The 12-month history is an additional SAFRWAY request to assess evidence, not a universal government requirement. The document list for a specific extension is checked separately.

### How long does the extension take?

There is no single guaranteed processing time. It depends on immigration checks and possible requests for further documents.

### If my KITAS has already expired, can I simply extend it for the same price?

We cannot promise that. The actual status and lawful options must first be verified; an overstay requires a separate assessment.
