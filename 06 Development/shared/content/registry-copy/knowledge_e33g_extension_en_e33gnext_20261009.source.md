## Quick answer

If your E33G is about to expire, start by checking the **ITAS expiry date**, your current category and whether an extension is available in eVisa. Immigration classification provides for E33G extensions, but the actual decision depends on meeting the requirements. Alternatives include a new application or another lawful immigration route.

## What is actually expiring: the visa, ITAS or entry permit?

With E33G, several documents must not be confused: the electronic visa used for initial entry, the ITAS/KITAS and the re-entry permit. The deadline for your first entry under a visa is not the expiry date of your active ITAS.

Before planning travel or an application, find the current date on your **valid electronic stay permit**. If you are unsure, send the details to a manager for checking.

## Three possible scenarios

### 1. Extend your current E33G

This may be appropriate when your active status and documents allow an ITAS extension. Current immigration instructions provide for such a procedure. SAFRWAY does not promise that it will be available without exceptions for every immigration history.

SAFRWAY client price: **12,000,000 IDR**. No separate expedited extension is offered.

### 2. Apply for a new E33G

We consider a new E33G where the existing permit cannot be continued or a different legally available procedure is chosen. A new visa may have separate entry requirements and should not automatically be equated with an extension.

SAFRWAY client price: **12,000,000 IDR** for a new standard application or **14,000,000 IDR** for an expedited one.

### 3. Change to another lawful basis

If you no longer work for a foreign company or your circumstances have changed, another eligible category may be worth checking. Investor status, family reunification and other statuses each have their own conditions; E33G does not replace them.

The procedure and price for such a change are determined separately according to the chosen basis.

## What to check before the year ends

- Your ITAS expiry date, not just the original visa's expiry.
- Valid passport and travel history.
- Whether you genuinely continue working for a company outside Indonesia.
- Whether your employment contract is still valid.
- Whether you still meet the required income level.
- Any Immigration requests or changes to your personal details.
- Whether bank history and income evidence need updating.

For an **initial** E33G, the official category requires annual income of **at least 60,000 USD**, a contract with a foreign company, a **three-month** bank statement and available funds of at least **2,000 USD**. SAFRWAY requests **12 months** of bank history as an additional preparation standard. The list specifically required for an extension may differ.

## How early should you start?

The published general procedure for annual ITAS permits provides for filing within the final 30 days before expiry. That is not permission to leave document checks until the last day. SAFRWAY recommends contacting a manager early to prepare for the allowable filing window.

If you plan to travel, separately check the validity of your re-entry permit and whether travel could affect the application.

## What not to do

Do not assume your old visa is automatically extended after payment; do not rely only on the phrase “one-year visa” without checking your ITAS; do not submit a new application instead of an extension without assessing the consequences; and do not treat a visitor visa as a universal substitute for an appropriate work status.

If the permit has already expired, this article is not an overstay remedy: an individual assessment is necessary.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a visa run mandatory after one year on E33G?

There is no universal rule. We first check whether the existing ITAS can be extended; if it cannot, we examine other lawful options.

### Will the extension be granted if I cannot provide new income evidence?

Approval cannot be guaranteed. Your documents and actual situation must still satisfy the category's requirements.

### Can I obtain E33G for a second year several months in advance?

The allowable filing date is governed by the current ITAS procedure. It is useful to prepare documents and check status in advance, but the application itself must be submitted within the permitted window.

### Can I just pay for a new E33G visa instead of extending?

Not automatically. A new visa and an ITAS extension are separate procedures with different consequences.

### What does the next term cost through SAFRWAY?

An extension, if available, costs **12 million IDR**. A new application costs **12 million IDR** standard or **14 million IDR** expedited.

### Can E33G be extended if I have changed my foreign employer?

The updated contract, income and continued eligibility must be assessed separately. Approval is not guaranteed.
