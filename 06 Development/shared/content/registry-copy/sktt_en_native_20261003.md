# SKTT: a clear plan for your documents

Local paperwork can be confusing after obtaining a stay status. We start by checking what you need to achieve and whether your request concerns an SKTT application.

## Where the work begins

We clarify your current status and residential address, review the initial information and identify where the enquiry belongs. We then prepare the list of materials and explain the order of preparation.

We do not rely on a universal document list without checking your circumstances. The applicable requirements and agreed procedure need to be clarified before work begins.

## Scope of assistance

Help organising preparation, a consistency check of the information you provide and coordination of the agreed stages. If information is needed from the property owner or another participant, we explain what needs to be clarified in advance.

You can see what is ready, what remains to be supplied and which stage follows next.

## Cost and outcome

The scope and cost are agreed after the initial review. The proposal identifies the expected service outcome and each party's responsibilities. Issuance of the document depends on the relevant authority's decision and the required supporting basis.

**Can I use a guide written for another area?**  
It can be a starting point for your questions, but the applicable process and supporting materials need to be confirmed for your address and circumstances.

**What should I tell the manager?**  
Share your current status, area of residence and reason for the enquiry. Full documents should be shared only after agreeing on a secure method.
