# Student KITAS E30 in Indonesia

## Quick Answer

**Student KITAS E30 is a temporary residence pathway in Indonesia for educational purposes.** The specific visa index depends on the educational programme: school education, university studies, academic exchanges and other recognised educational categories have different eligibility requirements. Before preparing the documents, it is necessary to confirm admission and identify an eligible sponsor.

If you are planning to study in Bali or another region of Indonesia, SAFRWAY can help determine the appropriate application process, review your initial documents and arrange assistance through qualified specialists.

Admission to an educational institution and visa approval depend on the institution and the relevant government authorities.

## What Types of Education Qualify?

The official visa catalogue includes a separate category, **E30A**, for school education. **E30B** is designated for higher education. Additional educational categories are also available, including those intended for exchange programmes.

A long-term student visa should not be pursued solely because someone has enrolled in a short language course or plans to study independently.

We begin by reviewing the name and status of the educational institution, the admission document, the duration of the programme and the applicant's age.

For a short visit to attend an educational event, a different visa category may sometimes be appropriate instead of Student KITAS.

## Why Contact with the Educational Institution Is Necessary

Official confirmation of enrolment and the proper involvement of the receiving institution are important requirements for obtaining educational residence status.

A university or school may provide an admission letter and coordinate with the sponsor. However, the institution's responsibilities cannot automatically be transferred to a visa agency.

We typically check:

- Whether an official admission document exists and whether it specifies the programme dates.
- Which visa index corresponds to the educational programme.
- Who is permitted to act as the sponsor and through whose account the application must be submitted.
- Which financial and personal documents must be provided.
- Whether the educational institution must obtain additional approval or complete an additional registration.

For more information, see [Documents for Student KITAS E30](/bali/knowledge/student-kitas/documents/).

## Length of Stay and Extensions

Official information distinguishes residence periods according to the specific E30 visa index and educational programme.

Different educational pathways may offer initial residence periods of one or several years. However, this does not mean that every student is entitled to the same duration, nor does it guarantee a particular length of stay.

We will confirm the available residence period immediately before submitting the application and separately review the conditions for extension.

**Student residence status is not equivalent to a work permit.**

Employment with an Indonesian company requires a different legal basis. See [Working KITAS](/bali/visas/working-kitas/).

If the intended activity is remote work for a foreign employer, the suitability of [E33G](/bali/visas/e33g/) must be assessed separately.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I Obtain Student KITAS Without Proof of Admission?

For a standard long-term educational residence pathway, confirmation from the receiving educational institution is essential.

If such confirmation is unavailable, the eligibility basis for studying in Indonesia must first be established.

### Are E30 and E30B the Same?

E30 is used as a general designation for educational visa categories, while E30B specifically relates to higher education.

We verify the exact visa index against the educational programme and the current official catalogue.

### Is Paying for a Language Course Enough to Obtain Student KITAS?

Not necessarily.

The institution's accreditation or otherwise eligible status, the nature of the course and the specific visa category are all relevant.

### Can I Work in Bali with Student KITAS?

Student residence status does not grant general permission to work as an employee or sell services in Indonesia.

Any employment plans require a separate assessment.

### Can My Family Accompany Me?

In certain circumstances, separate [family visa arrangements](/bali/visas/family-kitas/) may be considered.

However, their applicability to a particular E30 holder is not guaranteed and must be assessed individually.
