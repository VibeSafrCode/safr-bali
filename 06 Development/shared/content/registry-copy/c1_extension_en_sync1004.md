# Extend your C1 in Bali for another 60 days

If you are already in Indonesia on a [C1](/bali/visas/c1/) and want to stay beyond the initial 60 days, your current stay can be extended.

After the first extension, your total stay can reach 120 days. After the second extension, it can reach 180 days on the same entry.

**One C1 extension stage through SAFRWAY costs IDR 2,000,000 (≈ {{USD}}).**

Message a SAFRWAY manager. We will check your current visa and stay period and tell you which stage is available next.

## Who is the extension service for?

This service is suitable if:

- you are already in Indonesia on a valid C1;
- you want to stay beyond the initial 60 days;
- you have not reached the 180-day maximum;
- you want SAFRWAY to handle the next extension stage.

If your C1 has already been extended, the manager will check your current stay period and confirm which stage remains available.

## How the extension works through SAFRWAY

1. You message a SAFRWAY manager and send your current C1 details.
2. We check your entry date, current permitted stay and any previous extensions.
3. We confirm which stage is available and what information is needed.
4. We prepare and support the extension process.
5. Once completed, we confirm the new permitted-stay end date.

## How long can you stay?

The standard cycle is:

- up to 60 days — initial stay;
- up to 120 days — after the first extension;
- up to 180 days — after the second extension.

The maximum stay under one C1 entry can therefore reach up to 180 days.

## Price

**One C1 extension through SAFRWAY — IDR 2,000,000 (≈ {{USD}}).**

Each extension stage is paid separately.

If you only need to stay until day 120, you do not need to pay for the second extension to day 180 in advance.

## What do we need to check your case?

Message the manager and prepare:

- your passport;
- your current C1;
- your entry date;
- confirmation of the previous extension, if there was one.

If anything else is required, the manager will tell you after reviewing your case.

## What if another agency issued my C1?

That does not prevent you from using SAFRWAY for the extension.

Message the manager, send your current visa and stay information, and we will check the case and the next available stage.

## When should you start the extension?

Do not leave the extension until the final day.

It is better to contact the manager in advance so there is enough time to check your status and prepare the next stage before your permitted stay expires.

## FAQ

### Do I need to leave Indonesia to extend the C1?
No. A standard C1 extension increases the current stay without requiring an exit.

### How many days does one extension add?
Up to 60 days.

### How many times can a C1 be extended?
After the initial 60 days, two sequential extension stages are available, allowing a total stay of up to 180 days.

### Do I need to pay for both extensions at once?No. Each stage is paid separately. Each stage costs **IDR 2,000,000 (≈ {{USD}})** through SAFRWAY.

### Can SAFRWAY extend a C1 issued by another agency?
Yes. We first review the current visa and stay status.

### Can I leave Indonesia and return on the same C1?
No. The C1 remains a single-entry visa.

### What happens after 180 days?
Message a SAFRWAY manager. Depending on your plans, you may consider a new C1, [D12](/bali/visas/d12/), [E33G](/bali/visas/e33g/) or another suitable status such as a [KITAS](/bali/knowledge/kitas-indonesia/).
