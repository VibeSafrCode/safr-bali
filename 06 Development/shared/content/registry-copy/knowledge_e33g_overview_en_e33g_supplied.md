## Direct answer

E33G is a one-year ITAS for foreigners who live in Indonesia while working remotely for a company outside Indonesia. The main requirements are verifiable annual income of at least USD 60,000 and documented employment with a foreign company.

## Key facts

- One-year ITAS
- Remote work for a foreign company
- Minimum annual income: USD 60,000
- No Indonesian sponsor required
- Re-entry allowed with valid re-entry permit
- Online extension
- Official bank-history minimum: 3 months
- SAFRWAY preparation standard: 12 months

## Who should consider E33G?

E33G can fit if:

- you work remotely;
- your employer is outside Indonesia;
- the employment relationship can be documented;
- annual income is at least USD 60,000;
- income is visible in banking history;
- you want to stay in Indonesia for around a year;
- you need to leave and return without restarting the visa process.

If your case is non-standard, tell the SAFRWAY manager. We will review what options may still work before filing.

## Foreign employer requirement

The documents should clearly show who the employer is, where the company is registered, your role, employment start date, income and working arrangement.

## Income requirement

Minimum: **USD 60,000 per year**.

The income should be supported by the employment agreement, bank inflows and employer information.

## Bank statement: 3 months or 12?

### Official minimum
At least 3 months of bank history and at least USD 2,000 equivalent in funds.

### SAFRWAY standard
For preparation through SAFRWAY, we ask for 12 months of bank history to review consistency and continuity.

## Documents

Typical documents include:

- passport;
- bank statement;
- proof of funds;
- proof of annual income;
- foreign employment agreement;
- recent photo;
- CV;
- itinerary.

## Sponsor

A standard E33G does not require an Indonesian sponsor.

## Work rights

E33G is designed for remote work for a foreign company, not as an unrestricted work permit for an Indonesian employer.

## Travel

You can leave and re-enter while the re-entry permit remains valid.

## Family

Family members apply separately under the relevant dependent categories.

## E33G vs C1

C1 is a visitor visa for up to 180 days with extensions. E33G is a one-year ITAS with re-entry rights and is much more suitable for genuine long-term residence when eligibility is met.

## Processing through SAFRWAY

Standard: **7–10 working days**.  
Expedited: **5 working days**, rarely 6.

These are operational estimates, not guaranteed government issue dates.

## Tickets

The safest approach is to buy flights after the visa is actually issued.

## FAQ

### Is E33G a KITAS?
Yes. After entry, an ITAS is generated.

### How long is it valid?
One year.

### Minimum income?
USD 60,000 per year.

### Is 3 months of bank history enough?
That is the official minimum reference. SAFRWAY requests 12 months for preparation.

### Can a non-standard case still work?
Possibly. Send the details to the manager for review.

### Is a sponsor required?
No.

### Can I work for an Indonesian company?
Not under the standard E33G remote-worker purpose.

### Can I bring family?
Yes, through separate dependent statuses.
