# Corporate Documents for PT PMA and Other Indonesian Companies

## Quick answer

**SAFRWAY helps identify, obtain and prepare corporate documents for a specific purpose: a bank, business partner, transaction, due diligence, foreign registration or internal changes.** We first check the company’s legal standing and the applicant’s authority, then determine which documents are truly required and in what form they may be provided.

There is no single universal “document pack” for every Indonesian company. Opening an account, filing with a foreign registry and proving a director’s authority may require different records.

## Corporate documents that may be required

- **Akta Pendirian** — the company’s notarial deed of establishment, where applicable and in the required version.
- **Anggaran Dasar** — articles of association and their current versions.
- **AHU decisions and confirmations (SK)** — approvals, notifications and registration or change confirmations relevant to the document type.
- **Akta Perubahan** — notarial deeds recording subsequent amendments.
- **NIB/OSS records** — current business registration and licensing details.
- **NPWP and tax records**, where legitimately requested by the recipient.
- **Shareholders’ resolutions, director authorization, powers of attorney, extracts and evidence of shareholding**, where available and relevant.

This is an indicative list. It does not mean every document exists for every company, is publicly accessible or may be obtained without an authorized person’s participation.

## Current records and proper authority matter

The incorporation deed may not reflect the latest director or shareholder change. In reviewing a company, we compare available recent corporate actions with **AHU** records, and business activity information with **OSS/NIB**. These systems have distinct legal functions.

If corporate registrations need correction or updating, that is a separate [company amendments service](/en/bali/business/corporate-changes/), not merely document translation. A new company follows the [PT PMA incorporation](/en/bali/business/company-registration/) process.

## Why a corporate document package may be needed

### Banks and payment services

The recipient may require current management details, incorporation records, ownership structure, address or business activity information. Providing the documents does not guarantee approval of an account or service.

### Foreign counterparties and international transactions

A certified copy, translation, apostille or other form of legalization may be requested. We check which documents possess the required official status. An [apostille](/en/bali/documents/apostille/) cannot be promised for every commercial paper.

### Verification of authority and company changes

Shareholders’ resolutions, current deeds and information on duly authorized directors help establish who may represent the company. A notary or formal registration step may be required.

## Preparing documents in another language

If records are needed abroad, we confirm the target language, format and acceptability of a [sworn translation](/en/bali/documents/certified-translations/). Some cases call for [consular legalization](/en/bali/documents/legalization/) if the apostille process is unavailable or inapplicable.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I get company documents without access to its online account?

This depends on the document type, public availability and proof of the applicant’s authority. We do not promise access to another person’s restricted corporate information.

### Is NIB a company incorporation document?

No. NIB is part of business registration through OSS, while notarial and corporate records concern the formation and legal status of the company.

### Can an old set of articles be used after a director changes?

The current records and the nature of the change must be checked. An older deed may be insufficient for the particular recipient.

### Can you provide certified company documents for a foreign bank?

We can assess the required package, check the bank’s requirements and confirm availability of notarization, translation and legalization. Bank approval is not guaranteed.

### Do corporate documents automatically qualify me for Investor KITAS?

No. Having PT PMA and company documents does not replace the separate conditions for [Investor KITAS E28A](/en/bali/visas/investor-kitas/).

### Can you update AHU records while preparing the documents?

That is handled separately as a [company amendment](/en/bali/business/corporate-changes/), with a separately agreed scope and price.
