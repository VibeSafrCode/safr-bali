# D2 Multiple Entry — a business visa for regular business trips to Indonesia

If you need to visit Indonesia regularly for meetings, negotiations, checking goods, discussing contracts or other business matters, D2 is more suitable than a single-entry business visa or tourist D1.

The main idea:

**one multiple-entry business visa → several business trips → up to 60 days on each new entry.**

When needed, the current stay can be extended under the rules in force.

## Who is D2 for?

Consider D2 if you:

- travel to Indonesia regularly on business;
- meet partners;
- conduct negotiations;
- discuss commercial terms;
- sign business agreements;
- visit to purchase goods;
- check goods, production facilities, an office or a supplier;
- combine business travel with tourism;
- do not want to obtain a new business visitor visa before every visit.

D2 is particularly convenient for **repeat business trips**, not permanent employment in Indonesia.

## What can you do on D2?

Under the current official classification, D2 is used for permitted business-visit activities, including:

- business meetings;
- negotiations;
- discussions about cooperation terms;
- purchasing goods;
- signing business agreements;
- checking goods;
- visiting an office, factory or production site;
- other business visits within visitor status.

D2 can also be used for tourism and visits to family or friends.

## What does D2 not permit?

D2 is **not a work KITAS** and is not unrestricted employment authorisation.

It does not give you the right to:

- work as an employee of an Indonesian company;
- receive a local salary;
- receive payment for work from an Indonesian company or individual;
- use a visitor visa instead of work authorisation;
- carry out activities requiring a separate work or business status.

A simple distinction:

**negotiations and business visits — yes; local employment — no.**

## How do the 60 days per entry work?

D2 involves two different time periods.

### The validity of the visa itself

For example, D2 may be valid for 1 or 2 years and used for repeated trips.

### The duration of a particular stay

A separate period of stay begins after a new entry.

Initially — **up to 60 days**.

A 1-year D2 therefore does not mean continuous presence in Indonesia throughout that year.

## Can D2 be extended?

Yes.

Under current rules, the period of stay granted under D2 can be extended.

Initially — up to **60 days**.

With extensions, that particular visit can last a total of **up to 180 days**.

### Extension price through SAFRWAY

**2 500 000 IDR (≈ {{USD_2_5M}})**

It is the **current period of stay** that is extended, not D2’s overall validity.

After departure and a new entry on a valid D2, a new period of stay begins.

Read more: [D1/D2 extension](/bali/visas/d1-d2/extension/).

## D2 or D1?

The main difference is the purpose of the trip.

### D1

For:

- tourism;
- holidays;
- regular personal travel;
- visits to family and friends.

### D2

For:

- negotiations;
- meetings;
- purchases;
- checking goods;
- signing agreements;
- regular business trips.

Both categories allow multiple entries, and neither authorises local employment.

Read more: [D1 or D2](/bali/visas/d1-d2/).

## D2 or D12?

These cover different business situations.

### D2

Suitable for regular business visits:

- meetings;
- negotiations;
- contracts;
- purchases;
- checking goods.

The initial stay per entry is up to 60 days.

### D12

This is a separate category for pre-investment activities, exploring investment opportunities and longer business-related stays.

If your plans involve more than regular meetings, such as preparing an investment or a more complex long-term business situation, the manager can check [D12](/bali/visas/d12/).

## What documents are required?

The basic D2 document set includes:

- an international passport valid for at least 6 months;
- bank statements for the last 3 months;
- proof of funds equivalent to at least 2 000 USD;
- a recent colour photograph;
- a CV;
- a travel itinerary;
- sponsor / penjamin documents if required for your application;
- evidence of the business purpose if required for the particular filing;
- additional documents depending on the sponsor and the current eVisa process.

SAFRWAY checks the list before filing.

## Can I set up a company on D2?

Holding D2 does not make it a work or investor KITAS.

However, D2 may suit preparatory business activities:

- negotiations;
- meetings with advisers;
- discussions about the business structure;
- signing permitted agreements.

If the aim is to manage a company, work for it or obtain investor KITAS, the appropriate status must be selected separately.

## Can D2 be converted to KITAS inside Indonesia?

This switch should not be promised as a standard service.

Current official information states that D1/D2 stays can be extended, but their visitor stay permit **cannot be converted directly to ITAS**.

If your ultimate goal is KITAS, tell the manager in advance.

We will check current rules and suggest a lawful route rather than promise conversion simply because some agencies advertise it.
