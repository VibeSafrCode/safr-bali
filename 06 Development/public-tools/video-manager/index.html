SAFRWAY Video Manager

A local tool for the SAFRWAY channel owner to upload selected videos and manage their publication on YouTube, including commands carried out with an AI assistant.

This is an information website, not an upload portal. These pages do not connect your Google account, receive OAuth tokens, or accept videos. The desktop utility is for the owner’s personal workflow; public account registration is not offered.

What the tool does

  • Uploads owner-selected local MP4 files and thumbnails directly to YouTube.
  • Updates video titles, descriptions, visibility and publication schedules at the owner’s direction.
  • Checks the authorized channel and operation results to help avoid publishing to the wrong channel or duplicating uploads.

The original local video files are not modified by an upload. The current utility has no command for deleting published YouTube videos.

Authorization and control

Google authorization happens through Google’s own OAuth flow. The utility does not ask for or store a Google password. Its requested permission, youtube.force-ssl, is broader than uploading alone: Google describes access that can include viewing, editing and deleting YouTube data. The current tool does not expose every operation allowed by that permission.

Only expressly directed operations should be performed. An AI assistant may process selected materials and results needed for an owner’s command; this does not mean that all processing stays on the owner’s computer. See the privacy policy before connecting the utility.
