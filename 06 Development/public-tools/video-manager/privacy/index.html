SAFRWAY Video Manager

Privacy policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026 · Policy version 2026-10-23

This policy covers the personal desktop utility operated by SAFRWAY for its channel owner, and these two informational web pages. The utility uses YouTube API Services. It is not a public multi-user upload service.

DataUse and sharingStorage and deletionRevoke accessContact

Information handled by the utility

  • The authorized YouTube channel’s identifier and name, and information needed to confirm the intended channel.
  • Local videos and thumbnails selected by the owner; titles, descriptions, visibility, scheduled publication times and related upload instructions.
  • YouTube operation results, video identifiers and processing or publication status.
  • Local working records used for verification and duplicate prevention, which can include file paths, file hashes, schedules, status and resumable-upload session addresses.
  • OAuth credentials and access or refresh tokens needed to communicate with Google on the owner’s behalf.

The utility does not request or store the owner’s Google password. The Google permission youtube.force-ssl can authorize broader YouTube data operations, including deletion, although the current utility has no published-video deletion command.

Purpose and recipients

Information is used to carry out the owner’s chosen YouTube operations, check the channel and results, resume an interrupted upload where supported, and avoid accidental duplicate operations. Video files, thumbnails and their metadata are sent to Google/YouTube when the owner directs those actions. Google handles that information under the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service.

When the owner asks an AI assistant to carry out an operation, necessary selected materials, instructions and results may also be processed through the owner’s AI-assistant service, currently OpenAI. That processing is not necessarily local and is subject to the provider’s terms and the owner’s account settings. OAuth tokens and client secrets must not be placed in prompts or published on this website. This policy does not promise a provider-specific training or retention setting that has not been configured.

The utility has no function for selling Google user data, advertising profiles or credit decisions. Google-derived information is used only for the disclosed owner-facing workflow, not to train a general-purpose AI model through this utility. Access by other people requires the owner’s express authorization, except where necessary for security or applicable law.

Storage, retention and deletion

OAuth credentials, tokens and working records are held in the protected local application profile, outside the website and source repository. The public website does not store these tokens, upload-session addresses or local media. Access is limited to the owner’s local account and authorized tools; no security measure is a guarantee against every risk.

The operator must refresh or remove YouTube API metadata within 30 calendar days, including periods when the utility is not used. The utility checks retained data when it runs; there is no always-on cleanup service. Before a long period of inactivity, the operator must remove the local API-derived records manually rather than leave them indefinitely. Credentials are retained only while needed for authorized use. Deletion requests through the contact below are handled as soon as possible and within 7 calendar days.

Disconnecting or deleting local application data removes the account’s OAuth tokens, cached channel information and upload-result state. Desktop application configuration may remain because it identifies the application rather than the user’s YouTube data. Removing upload history also requires checking existing YouTube uploads before allowing new uploads, to avoid duplicates.

Owner-selected original videos and thumbnails are separate from API-derived records and are not automatically deleted by disconnecting the utility. Any copies shared with an AI service are subject to that service’s applicable controls; local deletion alone does not erase a provider’s retained records.

Deleting local application data does not delete videos or other information already stored by YouTube. Manage or delete published content using YouTube’s own interfaces. A disconnected utility cannot continue publishing, but YouTube may still carry out schedules already saved on its platform.

Revoke authorization or request deletion

  1. Use the utility’s disconnect operation, or open Google’s account security permissions and remove SAFRWAY Video Manager’s access.
  2. Contact SAFRWAY and identify “Video Manager” if you also want stored local application data deleted. Do not send passwords, tokens or private media in a public message.

The disconnect operation requests revocation from Google immediately and removes associated local authorized data. If that network request fails, local data is still removed but Google revocation is not confirmed: remove access through Google’s security settings as well. Deletion after a request or in-application revocation must be completed within 7 calendar days. Revocation through Google’s security page is not delivered as an instant notification to this local utility: it is checked at the next use. The operator must recheck authorization or remove retained API-derived records at least every 30 days, including during inactivity; deletion after external revocation must occur within that limit. If authorization cannot be refreshed, authorized operations must stop rather than bypass consent.

These informational pages

These pages do not offer Google sign-in, uploads, advertising trackers, embedded video or analytics scripts. Hosting and network providers may process ordinary request information such as IP address, browser information, requested URL and time to deliver and protect the site. Following an external contact or Google/YouTube link takes you to that provider’s service and privacy rules.

Contact and policy changes

For questions, complaints or deletion requests, contact SAFRWAY through the existing SAFRWAY support bot on Telegram and mention “Video Manager privacy”. Start with general information; arrange a safe channel if sensitive details are needed.

If the utility’s data practices change, this policy and the in-product notice must be updated, and fresh consent is required before using data for a new purpose. Continued Google authorization is not consent to undisclosed uses.
