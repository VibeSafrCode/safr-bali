📱 Where to start?

The main starting point is our Telegram bot, @Yoga_ganster_bot.

You can use it to open the Mini App — a small application that runs directly inside Telegram.

To open it, use the button in the bot's menu or the blue button next to the message input field.

If you access the app through Telegram, you don't need to register separately. The app automatically signs you in using your Telegram account.

To open the Mini App, use the button in @Yoga_ganster_bot within Telegram.

The project also has a separate website where you can explore its different areas.

We'll gradually bring more features into the Mini App so that you can handle most of your needs in one place.

Depending on the country and the services available, these may include:

- Visas and matters related to your stay;
- Motorbikes and other transportation;
- Insurance;
- Accommodation;
- Other services as the project develops.

Our goal is to make sure you don't have to remember everything yourself:

When your visa expires, when your motorbike or accommodation rental ends, what insurance you have, or whom to contact about a particular issue.

As the relevant features become available, the team will also be able to keep track of important dates and draw your attention to matters that need attention.

These features will be introduced gradually as the app develops.
