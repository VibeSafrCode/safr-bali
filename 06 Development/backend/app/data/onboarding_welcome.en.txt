Hi! 👋

Welcome to SAFRWAY.

If you've recently started using our bot, we'd like to briefly explain what's available here and how to use it all.

SAFRWAY is more than just a bot offering individual services. We're gradually building a unified ecosystem for living abroad, relocating, and handling everyday matters in another country.

Our most developed destination right now is Bali 🇮🇩

We help with visas, accommodation, motorbikes, insurance, and other matters that people face when relocating to or living on the island for an extended period.

At the same time, the project is gradually expanding to other countries.

📱 Where to start?

The main part of SAFRWAY is currently available in our Mini App.

You can open it directly through Telegram — using the button in the bot's menu or the blue button next to the message input field.

If you access the app through Telegram, you don't need to register separately. The app automatically signs you in using your Telegram account.

You can also open the Mini App in your browser and add it to your phone's home screen like a regular web app.

Over time, almost all the main features will be available there.

🏝 The first section — “My Life”

This is your personal dashboard within SAFRWAY.

It will bring together the services you use with us:

- Your visas;
- Motorbikes;
- Insurance;
- Accommodation;
- And other services as the system develops.

For example, if you arrange a visa through us, the visa itself and the number of days remaining until it expires will appear in the app.

As the expiration date approaches, the system will remind you when it's time to contact your manager.

The same functionality will gradually become available for other services.

As a result, you won't have to remember everything yourself:

When your visa expires, when your motorbike or accommodation rental ends, what insurance you have, or whom to contact about a particular matter.

Everything will be in one place.

We can also see these expiration dates in our internal system, allowing us to draw your attention to important matters in good time.

🛠 The “Services” section

This is where you'll find everything we can help you with.

Choose the service you need and submit a request. Our team will then contact you.

If the service you're looking for isn't listed in the app yet, simply send us a message.

We're constantly adding new services and can often help even when a particular service hasn't appeared in the menu yet.

Our goal is to save you from having to search for dozens of different companies and specialists on your own.

You can come to us with a request, and we'll do our best to find the most suitable solution.

👤 The “Profile” section

This is where you'll find your personal information, referral link, and SAFR Points.

You can use your referral link to invite friends and people you know.

All such referrals are already recorded by the system.

In the future, SAFR Points will become a fully developed rewards system. You'll be able to use them for discounts, services, special offers, and other rewards.

🌍 The “Main” section

Here you can choose a country.

Once you've selected a country, the app will display services and information relevant to that destination.

Bali is currently our most developed destination, but we're gradually adding new countries and expanding the project's capabilities.

💬 If you need help

There's a “Support” section in the app.

You can use it to contact us about any question.

If necessary, we'll connect you directly with a manager, because many situations are easier and faster to resolve through personal communication.

What are we building?

Our goal is to make sure that when you relocate to or live in another country, you don't have to:

- Find a different person for every issue;
- Keep dozens of contacts;
- Track document expiration dates yourself;
- Figure out whom you can trust;
- Start searching for a service from scratch every time.

We want to bring all of this together in one ecosystem.

The project is constantly evolving. We're adding new services, countries, and languages, improving the design, and automating more and more processes.

So if some part of the app currently looks unfinished, that's normal. SAFRWAY is actively developing and being updated.

For now, you can start with a few simple steps:

1. Open the Mini App.
2. Choose the country you need.
3. Explore the “Services” section.
4. Take a look at “My Life”.
5. If you have a question, contact us through Support.

Welcome to SAFRWAY.

We'll do our best to make handling everyday matters abroad as simple as possible for you — all from one place.
